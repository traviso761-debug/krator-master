# eastabyss: the winding lakes (plan, 2026-10-23)

The kit draws the eastern abyss's salt lake, flats and marsh, the coal-swamp jungle and the shelf. It predates 4.21,
which **rebuilt the abyss floor** (`tools/scale-model/README.md`, `ABYSS_LAKES`): 19 long, snaky lakes in the pits
between sills, about 10,400 km² of water, with desert strips between them that the lakes' own rain greens at their
shores. One station draws the new floor; the page as built stays station 1.

**Status: plan, awaiting the owner.**

## The ground (surveyed 2026-10-23)

64,000 km² (less the smaller polygons): median -1,560 m, 15% water; XS (abyssal salt steppe) 34%, XA 15%, XV 13%, XW
(abyssal desert) 11%; rain ~700 mm on average, from ~200-450 mm far from the water to ~1,500 mm beside it.

## Station 2: the winding lakes

A long lake winding between sills, **jungle on its shores and desert a few kilometres back**: the lake effect drawn as
a gradient from mat-reed shallows and coal-swamp jungle at the water, through salt steppe, to abyssal desert on the
sills; the old beds filled and showing as pale flats; Locus's lake on the horizon. The sharpest wet-to-dry change on the
map, under 2 atm.
