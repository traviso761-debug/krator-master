# biomes/rheolith: notes

Design rationale and settled worldbuilding live in `PLAN.md` (`2026-11-17`). Key implementation notes for Station 1 (*The Breached Throat*, `dist/rheolith.html`):

- **Strict `180-550 °C` viability enforcement:** Every live subsurface colony (`chokecoral`, `wirevein`, `foamreef`, `fluxcreeper`, recorded with `live = 1` in `RHEO.PLACED`) is gated through `RHEO_PHYS.windowAt({tempC, waterActivity, acidPh}).viable` on the inner shaft walls of the breached throat (`y = -18.5..-5.2 m`) and deep inside the satellite fumarole mouths. Surface formations (`live = 0`) are dead vitrified ceramic/glass byproducts or quenched `staregg` propagules. `HCHK.liveWindow` verifies every placed live colony on load.
- **Zero bioluminescence:** None of `RHEO.M` or the host rock/ground materials carry `emissive` or `emissiveMap` (`HCHK.noGlow`). Deep conduit incandescence at `y < -17 m` (`530-545 °C`) is true thermal Planck radiance added in `MAT_GROUND`'s shader at the bottom of the central shaft; metallic sheen on `wirevein`, `bismuthnest`, `staregg` cores, and `bellblister` domes comes from `BIO.iridBarkMat` thin-film oxide interference.
- **The Ranj volatile-vapour link (`PLAN.md` §6.3a):** `87-host-kin.js` borrows `throne.spice` in place via `BIO.kin`. Inside the warm siloxane-vapour halo (`22-68 m` from active throats, `RHEO_PHYS.ranjViability(...).fruiting === true`), wild Ranj bears red resin and Ranj fungus brackets (`T.planted = false`). Up on the cold graben scarp bench (`> 132 m` out, `fruiting === false`), Ranj grows thin, yellowing, and sterile (`T.planted = true`).
- **Interactive conduit valve:** `86-host-throat.js` (`window.THROAT`) wires the bar buttons (`[Gag throat]`, `[Salt seam]`, `[Heavy dross]`, `[Reset vent]`) through `RHEO_PHYS.choke(...)` to both dynamic constrictor/grazer/frost meshes anchored into the inner shaft wall and `89w-host-water.js`'s `WATER.geyser` column (`uH`, `uCyc`, `geyserOff`).
- **Focal drawn-ground mesh & slope seating:** `84-host-ground.js` builds a single `520x520` cubic-warped focal grid (`0.62 m` cell across the Breached Throat and fumaroles) with an exact closed-form Cardano inverse `DRAWN_H` registered via `KDRAWN`. All 4 guilds, 8 byproducts, dynamic throat jaws/creepers, and borrowed kin seat onto `DRAWN_H` / `DRAWN_N` (`HCHK.grounded`).
- **Firebliss inhabitants:** Kept open per owner direction (`PLAN.md` §6.4); no permanent Firebliss settlement or culture claim is baked into the kit.
