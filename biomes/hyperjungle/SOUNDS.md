# hyperjungle: a CC0 sound shortlist (2026-10-26)

Raw material for the hyperjungle's soundscapes. Every clip came from a Freesound search filtered to **CC0**
(`license:"Creative Commons 0"`), so it can be used, changed and shipped without attribution. **Nobody has listened to
these yet.** They were picked by title, description and download count, so audition them before any is used. When a
clip is downloaded, keep its URL and license in its `meta.json`, as the material library does.

Nothing here sounds alien as recorded. The "Treatment" column says how to make it alien. The air here is about 1.9 atm:
denser air carries sound further and makes it louder, so let the beds come in close and full.

## Gaps

- **Skull-pod rattles:** nothing good under CC0. Record dry seed pods or a gourd shaker, or synthesise them (bursts of
  filtered noise). The search found only seed pours.
- **Canopy rain on large leaves** and **a dry-season burn front** were not found yet. Search again.
- **Excluded:** clips whose description adds conditions to CC0 (for example Ko Phi Phi's "don't put this raw sounds/files
  on…"), because the condition makes the license unclear.

## Beyond Freesound

- **Sonniss GDC bundles:** royalty-free, not CC0. Professional jungle and creature design. Use it in built pages, but
  don't put the raw files in the repo.
- **NOAA and NPS recordings:** US government work, usually public domain. Hydrophone and natural soundscapes.
