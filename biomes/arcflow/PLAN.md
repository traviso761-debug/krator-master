# arcflow: the plan (locked design, 2026-11-24)

**Arcflow** (`Kulaneva-keel`: **`Külaneva`**, `rmwetn7jqgm9g` in Scale Model `5.1`): a **`174,912 km²` dry-land** (`+ 31,208 km²` coastal/estuarine margin, `X = 411..907, Y = 1..208`) ultramafic-over-foredeep river, waterfall-klint, and serpentine skerry-fen province north of [`biomes/doubledelta`](../doubledelta/PLAN.md) and [`biomes/riavalley`](../riavalley/PLAN.md), and homeland of **The Kulanewa Free-Towns** ([`kits/kulanewa`](../../kits/kulanewa/PLAN.md), `~125,000` souls across *Kaarum* Ledger-Syndicates and *Kūla-Nā* / *Ikki* Covenant-Communes).

Out in the NW/N Ocean, **The Arch** ([`biomes/arch/PLAN.md`](../arch/PLAN.md)) is Krator's concentric impact-flexural outer bulge: an obducted slab of mantle **harzburgite, lizardite-chrysotile serpentinite, chromitite–pyroxenite–anorthosite cumulates, and `arch-rift-basalt`** (`nickel = 180`). Striking ENE onshore into `Arcflow`, this ultramafic–mafic complex overrides the northern sedimentary foredeep under **`1.59–1.34 atm`** (`1.58–1.42 atm` across the inhabited river-and-fen belt, `p50 = +1,257.7 m`, `g = 7.4 m/s²` / `0.75 g`), **`Cfa` (`41.1%`) / `Am` (`25.2%`)** (`1,239 mm/yr` median rain, rising to `1,820–2,463 mm/yr` along the western rivers; `+22.0 °C` median), humic `-neva` peat-acids, and four perennial rivers ([`rivers.json`](../../tools/scale-model/model/data/rivers.json): `9107` **`Suur-Neva`**, `9103` **`Mere-Upe`**, `9108` **`Musta-Jõgi`**, and `9104` **`Helme-Koski`**).

Together with the **SW→NE Carnelian Ecological Frontier** ([`docs/CARNELIAN-FRONTIER-PLAN.md`](../../docs/CARNELIAN-FRONTIER-PLAN.md)), `Arcflow` showcases **five terrestrial landforms unique on Krator** and an unmistakable **`~75%` Earth / `~25%` Carnelian *Erythrophyll* edaphic crazy-quilt** where dark-green serpentinite skerries blaze like crimson jewel-islands out of emerald Earth sedge-fens.

### 1.2 Five Unique Terrestrial Landforms
1. **Zebra-Cumulate River Pavements & `-Koski` Bench-Cataracts (*Bushveld Dwars River × Karelian Shield*, `Station 2`):** River erosion strips peat off tilted onshore `Arch` layered intrusions to expose **mirror-slick alternating `2–15 cm` bands of jet-black chromitite (`Cr-Fe-PGE`), bronze orthopyroxenite, and bone-white anorthosite**, forming staircases of **`3–9 m` flat-topped rapids (`-koski`)** every few kilometres.
2. **Active Continental Serpentinization: `pH 11.5` Cerulean Travertine & Cold `H2/CH4` "Uncrowned Flames" (*The Cedars / Oman Ophiolite*, `Station 3`):** Meteoric rainwater circulating into fresh harzburgite along `Arch` ring-faults vents **`pH 11–12` `Ca-OH` springs** that flash-precipitate **snow-white and milky-cerulean calcite tufa drapes** over dark blue-green lizardite cliffs alongside cool abiotic **`H2/CH4` gas-seeps (*Jumala-Tuli*)** burning with pale-blue smokeless flames beside running water.
3. **Humic Bog-Iron & Garnierite "Coin-Ore" Fens (`Musta-Jõgi`, `Station 4`):** Between smooth, waxy green-black **serpentinite whaleback ridges (*kalju-selkä*)**—giving `Arcflow` a Fennoscandian/Estonian skerry-and-bog silhouette *without* glaciation—acidic humic peat-water (`pH 4.3`) leaches `Fe`, `Ni`, and `Co` from apple-green **garnierite** saprolite and re-precipitates **regenerating discoid Fe-Ni bog-ore nodules (*raha-maak*, "penny-ore")** on oxygenated shoals (`20-year` rake cycle).
4. **`Hiidenkirnu` Swirl-Kettles (`Helme-Koski`, `Station 2`):** Flood vortices spin dense chromite cobbles to drill `2–8 m` cylindrical potholes (`KBASIN`) into ultramafic sills, concentrating heavy black chromite/PGM sand at the base while swirling foredeep **golden `merevaik` (`ria-amber`)** in surface eddies.
5. **The Southern *Külaneva-Klint* Waterfall Escarpment (*Baltic Klint / Jägala–Narva Analogue*, `Station 1`):** Where the hard `Arch` basalt/serpentinite sheet overrides soft foredeep **golden *kukersite* oil-shale, amber lignite, and glauconitic blue clay**, differential erosion carves a **`35–80 m` (`55 m` at `Station 1`) undercut horseshoe waterfall scarp (*Klint*)**—the head of `lotja` barge navigation and seat of Kulanewa's *Kaarum* clearing-cities and Covered *Sampo* Mill-Bridges.

## 2. `Arcflow` on the Ecological Frontier: *The Serpentine Edaphic Crazy-Quilt (`~75% Earth / ~25% Carnelian Relict`)*

Per [`docs/CARNELIAN-FRONTIER-PLAN.md`](../../docs/CARNELIAN-FRONTIER-PLAN.md), `Arcflow` is where the **Crazy-Quilt Law** is sharpest at eye level:
- **Emerald- & Gold-Green Earth Matrix (`~75%`):** Fertile foredeep clay-loams, alluvial `Suur-Neva` levees, and humic `-neva` peatlands (`Ca/Mg > 1`, `pH 4.3–7.2`) are `100%` colonized by Earth silver-birch (`nevabirch`), black alder (`mustaalder`), escarpment linden (`klintlinden`), spire-spruce (`klintspruce`), cloudberry *Sphagnum* (`cloudberryfen`), and golden sedge-meadows.
- **Crimson & Carnelian Relict Islands (`~25%`):** Earth vascular plants suffer lethal **serpentine toxicity** (`Mg/Ca > 8`, `Ni > 2,200 ppm`, `Cr`, near-zero `P`, `pH 9.5–11.8`) on bare onshore `Arch` serpentinite whalebacks (*kalju-selkä*), zebra-cumulate ribs, and hyperalkaline travertine rims. Pre-terraforming **Carnelian *Erythrophyll* clades**—evolved on ultramafic impact crust—thrive on `Ni/Mg` rock! Every dark-green serpentinite skerry rising out of a green Earth sedge-fen blazes like a **scarlet-and-vermilion jewel-island** (`carnelian_organpipe`, `erythra_veil`, `bladder_tether`, `agate_window`), while the NE `Arcflow` uplands (`Station 5 auger`) preserve a straight orbital **Bleach-Swath** and a stalled Ancient **`ecof_soil_auger`** rig surrounded by a `45 m`-radius emerald-green lime-dosed Earth birch-and-clover oasis inside crimson serpentine barrens.
