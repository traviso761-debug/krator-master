# biomes/swouterwall — Design & Calibration Notes

- **Annealed Northern Boreal-to-Alpine Transect (`PLAN.md` sections 0-3):** Scale-Model 5.1 merges former `Region 177` (`rmwzq42zszc94`, `Y = 858..985`, `+2,800..+3,800 m`, `1.05..1.14 atm`) into `swouterwall` (`rmwvsqhhqyk3f`). `SWOW_PHYS.transectAt(py, elevM)` models how warm, misty Veiled Mountains spillover (`~1,050 mm/yr`, `Dfc`) feeds the stepped northern paternoster lakes and quaking sphagnum rafts before drying and cooling southward across the turquoise moraine step (`+4,360 m`, `0.91 atm`), Trigemini Pass (`+5,680 m`, `0.76 atm`), and the southern hanging breath-vales (`+6,100 m`, `0.69 -> 0.58 atm`).
- **Hypoxia Ceiling (`< 0.60 atm` at `~+6,465 m ASL`):** On Station 4 (`breathvale`), vascular flora (`lungcushion`, `glassbell`) stops dead at `SWOW_PHYS.HYPOXIA_CEILING_M`, while mineral-armoured `sunblood_crust` climbs up to `0.58 atm` (`~+6,550 m`) and the upper megabreccia headwall above `0.58 atm` stands airless and barren beneath a `0.62 atm` cobalt-violet sky.
- **Bellows Cushion (`lungcushion`) Microclimate:** Models a `+12 °C` sunlit thermal cavity and `~28%` photosynthetic internal O₂ share (`SWOW_PHYS.lungcushionAt`), raising effective partial pressure at `+5,680 m` from `0.756 atm` to `> 1.00 atm` for Dalab high-pass couriers.
