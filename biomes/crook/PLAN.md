# crook: the plan (locked design, 2026-11-22)

**The Crook** (`rmw4wknusgc7h` / `crookshore`, `rmwet8ethscuf` / `crookwash`, and `rmwetb495tgzt` / `crookhead` in Scale Model `5.1`, subdividing parent trough `rmvthecrook`): a `1,280 km`-long (`Y = 548..1191`, `303,876 km²` combined envelope, **`284,828 km²` dry land**, `19,048 km²` coastal sea and caldera lagoons) coastal-to-alpine compressional mountain trough wedged between the Western Outer Wall (`Farside` and `w outer wall`) on the northeast, the acoustic-suppressed **Silent Vales** (`rmw4wizrk66e1`) on the east, the **Copper Glacier** alpine cryosphere (`rmvcopperglacier`) on the south, and the megatsunami-battered NW Ocean (`Steaming Seamounts` and `Drownlands`) on the west.

Across `8,330 m` of vertical relief (`+3.9 m` wave-planed basalt spray-benches to `+8,334 m` glaciated granite-gneiss peaks) and a pressure drop from **`1.60 atm` at the surf line to `0.68 atm` on the high divide**, The Crook follows a strict **`85–90%` Earthlike coastal-to-alpine montane aesthetic + `10–15%` grounded physical/biochemical weirdness** (`CROOK-RIA-DECISIONS.md` §1.6). Its dominant matrix is lush Olympic Peninsula, Yakushima, Western Caucasus, and Fiordland conifer-and-broadleaf mountain forest grading into glacial slate and granite moraine tarns: ancient *benihi* **red-cypress**, Sitka-like **coast spruce**, giant **western redcedar**, **silver fir**, moss-sleeved **bigleaf maple**, **tree-ferns**, **devil's club**, **gorge rhododendron**, and subalpine **krummholz larch**. It is kept unmistakably distinct from **`biomes/farside`** (zero terrestrial land-kelp or strand-anemone forests), **`biomes/arch`** (zero ultramafic serpentinite blue-sap nickel barrens; `0%` ultramafic rock across all three Crook polygons), and **`biomes/doubledelta`** (zero flat alluvial marsh sponge-bioherms).

Within that grounded montane matrix, five site-specific physical and geochemical drivers produce Kratorian signatures where the data demands them:
1. **`Crookshore` (`< +65 m` spray-zone vs. `+65–+500 m` fog belt, `silica = 150`):** Wave-planed columnar basalt benches and four drowned `secondary-ejecta` impact calderas encrusted below the `+65 m` megatsunami trimline with **lithistid basalt chimney- and cushion-sponges** exhaling salt-mist plumes, giving way abruptly above `+65 m` to cathedral spruce-cedar rainforest hung with **celadon and amber spongin canopy cup- and beard-sponges** harvesting marine fog.
2. **`Crookwash` Skarn & Nephrite Biomineralizers (`B`, `jade = 200`, `skarn = 200`, `iron = 255`):** Deep `1.20 atm`, `+11 °C` gorge forests where metallophyte understory ferns, horsetails, and old gorge-cedars over contact-skarn seeps deposit **rust-burnished metallic bark scales** and **translucent, opalescent jade-white silica-glazed frond margins that clink faintly like thin porcelain** in canyon drafts (read by `Dzagh-Vardzi` prospectors to locate buried nephrite lenses).
3. **`Crookwash` Iron- & Jade-Stained Rimstone Steps (`D`) & Wind-Tuned Chambered Brakes (`E`):** Cold-water iron-oxidizing bryophyte and bacterial seeps building **stepped terracotta, ochre, and celadon travertine/rimstone pools** beneath dark red-cypress boughs, while silica-stiffened hollow **chambered canyon bamboo** (`core/biome/33b-core-bamboo.js`) and giant horsetails along chasm rims hum in quiet, breathy chords under `1.20 atm` gorge updrafts to summon acoustic-homing mountain pollinators.
4. **`Crookhead` Malachite Metallophytes (`B`) & Thermogenic "Hearth-Ring" Melt-Wells (`D`, `copper = 130`, `iron = 255`, `tin = 200`, `jade = 200`, `peat = 140`):** High `1.07 atm`, `+4 °C` subalpine krummholz larch and moraine tarn country bordering `Copper Glacier`, where *yareta*-like **malachite cushion-heaths** chelate copper and iron into verdigris-teal and rust-ochre rinds beside **frost-glass silica sedges**, and colonial starch-burning **hearth-ring rosettes** (`+15 °C` internal crown warmth) melt neat `1–2 m` circular wells through late-spring snowpack streaked with **malachite-teal and ochre snow-algae** blown off `Copper Glacier`.
5. **`Crookhead` -> `Silent Vales` Acoustic Threshold (`E`, *The Great Hush*):** Along high eastern granite passes (`+4,432–+5,238 m`), austere **bone-white, silica-fluted alpine seed-chimneys** sigh in harmonic intervals on ridge-gusts — and drop abruptly dead silent (while continuing to sway visibly in `ATMOS` wind) across the Ancient acoustic suppression boundary of **Silent Vales**.

Status: **Build-ready plan** incorporating all locked decisions in `CROOK-RIA-DECISIONS.md` (§1.1–§1.6) and the slated Scale-Model `5.1` fluvial tributary incision pass (§2.3). Zero existing repository files, scale-model rasters, or `LORE.md` lines are modified by this plan.

### 1.3 New-build survey compliance (`tools/checks_optin.json`, items 22–31)

When `biomes/crook` is built, `"biomes/crook"` is added to all five lists in `tools/checks_optin.json` from commit 1:
- **`recorded`** (item 25): `build.py` calls `buildlib.through_tools_build(__file__)` at top so `build-inputs.json` is always emitted, `tools/build_sources.py` `report()` runs before the first write (`encoding='utf-8', newline='\n'`), and Git LFS pointers are refused (`python3 tools/lfs_needs.py biomes/crook --pull`, item 23).
- **`minset`** (item 26): `CORE_BIOME` starts from `buildlib.CORE_BIOME_MIN` and appends opt-in core fragments — never a hand-rolled fragment list.
- **`samplers`** (item 27): `verify.py` calls `harness.verify_page(..., checks=harness.SURVEY)`, enforcing `< 15` active texture samplers on every linked WebGL program (`KRATOR_GL=gpu` required on GPU lanes, item 22; stale-cache notice active, item 31).
- **`drawn-ground`** (item 28): `KDRAWN.add(KDRAWN.grid(...))` registers every station's ground and terrace mesh so eye-clearance checks test actual drawn triangles.
- **`names`** (item 30) & **`ktest`** (item 29): zero apostrophes (`'`) or colons (`:`) in any preset view name across all six stations; Node tests use `tools/ktest.js` (`T.ok`, `T.neg`, `T.done()`).
