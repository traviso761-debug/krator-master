# ebadlands: the transect as stations (plan, 2026-10-23)

The kit draws its whole transect, from the sulphur flats and the hot waste to the tundra under the airless rim (BSh to
EF), on one page. Its polygon is 209,000 km², the largest kit polygon after Korona, and each band is big enough to want
room of its own. The proposal splits the transect into four stations; the page as built becomes station 1 (or the
overview), and the strata upgrade (`core/terrain/STRATA-PLAN.md`, the `badland` recipe) lands under all of them.

**Status: plan, awaiting the owner.**

## The stations

1. **The hot waste and the painted badlands** (BSh): the sulphur flats and acid pools, the alien flora (ember crowns,
   sunspires, needle blooms, stilt pods), the banded mounds on the `badland` recipe.
2. **The canyons and green valleys** (Cfa, BSk): the Zion-like canyons, cottonwood and maple along the streams, gambel
   oak, sagebrush and pinyon-juniper on the benches. The largest band.
3. **The montane forest** (Dfb, Dfc): ponderosa and aspen, then spruce-fir, a meadow and a burn.
4. **The tree line and the tundra** (Dfc's top, ET): krummholz and bristlecones, tundra, the glaciers the kit's
   `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` says the showcase lacks, the airless rim's edge over it.
