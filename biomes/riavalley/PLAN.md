## 2. Ecological Signature: The Ancients' Boomtown Frontier Frozen Mid-Ecocide (`CROOK-RIA-DECISIONS.md` §2.3)

Where the Inner Crater completed Earthly ecological replacement and the Rift never received it, **`Ria Valley` was the Ancients' rapidly expanding northern boomtown frontier when the Cataclysm froze it mid-ecocide**:
- **Visible Landscape Scars (`Heartland`, `Weeping Valley`, `Upper Ria`):** Kilometer-wide geometric **defoliant "bleach-belts"** and caliche hardpan corridors where Ancient orbital/aerial sprayers stripped native retinol-purple and iridescent blue-green Kratorian forests; active **biological strangler-fronts** where surviving native symbiotic creepers wrestle feral engineered Earthly orchards; and ruptured **sap-acclimatization groves** in `Weeping Valley` (`amber = 120` across `7,299` trunk nodes) whose golden weeping resin hardens into electrostatic **`ria-amber`** while venting psychoactive afternoon pollen-mist around `996, 156`.
- **Why Lower Ria Is a Drowned Dendritic Sound (Never a Glacial Fjord):** In frost-free `Am`/`Cfa` air (`+26..+28 °C` at `0–625 m`, `1.59 atm`), lower `9101` `Mā-Rīa` was incised as a V-shaped multi-branched **dendritic fluvial valley** and subsequently drowned by Western Ocean sea-level rise — forming winding oak-, camphor-, and pine-clad promontories with `24 h` libration-tide oyster visors on `KSTRATA` `carbonate-shelf` bluffs capped by `loess` terraces (Galician *Rías Baixas* / Chesapeake analogue), **never** U-shaped glaciated fjords.
