## 1. Region Bounds & Geomorphic Architecture
- **Region Polygons:**
  - **`rmvriavalley` (*ria valley*):** `X = 642..1465, Y = 56..268` — `1,400 km` long dendritic river-and-sound system draining the northern outer slope westward into the `0 m` Western Ocean (`1.60 atm`) via trunk river **`9101` (*Ria River* / Eternian *Mã-wè*)**, cleanly separated from Spider Lake (`1295, 212`) by Spider Lake's unbroken `+1,740 m` NW `secondary-ejecta` rampart (`1278, 166`).
  - **`rmvyiyw8ucd1y` (*neo-ozarks*):** `X = 1109..1318, Y = 107..231` — dissected cherty-dolomite plateau and entrenched meander highland on the upper `Ria River` watershed (including **`tube_ozarks_15`** from `1144, 168` to `1246, 172`), modelled on Earth's Ozark Plateau / Current & Jack's Fork shut-ins.
  - **`rmvdoubledelta` (*double delta*):** `X = 502..625, Y = 98..208` — coastal distributary plain just SW of Ria Sound where The Arch's NE onshore **`arch-rift-basalt`** horst (`518, 152`; `biomes/arch/PLAN.md` §17) splits the lowland outflow into twin tide-reworked deltas.
- **Why Ria Valley Is a Drowned Dendritic Sound (Not a Glacial Fjord):** Set in mild, ice-free `Cfa` / `Csb` maritime air (`+15 to +22 °C` mean at `0 to +400 m`, `1.54–1.60 atm`), lower `9101` (*Ria River*) was incised as a V-shaped, multi-branched **dendritic fluvial valley** during lower ocean stands and subsequently **drowned by Western Ocean sea-level rise / Arch-margin flexural tilt** — producing branching oak-and-pine-clad promontories, winding oyster-rich tidal creeks, and V-profiled sound arms (Galician *Rías Baixas* / Chesapeake / Marlborough Sounds analogue), **never** U-shaped glaciated fjords.

## 2. Stratigraphy & `KSTRATA` Bindings (`core/terrain/35-core-strata.js`)
- **Drowned Ria Bluffs & Neo-Ozark Karst Palisades:** Bound to **`carbonate-shelf`** (`2–5 m` cream-to-buff cherty dolostone, bioclastic platform limestone, and recessed `marl` solution ledges with `24 h` libration-tide oyster visors at sea level and big Ozark-type phreatic cave-springs upslope).
- **Igneous Shut-Ins & Coastal Horst Gates:** Red rhyolite/granophyre basement knobs (`outland-tuff` / porphyry shut-ins in `neo-ozarks` narrows) and **`arch-rift-basalt`** columnar horst ribs along the western `Double Delta` gate (`518, 152`).
- **Sheltered Cove & Terrace Soils:** Bound to **`loess`** (`2–6 m` warm buff silt benches on drowned river-terrace shoulders, supporting Eternian terraced orchards, vineyards, and wet-rice/taro head-of-tide paddies).

## 3. Showcase Station #16 — `Ria Valley Drowned Dendritic Sound`
- **Canonical `5.1` Coordinates:** **`(658, 148)`** (`1,316 km E, 296 km S` on the `1821 × 1723` `2 km/px` canvas — main drowned dendritic confluence of lower **`9101` (*Ria River* / *Mã-wè*)** where three branching tidal ria arms converge inside the Western Ocean mouth, NE of `Double Delta` `518, 152`).
- **Elevation & Atmosphere:** **`0 m`** Western Ocean tidal sound rising across **`+15 to +240 m`** interlocking V-ridge promontories (**`1.56–1.60 atm`**, `Cfa` / `Csb` warm-temperate maritime climate, `+19 °C` annual mean, golden dense-air coastal light and morning valley-arm sea mist).
- **`KSTRATA` Binding:** **`carbonate-shelf`** (`2–5 m` warm cream/buff cherty limestone and pale marl cliff tiers along the winding promontory bluffs, undercut at `0 m` by a crisp `24 h` libration-tide notch crusted with intertidal oysters and sea-stack arches) capped on gently sloping `+30–90 m` fluvial terrace shoulders by **`loess`** orchard soils.
- **Camera Framing (`6.4 × 6.4 km` dendritic-sound vista):** Elevated headland camera at `+145 m` on a `carbonate-shelf` bluff spur looking WSW down the drowned dendritic arbor of **Ria Sound** toward the gleaming `0 m` Western Ocean horizon: three winding blue-green tidal arms (`WATER.open` calm inner-ria wave share + `WATER.plume` golden-silt `Ria River` mixing front) interlocking around forested `Cfa`/`Csb` oak, cedar, and stone-pine fingers (`swlowlands` + `nwlowlands` kin), cream limestone tidal visors and anchored **Eternal State** (*Mã-wè*) junk-rigged timber coasters and oyster rafts moored in a sheltered cove below `loess` citrus/tea terraces, and faint morning mist (`ATMOS.fogBelt`) threading the side creeks.
- **Hydrology & Trade Tie-In:** `9101` (*Ria River*) delivers nutrient-rich carbonate water from the `Neo-Ozarks` karst springs (`1144, 168`) into `70+ km` of deep, storm-sheltered tidal ria arms flushed twice daily by the gas-giant/solar tide; this drowned sound is the **Eternal State's** premier northern shipbuilding, pearl-oyster, timber, and river-to-ocean transshipment anchorage linking **Farside / Farport** coastal fleets with upriver *Mã-wè* and Hum canyon trade.
