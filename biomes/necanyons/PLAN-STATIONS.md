# necanyons: the stations to Korona (plan, 2026-10-23)

The owner (2026-10-23): the `ne rim` polygon (renamed from `ne canyon`) is this kit's ground, **a transition zone to
Korona that may need several stations**. The page as built (Pinnacle Rock's canyon country) stays station 1.

**Status: plan, awaiting the owner.**

## The ground (surveyed 2026-10-23)

27,000 km², median -870 m, ~1.8 atm. Not a smooth gradient but a mosaic, south to north:

| Band | Height | Rain | Climate |
|---|---|---|---|
| South edge | ~-900 m | ~1,270 mm | Aw 60%, Am 27% |
| The middle | rises to +800 m | ~1,300 mm | Am, **Cfa 23%, BSh 18%** on the raised benches |
| North (toward Korona) | ~-1,150 m, the lowest | ~880 mm, the driest | **XV 24%, BSh 23%**, Aw, XA, XS 9% |

**A note against the kit's plan**: it assumes about 3,000 mm at Pinnacle Rock; 4.21 reads about 640 mm on the knoll and
about 1,050 mm round it. The owner's call (2026-10-23): the knoll's dry island is a model artefact, blended in 4.22 to
~1,100-1,400 mm (`tools/scale-model/README.md`), which makes station 1's floor **monsoon forest with savanna glades**,
not closed jungle: the kit reads the hyperjungle's savanna-edge station there when it exists. The plan's explanation of
the dry caps (the rock drains, not the climate) holds unchanged, and so does the carnivore rule.

## Station 2: the high benches

The raised middle: benches and mesas standing into cooler air (Cfa) with dry tops (BSh): flametip scrub forest, sandpan
fonio, the first **tepui-like tables** (the strata plan's `tepui` recipe) with sun pitchers and yellow tubes on top, the
canyons between them deeper and greener.

## Station 3: the savanna floor toward Korona

The low, dry north: abyssal savanna and salt steppe between scattered sandstone stacks, salt pans, monkeycup vines only
along the creeks, the first of Korona's trench country and a ring ridge on the horizon; the carnivores of the poor, dry
ground growing larger toward Korona's man-eaters, still harmless here.
