# Arachnoid Canyons: Seed Biome Plan (`rmvarachnoids`, Scale Model `5.1`)

> **Coordinate Frame:** All `(X, Y)` coordinates are on the **Scale Model `5.1` (`1821 × 1723`, `2 km/px`)** canvas (`g = 7.4 m/s²` / `0.75 g`).

## 1. Region Bounds & Tectonic Origin
- **Region ID & Bounds:** `rmvarachnoids` (*arachnoid canyons*, `X = 845..1345, Y = 180..445`), occupying the north-northeast outer-crater slope between **Spider Lake** (`1295, 212`, `+1,460 m`) on the east, **N Semiarid / Dam 2** on the south, and **Ria Valley** (`rmvriavalley`) / the Western Ocean on the north-northwest.
- **Venusian-Type Arachnoid Volcanotectonic Complexes:** Named for 3–5 nested concentric ring-graben and radial-dike cauldron structures (`D = 45–110 km`, Venusian *arachnoid* / terrestrial Mull & Ardnamurchan ring-dike analogue) formed where abortive mantle-plume diapirs arched and fractured the northern outer-rim sedimentary/impact slope without building tall stratocones.
- **Master Trunk Gorge (`9102` *Hām-Tsēk*):** Receives the single unbifurcated SW spillway of **Spider Lake** (`1272, 193`, `+1,460 m`; `biomes/spiderlake/PLAN.md` Showcase Station #1) and slices `890 km` WNW directly through the concentric ring-dike ribs — anchored at **Web-Cleaver Gorge (`5.1 px 1055, 219`, `+680 m` river bed beneath `+1,320 m` ring-dike walls, `1.47 atm`, `Cfa` / `Cwa`)** — before descending into the Western Ocean / Double Delta (`518, 152`) structural belt.

## 2. Stratigraphy & `KSTRATA` Bindings (`core/terrain/35-core-strata.js`)
- **Radial & Concentric Ring-Dike Ribs:** Bound to **`arch-rift-basalt`** (`3–7 m` hard blue-black `colonnade` and `entablature` dike walls with brick-red baked `bolus` contact margins) standing `80–350 m` proud as natural stone hogbacks and river-shut-in gates across the canyon country.
- **Intracaldera Ponded Sills & Lava Benches:** Bound to **`tibasalt-ponded`** (`4–12 m` tabular flood-trap tiers flooring collapsed cauldron centres).
- **Pre-Intrusive Country-Rock Walls:** Uplifted northern outer-slope **`tepui`** cross-bedded sandstone/quartzite and cherty **`carbonate-shelf`** dolostone benches notched into overhanging Hum cliff-alcoves between the dark basalt radial spokes.

## 3. Climate, Ecology & Cultural Corridor
- **Climate Gradient:** Steps from cool-temperate `Cwb` / `Dwb` (`+1,200–1,650 m`, `1.30–1.38 atm`) on the wind-swept ring-crest steppes down into humid `Cfa` / `Cwa` (`+350–850 m`, `1.44–1.52 atm`, `950–1,650 mm/yr`) gallery forests, warm dike-dammed plunge pools, and bamboo-fern ravines (`nwlowlands` + `korona` dry-rim kin) along `9102` (*Hām-Tsēk*).
- **Hum River-Gorge Culture & Trade:** Spoken tongue is the whistled/tonal **Hum** continuum (`Hām-Tsēk`, *"Spider-Web Gorge"*), linking Spider Lake's silk-weir fishers (`1272, 193`) and Korona's northern rim tribes downriver to **Eternian** river-barge factors at the head of navigation above **Ria Valley** and **Double Delta**. Radial basalt dikes form natural fortified toll-gates and suspension-bridge piers across the gorge.

## 4. Candidate Station Anchor (`Web-Cleaver Gorge`)
- **Site:** **`(1055, 219)`** (`+680 m` river rapid to `+1,320 m` ring-dike wall; `1.47 atm`, `Cfa`): where `9102` (*Hām-Tsēk*) breaches two intersecting `arch-rift-basalt` radial/concentric dikes in a `40 m` wide whitewater shut-in spanned by braided Spider-Lake wild-silk cable bridges over `carbonate-shelf` / `tepui` undercut alcoves.
