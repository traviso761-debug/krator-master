## 1. Executive Concept & Geological Setting

**The Great Chasm** (`rmw4vh9t20da4`) is a colossal, `468 km`-long volcano-tectonic extensional megatrench and collapsed flood-basalt lava-curtain rift that rips through the high eastern shield plateau of the **S Outlands** (`rmvp52gbbc8pf`) and links at its southern throat (`Y = 986..1032`) directly into the northern rim of the **Loom-Chasms** (`Region 99`, `rmvraoas90co7`).

On the uncarved `5.1` plateau, the polygon sits across a freezing, hyperarid, hypobaric-to-vacuum basaltic upland (`min = 1,958 m`, `median = 5,643 m`, `p90 = 8,139 m`, `max = 11,718 m`; `EF / O / ET / HF / HT`; `0 mm/yr` rain). By excavating the axial graben of `great chasm` down to an **abyssal volcano-tectonic floor at `-1,200 m` to `+400 m`** while preserving its `5,600–11,700 m` flood-basalt rims, the biome achieves **`6.0 to 10.5 km` of continuous vertical wall relief** — the steepest non-crater-wall atmospheric pressure and thermal gradient on Krator:

$$\Delta h \approx 6,500\text{--}10,500\text{ m}, \qquad P_{\text{rim}} = 0.37\text{--}0.80\text{ atm} \;\longrightarrow\; P_{\text{floor}} = 1.52\text{--}1.86\text{ atm}$$

### Why the Great Chasm Exists Geologically
1. **Failed Mantle-Plume Radial Dike & Graben Collapse:** During the emplacement of the eastern Outlands flood basalts, a mammoth NNW–SSE feeder dike swarm drained laterally southward into the subsiding Loom-Chasms / Siphon Pits plumbing (`Y > 1000`). The evacuation of `~150,000 km³` of picritic and komatiitic magma caused the roof of the `5.6–11.7 km` plateau to founder along paired inward-dipping listric normal faults, dropping a stepped inner trench `6 to 10 km` below the surrounding shield.
2. **A Vertical Barometric & Thermal Inversion Engine:**
   - **Rim (`+5,600 to +11,700 m`):** Airless or near-airless (`O`, `HF`, `EF`), bone-dry (`rain = 0 mm`), `-22 to -55 °C`, blasted by stratospheric ultraviolet radiation and supersonic katabatic rim winds.
   - **Floor (`-1,200 to +400 m`):** Compressed dense air (`1.52–1.86 atm`) plus volcanic $\text{CO}_2 / \text{SO}_2$ pooling and intense conductive + hydrothermal + radiative heat from exposed lava skylights and sheeted dikes keep the chasm floor at **`+26 to +42 °C`** (`XW` / geothermal `BWh-thermal`).
3. **Structural Bridge to the Loom-Chasms (`Region 99`):** At its southern tip (`px 1660..1745, py 987..1032`), the Great Chasm's floor rises over a fractured basalt sill (`+450 to +750 m`) directly into the northern horst-and-graben labyrinth of `Region 99`, serving as the primary deep-crustal heat, brine, and buoyant-gas source for the Loom-Chasms.

### 2.2 Three-Tier Vertical Zonation

1. **Tier 1 — The Hypobaric Basalt Palisades & Vacuum Rim (`+5,500 m to +11,700 m`, `0.37–0.80 atm`, `O / HF / EF`):**
   - Sheer, step-faulted flood-basalt traps (each flow `40–120 m` thick with colonnade and entablature jointing) topped by dark rust-red oxidised regolith and black tachylite impact/fault glass.
   - Where katabatic moisture from the inner chasm condenses on the freezing upper cliff (`-25 to -45 °C`), `50 m`-tall curtains of yellow-white **sulfuric hoarfrost** and acid-rime icicles cling to the overhang lips.
2. **Tier 2 — The Sheeted Dike Galleries & Scoria Cataracts (`+1,200 m to +5,500 m`, `0.80–1.38 atm`, `ET / BWk`):**
   - Series of `3 to 5` back-tilted rotational fault benches (`2–6 km` wide) separated by `800–1,400 m` vertical cliff risers.
   - Cross-cutting the cliff faces are hundreds of vertical **sheeted feeder dikes** of dense titanium-basalt and purple-black obsidian (`4–18 m` wide) that weather out as freestanding buttresses, natural stone bridges, and slotted chimney flues above the abyss.
   - Dry avalanches of iridescent scoria, Pele's hair drifts, and sulfur-cemented talus cascade down V-chutes from bench to bench (**pyroclastic dry cataracts**).
3. **Tier 3 — The Abyssal Fire-Fissure & Dense-Air Thermal Trench (`-1,200 m to +400 m`, `1.52–1.86 atm`, `XW` / Geothermal Hyperarid):**
   - A `6 to 18 km`-wide inner graben floor plunged in perpetual canyon shadow (receiving direct zenith sunlight for only `75–110 minutes` at solar noon), illuminated from below by cherry-red **crustal tension skylights**, crusted lava lakes, and blue-burning sulfur vents.
   - Subaerial **black-smoker and white-smoker hydrothermal chimneys** (`8–28 m` tall, built of anhydrite, barite, chalcopyrite, and native sulfur) vent superheated (`140–280 °C`) magmatic brines into boiling, emerald-green and blood-red hypersaline acid-sulfate pools (`pH 1.2–3.5`).

## 3. Native Ecology: Life in the Six-Kilometre Abyss

Because the eastern Outlands plateau (`5.6–11.7 km`) is an airless or near-airless desert barrier (`O / HF`), the deep floor of the Great Chasm is an **isolated evolutionary island** bridged only at its southern sill to the Loom-Chasms (`Region 99`). Three native functional guilds dominate the chasm:

### 3.1 Abyssal Radiotrophic & Thermosynthetic *Chasm-Hearths* (*Pyrohearthia abyssalis*)
- Along the margins of crusted lava fissures and `80–160 °C` fumarole aprons on the `-1,180 to -300 m` floor, specialized members of the **Hearth** clade (*Chasm-Hearths*) tap volcanic ground heat through copper-sheathed, brine-filled heat-pipe taproots.
- Unlike upland hearth trees that radiate heat to a freezing night sky, *Chasm-Hearths* extend tall (`18–32 m`),chimney-like **convective cooling sails** into the updraft flues along the canyon walls, shedding waste heat into the rising katabatic-return air column to drive internal thermosynthesis in near-total darkness.

### 3.2 Sulfide-Oxidizing *Curtain-Gills* & Barite Organ-Pipes (*Branchiostroma sulfuricum*)
- On Tier-2 and Tier-3 cliff overhangs directly above boiling acid-brine pools and $\text{H}_2\text{S} / \text{SO}_2$ fumarole chimneys (`+400 m down to -1,100 m`), colonial **Gill** organisms form `30–80 m`-long pleated velvet curtains of crimson, ochre, and peacock-violet tissue.
- Their mineralized chitin-and-barite attachment ribs (`KSTRATA` / `CORAL` shelf morphology) filter rising hydrogen sulfide, sulfur dioxide, and steam, precipitating tiered aprons of golden barite and yellow orthorhombic sulfur crystals below each colony.

### 3.3 Buoyant *Loom-Spindle* Pioneers & Vent-Draft Drifters
- In the southern half of the chasm (`Y = 920..1032`, `1.50–1.78 atm`), the dense chasm air and powerful thermal updrafts support deep-rooted **buoyant cable-vines** (*Funisulcus pyrophilus*) that span `150–400 m` across narrow inner-fissure throats, suspending hydrogen-filled gas bladders in the warm updrafts above lava skylights.
- **Fauna:**
  - *Cinder-Skaters & Vent-Crabs:* Armoured, heat-shielded hexapods and blind white galatheid-like vent crustaceans grazing bacterial mats around subaerial black smokers.
  - *Thermal-Soaring Chasm-Drakes / Cliff-Stalkers:* Gliding predators riding the permanent `12–25 m/s` central thermal updraft column between Tier 3 (`-1,000 m`) and Tier 2 (`+2,800 m`).

1. **`core/terrain` (`KRELIEF`, `KSTRATA`, `KCAVERN`, `KSPELEO`):**
   - **`KRELIEF`:** Stepped rotational normal-fault horsts, vertical sheeted-dike ribs (`dike` / `fin`), and deep axial fissure trenches.

   - **`KCAVERN` & `KSPELEO`:** Drained feeder-dike galleries and giant **amethyst & celestine basalt geodes** (`KSPELEO.AMETHYST`, aligned with `biomes/CRYSTAL-CAVES-5.1.md` §3) exposed in collapsed chasm-wall vesicles.

   - Subaerial black-smoker sulfide cones, boiling acid-sulfate cauldrons (`pH 1.5`), and crusted lava-skylight thermal glow.

   - Extreme canyon-wall horizon occlusion (narrow zenith sky slit on Tier 3), dense volcanic haze (`SO2` / `H2S` / steam optical scatter), radiative lava glow at night, and `KHAZ` thermal/sulfide gas pockets in low undrained floor sumps.
