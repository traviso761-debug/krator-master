The Rift Line

The Ancients' four-track line under the Outer Wall, which the Tungangs still run in pieces: 304 km of bore from the foothills under Shelf to the tableland above Ledge, a hot middle only the sealed trains cross, and three falls the travellers walk round. This page shows the plan's proposals before anything is built.

The board segments and gaps

The Tungangs' timetable board, as a loj hall would paint it. Each solid run is a segment whose trains have been trapped between its falls for a thousand years and shuttle end to end; a dashed run is a gap walked on foot, with the walk's time. The hot section is the middle of the long run, closed by its two doors.

The profile scale model 5.1, surveyed

Dalab to Ledge on the 5.1 heights, with the rock the geology bake gives under each kilometre and the virgin rock temperature (the surface's mean plus 25 °C a kilometre of cover). The bore runs level at about 3.5 km; the crest over it reaches 10.4 km and is near-airless. The north end climbs inside the Wall on spirals; the south end is shallow, 30 to 60 m under a wet tableland.

Ore along the bore (the geology bake's bytes, 0-255): rift fill copper 120 and rare earths 190 · granite tin 200 and rare earths 160 · gneiss iron 160, gold and silver 110 · the north valley iron 180 · breccia the only nickel, 60. Shafts only where the cover is thin: km 4, 10, 24, 218 (the 2.2 km great shaft), 250, 260 and every 400 m in the shallow south; none over the hot section. All hidden from outsiders; those within 15 km of Dalab's road (km 4, 10, 24, 290-304) are a striking loj's raid points.

From the survey (biomes/line/tools/survey_line.py): 304 km bored · cover over 4 km for 113 km, over 6 km for 36 km · deepest 6,881 m at bore km 124 · rock over 60 °C from km 57 to 201 · shallowest point south of the crest km 215 (2,126 m), 25 km west of Loj Twenny.

The section to scale

One gallery, 37 m between the lining's faces and 14 m to a flattened vault: the underground twin of the Ancients' 30 m way. Four guideways (concrete beams with a guide slot, no rails), two each way; a 5 m strip between the pairs for the crossover pits, the signal masts and the lamp posts; a walkway on the east wall; and along the west wall the access road the owner asked for, two lanes for the Ancients' service vans behind a barrier. In bad ground it parts into two twin-track bores with a small service bore for the road between them.

States per track: built · kept · worn · blocked · cut · flooded (a depth). Per 20 m of section: intact · cracked · spalled · fallen · flooded · silted. A gap is wherever every track is cut; segments are derived from the record, never written.

The Line gives

A route per track along the guideway top; one KSCHED timetable per train with its dwells; the car pose by the riding plan's formula (pitch at most 0.04, no roll); each car's seats, standing room, bays and doors on its record; a stop for every platform face; the gap walks as routes.

Floors that ride in core/walk/frame, the ride leg and journeys in core/simulation/ride, capacity, cargo as records, porters across the gaps, the player's carry and the Godot twins. The Line swaps its stand-ins for the real records when both are built.

Above ground an Ancient highway runs beside the line, and over bog and water the whole corridor rides elevated decks like Louisiana's swamp highways. Underground a smaller access road runs beside the tracks.
