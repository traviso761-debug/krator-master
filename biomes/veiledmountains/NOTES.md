# biomes/veiledmountains: field notes

- **Geography & Climate:** Scale Model `5.1` polygon `Veiled Mountains` (`rmwzq1pa88m7q`), incised glacial/fluvial U-valleys (`Veilpass` `rmwvrcfyfbttj`, `mist valley 2` `rmwx2eaiwaen8`, `mist valley` `rmwx2k9csuncc`, `Region 177` `rmwzq42zszc94`; `Timpar-Kuran` homeland `rmwzqi9dwnd31`). Warm NW sea-drafts (`1,400–4,000 mm/yr`) funnel into `+2,100–+3,050 m` floors (`1.13–1.24 atm`) walled by `+5,000–+9,491 m` tilted impact `megabreccia`.
- **The Omnipresent Veil:** Condensational mist never drops to zero (`VEIL_PHYS.mistAt >= 0.22`, `uWet >= 0.55` even in `Sun-Break` mode), combining `ATMOS.heightFog` (`fogPool` + `fogMistField`), `ATMOS.fogBelt` (`fogSlab`), `ATMOS.cloudDeck`, `ATMOS.slopeWind`, and cataract `spray` + `fogBank` particles.
- **Stations (`5`):** `hall` (`veiledmountains.html`, `+2,380 m` nurse-log cathedral & `12 m` tree-ferns), `gorge` (`+2,560 m` Sol-Cleft cataract & `KSPAN` wedged logs/bridge), `kanopi` (`+2,650 m` `95 m` tilted Ancient weeping oculus watched by `KRUINS.watch`), `veilpass` (`+3,520–+5,400 m` braided boulder torrent, `slidealder` chutes & `KSTACKS` crag spires), and `southvale` (`+2,880 m` pillow-moss boulder labyrinth & red-peat trail).
