# veiledmountains: the plan (proposal & build-ready design, 2026-11-27)

**The Veiled Mountains** (Scale Model `5.1` biome polygon **`Veiled Mountains`**, redrawn live id **`rmwzq1pa88m7q`**, px `123–336 × 609–979`, succeeding `edit_535` id `rmwx28eysqk2e`; carved valley sub-polygons **`Veilpass`** `rmwvrcfyfbttj`, **`mist valley 2`** `rmwx2eaiwaen8` [was `Region 174`], **`mist valley `** `rmwx2k9csuncc` [was `Region 175`], and newly drawn southern **`Region 177`** `rmwzq42zszc94`; political homeland **`Timpar-Kuran`** `rmwzqi9dwnd31`, px `126–281 × 612–961`): the shattered north-western rim-and-valley massif between `Crookshore` / `Matron's Coast` on the NW ocean and the Eternal State's `w outer wall` bench on the east.

The biome's whole character is in its name: **omnipresent fog, cloud-veil, and valley mist**. Warm, moisture-laden north-westerlies off the Tsunami Coast (`1,400–3,460 mm/yr`) funnel directly into deeply incised glacial-and-fluvial U-valleys (`+2,100–+3,050 m` floors at `1.10–1.26 atm`) walled in by `+5,000–+9,491 m` Voronoi-tilted blocks of impact **`megabreccia`**. Chilled against cold valley floors and `1,400–2,600 m` rock walls, that sea air condenses into **permanent, multi-layered valley fog, mid-slope cloud-ribbons, cataract spray plumes, and dripping canopy haze** across every hour and season. Beneath the veil grows an **altitudinally varied, slightly cooler, super-lush version of the Olympic Hoh Rainforest** — colossal stilt-rooted nurse-log conifers and bryophyte-bolstered maples hung with `3–5 m` clubmoss curtains, **gigantic `8–14 m` arborescent tree-ferns**, prehistoric giant mire spore-spikes, pillowed cushion-moss boulder labyrinths under contorted elfin cloud-woods, and **ubiquitous Earth + alien epiphytes** (from emerald cistern-rosettes to `1.5–2.8 m` crimson-magenta-violet structural-tinted crown-chalices and opalescent Kratorian mist-gill brackets) — climbing up braided slide-alder U-headwaters to mist-wrapped `megabreccia` crags and the weeping ruined oculus of **`Kanopi`**, ancestral womb of the **`Utari-Kuran`** (`Hayrant`, `Hueru-Kuran`, and the unconquered **`Timpar-Kuran`** raiders who hold these valleys).

Status: **Build-ready biome plan** aligned with [LORE.md](../../LORE.md) §6.19 & §6.25, [kits/timpar-kuran/PLAN.md](../../kits/timpar-kuran/PLAN.md), `tools/scale-model/edit_531_mistheights.py`, `edit_535_veiled_mountains.py`, `edit_536_wind_climate.py`, and the owner's `2026-11-28` live region polygons (Section 1.2).

### 3.2 How the omnipresent fog & mist work (`VEIL_PHYS` + `core/atmos`)

Unlike dry or coastal biomes where fog is only one weather state among many, **in `biomes/veiledmountains` the veil never drops to zero**:
1. **Three stacked `core/atmos` mist layers active in every weather mode** (modulated, never switched off, by `ATMOS.weather` and `KCLOCK`):
   - **Valley-Floor Radiation & Bog Pool (`ATMOS.heightFog` `fogPool` + `fogMistField`):** Cool, blue-emerald ground mist (`0.8–4.5 m` thick in `hall` & `southvale`, thickest at dawn `04:00–09:00` and along slow streams, `image_10`, `image_12`, `image_13`), baked from `VEIL_PHYS.mistAt(x, z)` so hollows, tarns, and nurse-log colonnades hold lingering pools while dry spurs stand slightly clearer.
   - **Mid-Slope Cloud-Belt & Gorge Veil (`fogSlab` / `ATMOS.fogBelt` + `ATMOS.cloudDeck`):** A drifting, wind-warped stratus band hugging `+2,600–+3,550 m` (`image_7`, `image_11`), threading directly through the upper tree crowns on `hall`/`kanopi` and immersing the entire elfin moss-wood on `gorge`/`southvale`.
   - **Cataract Plunge-Spray & Canopy Drip (`ATMOS.smoke` spray kind + `fogBank` + `fx/raincover`):** Rising white vapour plumes at every waterfall foot (`image_4`, `image_8`, `image_11`), while `cloudShadeScene` keeps bark, moss bolsters, and `megabreccia` faces glistening wet (`uWet >= 0.55` baseline even in `"sun-break"` mode!).
