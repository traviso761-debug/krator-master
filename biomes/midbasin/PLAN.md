# midbasin: the plan (seed, 2026-11-03)

**Mid Basin** (`rmvmidbasin101` on scale model 5.1) is the owner's transitional zone between the Siphon Pits
(`biomes/siphon-pits/PLAN.md`) and the Loom-Chasms (`biomes/loom-chasms/PLAN.md`). The owner's brief (2026-11-03): its identity
is **the geology that crashing those two features together would make**. It is not a blend of their looks. It is the
landforms that only exist where the Pits' volcanism meets the Chasms' propagating faults.

This is a seed: the full plan (stations, flora, fauna, exchanges, Core adoption) is still to write, in the style of the other
biome plans, before any build.

## What the map already carves (Gemini, `tools/scale-model/smodel/se_provinces.py`)

- **A volcano-tectonic hinge.** It runs north-south from Region 99 (the Loom-Chasms side, py ~857..965) to the Siphon Pits'
  northern border (py ~1060..1101).
- **The Western Loess-and-Tuff Corridor** (+1,950..+2,320 m), joining the Ostrukh Terraces to the Weavers' Cradle, with a
  gentle saddle at about +2,010 m in the central west.
- **Three arcuate, en-echelon faulted basalt relay benches** stepping down from the Siphon Pits plateau (+4,350 m) to the
  Loom-Chasms' Shuttle Mesas (+1,550 m), on the central and eastern hinge ramp (px ~1295..1485).
- **Six horseshoe breached maar-diatreme amphitheatres.** Their northern rims are cleaved by the Loom's warp-chasm faults as
  they propagate south. **Warm artesian springs** come up in them, fed by seepage from fresh Lake Ostrukh, and cascade north
  into Region 99.

## The collision, to develop

- The maars are where the two meet: a Pits explosion crater sliced open by a Chasm fault. Each breach is a cross-section
  through the diatreme (tuff rings, breccia pipe, baked country rock), with a warm spring at its foot.
- The relay benches are basalt from the Pits pulled apart by the Chasms' extension: tilted blocks, fault scarps, columnar
  joints broken across.
- In the loess-and-tuff corridor, the Pits' ash and the Chasms' wind-blown silt interbed (`KSTRATA` layers;
  `kits/resources/ROCK-PROPOSAL.md` has the region's tuff and basalt).
- Hydrothermal ground where the faults tap the diatremes: mineral crusts and warm wet ground at the spring lines (`KTHERM`,
  `KHAZ`).
- Flora and fauna live in the gradient: a mixed edge of the Pits' and the Chasms' species (read in place), plus what only the
  spring-fed breaches can hold.
