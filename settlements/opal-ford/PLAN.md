# Ōpal-Phōrḍ, the Opal Ford: a river town on the opal road south of Gihon (plan, 2026-11-23)

The owner, 2026-11-23: *"we could also stand to put a settlement or two ... south of gihon (in the empty area south of
it)"*; asked the same day, the owner chose **"Opal-road river town"**: a town of about 1,500 on the river from Opal Valley to
Gihon, laid out in full on core/city (over 500, BUILD-OUT section 5), and its economy record added **with its build**, not
before (the Gihon session's tune changes are on its own branch). The order across Xanadu is
`settlements/xanadu/BUILD-PLAN.md`. Plans only. Everything not from the owner, the lore or the tune is *proposed*, the name
among it (in the Sultanate's loan-English, as Gōlḍ-Mārch and Lāiṭ-Hāus are).

## 2. What it is (*proposed*)

**The opal road's river town.** The owner's Gihon brief re-routes the opal from Lāiṭwāl to Gihon's Mint. The road from
Opal Valley comes down the Sitār Ḍrāi, and Ōpal-Phōrḍ is where it crosses: the last ford before the Lower Lake, and the
first stage out of Gihon for the caravans going up to Lāiṭwāl. It lives by the road and the flats:
- **The han and the ford.** A caravanserai at the ford, the paved ford itself with a footbridge beside it for the wet
  months, the toll house where the Sultan's factor counts what passes, and a post of the Golden Tears, who escort the opal
  and the bullion (`T.tears`; Gihon is their home in the Gihon brief).
- **The flats.** Rice terraces and paddies on both banks, watered from the river by a weir and its channels; the threshing
  floors and a watermill on the race.
- **The river.** A few dugouts and a ferry raft for the high water; fish weirs.

**People and trades** (*proposed*, for the tune record its build adds): 1,500, diet `town`, about 270 households; works:
the rice terraces (a share of its grain, Gihon sending the rest), the han (a stop on the road `road.gihon-laitwal`,
*proposed*), the ford's toll (a road `cut`, the Sultan's, as the Strongbow lift's toll is), the fish weirs (a small
fishery). Its people come out of P5's bulk district, as the rings' do.

## 2b. The Iridescent Order's house at the cataract (the owner, 2026-11-23)

The owner, 2026-11-23: *"Opal phord will also contain the monastery HQ of the iridescent order, which we will need to design"*;
asked the same day, the owner chose:
- **"Both are big houses"**: Rēnbō-Phāl at Lāiṭwāl's lake outlet (`settlements/iridescent/PLAN.md`, pin `siridesc0001`) stays the
  Order's head house; Ōpal-Phōrḍ holds its **second great house**, the Order's seat in the lowlands, on the opal road where it
  leaves the valley.
- **"At the cataract"**: its own precinct at the cataract's foot up the river from the town, the bow standing in the spray; the
  town is its lay town, joined to it by the river road.
- **"~450, Qiblai Turquoise"**: about 450 (some 300 monks and novices, the lay servants and their households, a guest house of
  60 beds), in the Qiblai kit's dress for the Turquoise spoke, the Hall of the Bow glazed with the Glaziers' moonglass.
- **"Town first, then design"**: step OF1 lays the town with the precinct's ground kept clear; the house's blueprint is its own
  step (OF1b, below), as the Beacon's and the Tempest's were, and then goes into the page.

**The cataract** (the scale model): the Sitār Ḍrāi falls from 265 m (px 981.5,1641.5) to 120 m (975,1637.5) and on to the town
(-14 m at 969,1634) and below it to -155 m (964,1629): the town stands in the middle of the cataract reach, on a bench of flats
where the river runs slack for a few kilometres (the ford). *Proposed:* the page draws the reach above the town as rapids with
one fall of about 20 m some 3 km up (the bow's fall), and the precinct on the north-east bank at its foot.

## 3. The layout (on core/city)

*Proposed:* the town on the river's east bank on the first rise above the flood (the flats flood in the wet season), its
main street the road down to the ford, the han and the market square at the ford's head, the temple of the Decree above
the square, the houses in blocks either side of the road and along the bank, the paddies on the flats round it and the
terraces on the rise behind. Quarters: the ford (the han, the toll house, the Tears' post: civic), the market (market),
the bank (fishers' and ferrymen's houses: harbour), the town (residential, the rest), the farm edge (farm).

**Kit pieces:** `xa_caravanserai` (88c), `xa_river_span` and `xa_bridge`, `xa_customs_house` (88g, v 1: a road's toll house
with its toll bar), `xa_fort_post` (the Tears' post), `xa_temple`, `xa_shops`, `xa_stall`, `xa_watermill`, `xa_granary`,
`xa_farm`, `xa_farmhouse`, the houses (`xa_poor_*`, `xa_mid_*`, `xa_rich_*`, a share of the Andean set). **Missing:** a
**paved ford** (`xa_ford`: stepping stones and a paved causeway under the water, *proposed* for `kits/xanadu/extra/`), and
**fish weirs** (a stand-in of stakes until one is drawn).
