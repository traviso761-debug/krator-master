# settlements/pher: known issues

- **The ground is the page's own.** A closed form of the survey's profile (`phH`), not the open world's ground. Placing
  the town in `openworld/xanadu-north-shore` (a town footprint on core/world, PLAN.md step P7) will need the two to meet.
- **The caravanserai is owed.** The Xanadu kit has none; Qiblai's kit has one (`qb_caravanserai`), which this page does
  not load. The caravan yard stands in: two warehouses, a corral and pens.
- **No flora.** The page plants no trees or gardens yet (Qiblai builds the Vale of Xanadu biome over its mask: a later
  pass).
- **No interiors.** The governor's house, the factor house and the inns are shells (PLAN.md step P5).
- **No boats.** The lake craft are owed (PLAN.md step P4).
- **The ground is painted, not textured.** A canvas of colours (sand, grass, the plain's fields, the ways), not the
  library's sets: a later pass.
- **The wall's middle is the avenue's alone.** Between the two levels the switchbacks carry only a stall or teahouse at
  each turn; the plan's houses along the avenue's lower legs (PLAN.md section 3) are not laid yet.
- **The stair avenue's grade.** Lower Phēr's stair avenue climbs straight up the wall (about 12 %): a stepped street for
  people and pack animals. The carts take the switchbacks.
