# Dilihan — Known Issues & Scope Notes (`settlements/dilihan`)

1. **Scale Model 5.1 Coordinate Reference (`ATMOS.siteAt`):** `core/atmos/sky/89-atmos-e-skymodel.js` still defaults `refPx` to the `4.21` grid (`764, 690`). As documented in `PLAN.md` §2.2, Dilihan passes explicit `{ latitude: -31.50, subGiantLon: 56.27 }` (`4.21` `px 472, 333` = `5.1` `px 744 - 272, 663 - 330`) to `ATMOS.skyBiome` and locks `JJ_SOLSTICE` before building `jj_temple_sun`, verified by positive and negative tests in `tests/test-dilihan.js` and `verify.py --assert`.
2. **Marr Pilgrim Envoys (`kits/marr`):** Per `PLAN.md` §5.3, `kits/marr` does not yet exist in the repository; the Southern Pass & Lower Town host the walled **Hykkousoi League Envoys' House** (`dl_hykkousoi_legation`, `hykkousoi` catalog culture) and customs post without creating stub Marr kits.
3. **Lightweight Instanced Citizens & Muster Ranks:** Citizens, Eastern Terrace muster ranks, and North Caravanserai pack trains draw through the shared `KIT` instancer (`figB`, `figH`, `vWood`, `vSack`) at zero extra draw calls and zero extra shader samplers while `core/charactermodels` crowd integration remains a separate roadmap phase.
