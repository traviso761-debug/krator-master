Stocks
At the site: made, not yet hauled (a producer's own store). In transit: a cart's or a carrier's load (a caravan, a barge, a ship, on the map), and a lot landed at the caravanserai, a quay or a pier for the carts. In storage: the warehouses and granaries, and the households' food stores. Owned: what the citizens wear, keep at home and work with. Shops and stalls keep no shelf: they sell from the warehouses. The change is the total at one midnight against the one before (the simulation's daily snapshot); used up = drawn from the stores, less what went into households' stores, plus what was eaten from them (an item: worn out and broken). Low: fewer days of cover (the warehoused stock over its use a day) than the economy's import threshold. Out: a draw or a purchase went unmet. Click an item for its flows and holders.
