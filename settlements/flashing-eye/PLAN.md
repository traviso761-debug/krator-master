# The Order of the Flashing Eye: its head house on the east volcano (site plan, 2026-11-16)

The crimson great monastery of Qiblai's council (LORE.md 6.13; `settlements/qiblai/PLAN.md`) has had no head house
(`core/simulation/PROPOSAL-XANADU.md`: "no head house is given for it yet"). It holds Sekriḍ, the shrine village on
Kirstabal's lake (986,1588), and its Prismarch takes a tenth of what the council shares out
(`settlements/kirstabal/PLAN.md`). This plan settles the site; the house itself (its layout, blueprint and page) comes next.

## 0. The owner's decisions (2026-11-16)

- **The name is the Order of the Flashing Eye**, as the lore has it ("Flashing eye is correct").
- **The site is the east volcano of Erewhon's lake**, `volc_erewhon_east`, summit 881,1526 on scale model 5.1 ("Agree with
  crimson recc"). A cliffside house on the cone.
- **It faces the Rift**, south, not the lake ("Will face the rift").
- **The cone gets a vent** ("Give it a cone"): the dormant volcano becomes a smoking crater on the scale model, a vent
  record queued with the scale-model session (`edit_517` drew it "not vents"). The house's fire is the mountain's own.

- **Neighbours.** Ṭī-Ṭeras (mist tea "on the east volcano's flank") 25 km, Hoṭ-Bāṭh ("the east volcano's hot springs;
  sulfur and pumice") 28 km, the moved `march.e` and the Beacon (853,1540) 63 km, Ālph-Hēḍ 47, Theodiga 57, Erehwon 82.
  The lake is 26 km off, the Ālph and its barge road 28 km. No road crosses the cone.
- **The other two volcanoes are claimed:** the west (829,1526) holds Erehwon's basalt quarries, the middle (856,1532)
  stands by the Beacon. The east one was the only one free.
- **What the summit sees** (the Beacon plan's method: eye 20 m up, target 15 m, Earth's curve, k 0.13): Erehwon (82 km),
  Grēṭ-Āil (84), Phēr (103), Ōlḍ-Thalaba (73), Ālph-Hēḍ (47), the Beacon (63), the Ālph wall (129), and south over the Rift
  to the Golden Tears' camp (213). Not Theodiga (in its gorge) or Qiblai.
- **No true cliff at model scale.** The house's cliff is the page's to author, as the Beacon and Strongbow plans do: the
  crater's south wall, or a lava-dome or columnar-basalt face on the south flank. Facing the Rift puts the house on the
  south side of the cone, its back to the lake and the capital.

## 2. What the site gives the Order

- **Fire and an eye.** A live crater, lamps round its rim, a fumarole's glow: the Flashing Eye's own sign, seen from
  every lakeshore town's skyline behind it and from the Rift in front.
- **A signal partner.** It sees the Beacon (the gold Order) at 63 km and the Rift beyond: the Beacon's lamp relayed on
  to the south, or a rivalry of lights.
- **Supply on the doorstep.** The tea village on its flank and the bath village at its foot are already in
  `core/region/data/xan-tune.js`; the barge road is 28 km off.
