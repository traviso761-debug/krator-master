# settlements/qiblai: Qiblai, the holy city of the Decree

The city of the faith of the Decree (*Ḍikrī*) in the Xanadu Sultanate, at the cataracts of Nahr ash-Shalālāt: a mandala of
nested walled squares round the Temple Mountain and its Rainbow Temple, ruled by the Prismarchy (`LORE.md` §6.13). Built from
the Qiblai kit (`kits/qiblai`) and the Xanadu kit on core/city's nested-squares site. `PLAN.md` is the build plan (Q7) and the
owner's answers; `kits/qiblai/PLAN.md` the design.

## Build and verify

```
python3 build.py                                   # dist/qiblai.html
KRATOR_GL=gpu python3 verify.py dist/qiblai.html --assert --views "Overview,From the south gate,The Rainbow Temple"
python3 ../../tools/build.py settlements/qiblai    # the same through the repo's build wrapper
```

`build.py` writes `gen/69q-kcity-bundle.js` (core/city and core/space sealed in one closure, as Verge seals core/city) and
the Qiblai kit's Xanadui script, then runs `settlements/xanadu/build.py --kit settlements/qiblai`: the city is a target on
Xanadu's page as Erewhon is, with the Qiblai kit's fragments read in place (`kit.json` `externalDirs`) and its texture pack
(`packFrom`). Xanadu's own pages come out byte-identical without `--kit`.

## How the city is made

| Fragment | What |
|---|---|
| `targets/city/89z-rows.js` | `window.CITY` (Xanadu's showcase is skipped), the title, no sheet rows |
| `targets/city/89zq-qc-site.js` | the site and the plan, as records: `KCITY.squares.plan` on the country (a plane falling gently east), the terraces built up (`KCITY.squares.ground`), the river's channel; the gardens and courtyards in a pattern symmetric about both axes and both diagonals, fading from the centre (`QC_GARDEN`, `QC_COURT`), and the four meditation gardens in the corners; the core/city instance (`KCITY.squares.steps`, then `infill`: frontage-courts with the kits' keys by pool, a third of the lay houses Xanadu's); row houses' party walls (`o.ends` from their neighbours); the Temple Mountain, the eight monasteries (whole on the diagonal spokes, split astride the avenue on the axial four), the gate markets; the people by ring |
| `targets/city/90p-qc-flora.js` | the meditation gardens' plants picked by colour: `qnMedPlant.use` answers a garden's colour role and kind with a species, an Opal Valley kin tree or a kit's floor plant from the Rift, the Rift Rim, Oidong, the Vale of Xanadu or Opal Valley (`gen/69r-flora-bundle.js`, `QC_FLORA`: today's core/biome and the five kits sealed in one closure, generated by `build.py`) or the page's own Vale (the ginkgo); `qcFloraBake` grows and bakes them after the city (`window._gardensFlora`: asked, grown, gaps) |
| `targets/city/90t-qc-interiors.js` | the great rooms, on demand: the Rainbow Temple's sanctum, the Hall of the Decree's council chamber, every great monastery's assembly hall (twelve, in their spokes' colours), each whole one's gate hall (the four guardian kings), a hostel's dormitory, each the kit's interior piece drawn full height inside its building (kits/qiblai `89q5`, `o.cut` false) and furnished by the kit's pass (`89q5f`), placed and baked the first time the camera comes within 70 m; walked into (core/walk: each room's floor and walls, its drawn doors unlocked with a depth-only pane in each, flights up to raised sills; each monastery's court as a floor with a flight to its gate; the Rainbow Temple's three terraces and their flights); `window._qiblaiIx` (`buildAll()`, `walkIn()` for the verify); a view inside each |
| `targets/city/90u-qc-lot-interiors.js` | every lot building's interior, on demand: the shell each building's storey stacks gave while 90r drew it (kits/qiblai `89q5s`, `QB_SHELLREC`), its programme from its tags (a dwelling, a shop, a workshop, a tavern, a temple, a civic hall, a store); each type (key and shell, every storey) planned (kits/interiors `planBuilding`: its rooms, a stair between storeys), drawn with the kit's textured items (floors and their stair wells, ceilings, walls, stairs) and furnished once, then cloned onto every lot of it within 34 m. The street door is the drawn one (core/door's record), unlocked when the lot takes its clone, with a doorway drawn through the wall and a depth-only pane on the face; the plan's floors, walls, stairs and entry flight go into core/walk at the lot's pose. The monasteries' cell ranges are lots here too, on the monks' programme (kits/qiblai `QB_IX_MONKS`), entered from the court; `window._qiblaiLix` (`buildTypes(n)`, `walkIn(n)` for the verify); views inside (one upstairs, the monks' quarters) and at an open front door |
| `targets/city/90q-qc-world.js` | the Krator sky and its light, the ground mesh (8 m cells) wearing a canvas painted from the plan's records (never read back), the river |
| `targets/city/90r-qc-build.js` | the walls (each segment a `qb_wall` run of its length on an ashlar retaining face), gates and towers; the kora's arcades and its chortens; the ghat way, the ghats, the caravanserai; every building, chunk by chunk (400 m), each chunk baked into its own group and drawn while in view and range |
| `targets/city/91z-views.js` | the views; the city as one budget (as Erewhon's) |
| `verify.py` | Xanadu's verify plus the city's invariants: the site laid, the monasteries on their spokes' bearings, the people by ring and the Xanadu share (reported) |

**Spokes and colours.** core/city's site numbers the spokes 0-7 clockwise from north and gives each monastery `colour = spoke`;
the kit's `QSPOKE` is in the same order, and the Rainbow Temple's banners face the same bearings. The Temple Mountain is placed
unturned, so the banners face true bearings.
