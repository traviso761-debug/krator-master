# Major Historical & Pilgrim Landmarks of Jimjam: Blueprint

Covers Jimjam's three non-palace, non-temple landmark complexes:
1. **The Decayed Izani Governor's House (*Sid'kiñ-Pāḷu*, `70 × 56 m`)** — built ~600 years ago on the canonical `VASSAL-KITS.md` §3 7-pavilion *praetorium* plan during Iziz's northern basin occupation (`LORE.md` §6.2), now fallen into monsoon ruin and layered adaptive reuse;
2. **The Order of Historians' Small Chapterhouse (`48 × 42 m`)** — built in Jimjam banded brick with the Order's signature parabolic pylon gate, cobalt-blue mosaic string course, round drum reading hall, and clean electric oculus (`LORE.md` §6.15);
3. **The Great Pilgrim's Market & Cut-Gate Caravanserai Fairground (`320 × 220 m` + `57.2 × 71.6 m`)** — the seasonal flood-bazaar (`SIM.pilgrimMarket` in `77-sim-6p-pilgrims.js`) where arriving `Serai`, `Hykkousoi`, `Eternal State`, `Dilihan`/`Ardent`, **White-Pass `Yeti`/`Yonni`/`Rian`**, and `Inner Crater`/`Dalab–Shim`/`Yuni` streams trade rare reagents, tech, and artifacts.
