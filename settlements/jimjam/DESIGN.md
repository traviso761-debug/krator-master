# Design notes — Round 1

Jimjam architecture brings brick to a large, legible scale: deep red and warm yellow running-bond masonry, pale marble courses, gold, dark slate, and terracotta. The helper gallery tests the core silhouette vocabulary before any full settlement is placed.

The shaft study is the close-range acceptance view. It compares six surface patterns in both texture-only and raised-relief forms. Dome materials and profiles are shown together, while the arcade, crenellated wall, stacked chimney and tiered spires test shared masonry details. The plaza carries a radial sun inlay. All structures use a local ground-level frame with +z as the front.

## Round 1 scope

The current site table contains one registered helper-gallery volume. Jimjam district layouts, the final culture pack, temple and calendar alignment, life layers, flythrough, and full documentation belong to later stages.
