# settlements/tempest: the House of the Tempest

The Order of the Violet Storm's mother house on the West Arm (scale model px 886,1619, pin `stempest0001`, the crest at 519 m), a
concentric dzong round the Qiblai kit's violet great monastery. The blueprint (the sheets, the owner's forks, the lore, the
furniture manifest, `tempest_plan.json`) is `settlements/strongwall/tempest/`; this folder is its build. `PLAN.md` has the steps
(0 and 1 done, 2026-11-23) and the core adoption; `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` what is not here yet and what the fort asset is asked for.

## Build, test and verify

```
node tests/test-tempest.js                                   # step 0: the layout's rules (from the repo root: node settlements/tempest/tests/test-tempest.js)
python3 ../../tools/build.py settlements/tempest             # dist/tempest.html (from the repo root: tools/build.py settlements/tempest)
KRATOR_GL=gpu python3 verify.py dist/tempest.html --assert --views "Overview,The Main Gate,The inner court and the monastery"
```

In a fresh worktree, pull the textures first: `python3 tools/lfs_needs.py settlements/xanadu --pull` and
`git lfs pull --include="kits/qiblai/tex/*,kits/catalog/tex/*"`.

## How the page is made

| Fragment | What |
|---|---|
| `src/80t-tp-plan-data.js` | `TEMPEST` `[G data]`: the layout as records in the Xanadui fort's shape (XFORT, `kits/xanadu` 80f): the outer ward (260 x 190 m, 4 corner drums, interval towers at 30 m, the Main Gate east with its gate towers, the sally gates north and south, the Storm Tower on the west front, the barbican), the inner ward (110 x 96 m on a 4 m talus, its curtain 12 m over the outer ground), the utse astride its front with the inner gate, the ranges and blocks, the open grounds, the violet monastery it hosts; XFORT's rules and the concentric ones (`TEMPEST.check`); `TEMPEST.outer`, `TEMPEST.inner` the wards apart |
| `src/80u-tp-house.js` | the drawing: two defs, `xa_tempest_outer` (the outer ward, the talus, the utse as the fort's keep with its gilt chapel and beacon) and `xa_tempest_inner` (the inner ward, the Field of the Fallen, the Founder's Chorten), by kits/xanadu 80g's builders, read in place |
| `targets/house/89z-rows.js` | `window.CITY`, the title, no sheet rows |
| `targets/house/89zs-tp-site.js` | the site as data: the model's ground (`tpModel`), the House's place (its middle on the pin, its front east: `TPS`), the pad (`tpGround`: the lot levelled to the median of the model's ground there, eased back over 60 m) |
| `targets/house/90q-tp-world.js` | the Krator sky and its light; the near ground (4 km at 10 m) and the far land out to 40 km |
| `targets/house/90r-tp-house.js` | the two wards at the pad and 4 m up, `qb_monastery` (o.v 6) in the inner ward; baked; the page's record (`window._tempest`) |
| `targets/house/91z-views.js` | seven views; the House as one budget |
| `gen/69t-tp-ground.js` | GENERATED by `build.py` (`tools/scale_ground.py`): the model's ground round the pin |
| `verify.py` | Xanadu's verify plus `tempest-laid`, `tempest-rules`, `on-the-model`, `house-on-its-pad`, `house-walks` |

**The ground is the model's.** At the pin the model gives 518.8 m. The pad is at 518.7 m with 1.3 m of cut and 2 m of fill: the
crest is flat.

**The verify** (2026-11-23, `KRATOR_GL=gpu`, the RTX 5060 lane): every assertion passes, the House's five among them (149 of 149
walks and stairs of the outer ward reached, 74 of 74 of the inner; 88 registered volumes; 0.48 M triangles, 56 draw calls in the
overview).
