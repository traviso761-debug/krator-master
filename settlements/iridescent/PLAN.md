# The Iridescent Order's head monastery at the lake's outlet (plan, 2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-16: *"there will also be a monastery of the Teal great Qiblai monastery in Laitwal, the Iridescent Order"*;
2026-11-17: *"can we find a good site for an Iridescent Order main monastery somewhere in the valley?"*, and of the five sites
surveyed, **"C, the lake's outlet"**.

What is settled: the turquoise (teal) great monastery of Qiblai is **the Iridescent Order** (`LORE.md` called it "not yet named");
it keeps a house in Lāiṭwāl (`settlements/lightwall`: the Qiblai kit's `qb_monastery` in the Turquoise spoke's dress on the
eastern spur); its **head monastery** stands at the outlet of Lāiṭwāl's lake, where Terrace II's lake spills over the lower riser.
Everything else here is *proposed* and marked so; section 8 asks the owner.

**A second great house (the owner, 2026-11-23).** *"Opal phord will also contain the monastery HQ of the iridescent order"*;
asked, the owner chose **"Both are big houses"**: Rēnbō-Phāl here stays the head house, and the Order keeps a second great house
(about 450, the Qiblai kit's Turquoise dress) at Ōpal-Phōrḍ, the opal road's river town at the valley's mouth, in its own
precinct at the cataract's foot (`settlements/opal-ford/PLAN.md` section 2b, step OF1b). This is close to the survey's site D.

Files: `survey_site.py` (the survey, re-run from the scale model's data), `sites.png` (the five sites on the valley's map).

**Why it reads as iridescent** (`biomes/opalvalley/PLAN.md` section 2): the lake is milky turquoise with the silica in it (the
Order's own colour); it spills over dams of banded harlequin sinter, opal cementing the riser's cliffs; the cliff glaze shifts
from copper-rose to teal as the sun moves; and the falls' spray stands in bows whenever the sun is behind the watcher.

## 2. The Order (*proposed*, for the owner)

- **Its name** is the owner's: the Iridescent Order, the turquoise monastery of the Prismarchy, its spoke in Qiblai its town house
  on the council (spoke 4, `QSPOKE` 'Turquoise').
- **Its devotion** (*proposed*): the light divided. Where the Rainbow Temple flies all eight colours, the Iridescent Order keeps the
  moment the white light breaks into them: the bow in the falls' spray, the play of colour in an opal, a prism's fan. Its morning
  office is said facing the falls while the bow stands in them; its monks read the colours the way the Beacon reads its lamp.
- **Its tie to the Glaziers** (*proposed*): the Glaziers of the wall make its moonglass and its prisms (their crystal-opal lenses,
  `kits/tungang/PLAN.md` 1.4); the Order is their oldest customer, and the house in Lāiṭwāl is where the two meet.
- **The head monastery's name** (*proposed*): **Rēnbō-Phāl**, "the Rainbow Fall", in the Xanadui English-in-Indic form of
  *Ōpal-Bhālī* and *Lāiṭwāl*.

## 3. Who lives there (*proposed*)

About **450**: some 300 monks and novices, 60 lay servants and their households, a guest house that sleeps 60 pilgrims, and the
boatmen of the lake landing. The sister houses for scale: the Viridian estate ~400, the Tempest ~600, Grēṭ-Āil 900 with its
fishing villages, the Beacon ~300.

## 4. The programme and the layout (*proposed*)

On the shelf below the sill, its long axis along the sill, facing the falls and the north-west (the bow stands in the spray when
the sun is behind):
- **The landing at the sill**: the lake's boats from Lāiṭwāl tie up in the slack water above the lip.
- **The Hall of the Bow** (the assembly hall): its north wall of moonglass panes by the Glaziers, a prism in each, so the morning
  throws the eight colours across the floor.
- **The cells** stepping down the riser beside the falls, each with a window on the spray.
- **The Kora of the Falls**: the circuit down the falls' stair to the first plunge pool and back up the far bank, crossing the
  outflow above the lip by a bridge.
- **The guest house and stables** on the valley road where it leaves the lake for Terrace III.
- **Gardens and orchards** on the shelf (Opal Valley's middle terrace: the opal-bark and prism-pine kin, `biomes/opalvalley`).
- Built in the **Qiblai kit**'s dress for the Turquoise spoke (as the house in Lāiṭwāl), on the **Xanadu kit**, with the
  **Glaziers' moonglass** (`kits/tungang` gz set) for the Hall of the Bow.

## 5. The economy (*proposed*; for the sim session, nothing done in the tune)

- **People** 450 (the town diet), fed from Lāiṭwāl by boat (24 km of lake) with the shelf's gardens.
- **Its income**: Qiblai's grant to the eight Orders; pilgrims' gifts; a tithe on the opal the valley's Glaziers set aside for the
  Order (*proposed*: the owner's call).
- In `core/region/data/xan-tune.js` it would be a settlement under `slaitwal0001` (Lāiṭwāl), a lake road to it.
