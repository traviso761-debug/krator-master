# Theodiga today: furniture manifest

Generated by `theodiga_blueprint.py` (on `settlements/voth/palace/blueprint.py`) from the same room data as the sheets. Keys are catalog pieces (`kits/catalog`): `xanadu_court_*` and `xanadu_common_*` (the Xanadu sets, through Longsong's key map), `order_*`, `generic_*`. Each room is counted for ONE storey: the crest's blocks have four, the gallery four, a fin head eleven; the census (BLUEPRINT.md) says how many are lived in. The site sheet (T0) is the town's quarters, not rooms: it is not furnished here; core/city lays its lots.

Decor: 0 bare (stores, cells), 1 plain (service), 2 fine (offices, the lesser guests), 3 rich (state guests, kin, officials' residences), 4 sumptuous (the state rooms, the ruler's own).
