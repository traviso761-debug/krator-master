# Zeijani kit: the contract for a world that uses it

The kit is a registry of building defs (`zj_*`) and the code that places them: the geometry engine, the cavern host that
carves the carved defs into rock, the walk registry they write, the furniture and interiors pass, the lamps, the camera and
dev tools. A world takes the kit's `src/` as it is, drops the sheet's host and supplies its own. `settlements/dhelv` is the
worked example. `DESIGN.md` says how to write a def; `PLAN.md` sections 5 to 9 are the reasoning.

Units are metres; x east, y up, z south. A person is 1.75 m; a Zeijani doorway 2.1 m.

## Including the kit in a world

`settlements/dhelv/build.py` is the pattern. The page is one script, in filename order:

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| core | `rand`, `walk`, `materials/record`, `tags`, `furnish`, `atmos`, `sockets` (the kit's set), plus `terrain/39-core-cavern.js`. Dhelv adds `clock`, `sched`, `simulation` (its ramblers) and core/biome (its forests) |
| `kits/zeijani/src/*` | every fragment **less the sheet's host**: `KIT_SKIP = {'00-head.html', '41-zj-block.js', '89-rows.js', '90-scene.js', '91-probe.js'}` (the head, the test block, the rows, the scene, the probe) |
| the world's `src/*` | its layout, head, scene and probe. A file with a kit or core file's name overrides it (record why in its `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`) |
| virtual fragments | generated by `build.py`, never in `src/`: `26-matlib-pack.js` (the kit's `tex/` as data URLs; Dhelv has no `materials.json` of its own), `38-furniture-bundle.js` (`furniture_bundle.bundle(['zeijani', 'nomad', 'generic', 'generic-goods'])`: `KratorFurniture`), `39-interiors-bundle.js` (`kit_bundle.bundle(['zeijani'])`: `KratorInteriors`, holding the carved defs' void plans) |

The checks `build.py` runs are the kit's: one shared scope (no name declared twice), every building fragment (4x to 7x)
opens with `// prefix: xx` and declares only names with that prefix, every `defBuilding` has a unique `seed`, the port lint
(`tools/check_port.py`), and `node --check` when node exists.

**What the world's host must declare** (the names the sheet's `90-scene.js` declares and the kit's fragments read):
`renderer scene camera skyScene SKY qs hemi sun LIGHTDIR skyApply groundMat groundM ZJ_CUTSITES ZJ_MASSES ZJ_HOLES SITES
ONLY ROWS WORLD buildWorld autoViews ATMOS_HOOKS ATMOS_GROUND`, a head with the error panel (`#errs`), and its own
`window._api`, `hostChecks`, `hostNegatives`. Copy them from `settlements/dhelv/src/90-dhelv-scene.js`, not from memory.

**The world's ground.** `10-core.js` defines `terrainH(x, z)` as 0 (the flat sheet). A world reassigns it
(`terrainH = DH.groundY` in Dhelv); `place()`, the cavern and the camera read it. A world with an underground also defines
`camGroundY(p)` (`92-camera.js` calls it when it exists: no floor while the camera is inside a void).

**Building the world.** `buildWorld()` resets the buckets, `REG`, `ZJ_LIFE`, the halos and the smoke; starts the tags
registry (`zjTagsReset()`), clears `KWALK`, makes a fresh cavern (`cvNew()`), sets the culture pack
(`CULT.cur = CULT.packs.zeijani`), places every site with `place()`, flushes the buckets, then builds the cavern
(`cvFinish(WORLD)` on the sheet; Dhelv calls `CVC.build()` and meshes its chunks near the camera with `cvChunkMesh`).

## Defs (`36-def.js`)

`defBuilding(d)` registers a def in `DEFS[key]`. Its fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `key`, `name`, `seed` | `zj_*`; a unique four-digit seed (build.py checks). A generated family (the shops) writes `seed: base+i` and claims base..base+99 |
| `cls` | the core/tags class: `building` (the default), `landmark`, `infrastructure` (a vent head, a stone door, the palisade), `life`, `feature` (the test block, the farms' plots) |
| `kind` | the core/tags kind (default: the key) |
| `tags` | core/tags vocabulary. `culture: 'zeijani'` is filled in. `types` (from `BUILDING_TYPES`: `dwelling-single`, `dwelling-multi`, `shop`, `market`, `tavern`, `inn`, `civic`, `religious`, `funerary`, `industry`, `farm`, `infrastructure`), `wealth` (`poor`, `middle`, `rich`), `style` (`carved`, `constructed`, `wooden`), `rock` (`tuff`, `basalt`), `finish` (`hewn`, `plaster`, `polished`, `raw`), `role`, `job` |
| `w`, `d`, `h` | the declared box in the local frame. verify asserts the measured box is within 1.6 m of `w` and `d` and 1.5 m of `h` |
| `budget` | the triangle limit (default 120,000) |
| `originFront` | a carved def: its origin is the foot of its front, its rock behind it at -z |
| `sunk` | a def sunk under the ground (the kiva, the council): the cut-away opens the ground over it |
| `cut` | a constructed or wooden def opens on the camera's side in the cut-away (a carved def's rock opens instead) |
| `front` | `{x, z, yaw}`: the main door in the local frame. Without it, the widest `door()` the builder declared facing +z |
| `note` | one line for the inspector and the tags record |
| `build(o)` | the builder. `o.v` is the variant; `place()` has already called `reseed(seed + v*7)` |

**Frames.** A builder draws in its local frame: origin the plot centre on the ground, +z the front, x its right. A carved
def (`originFront`) has its origin at the foot of its front, the rock behind it at -z. `W(x, y, z, ry, fn)` enters a sub-frame.

**In a builder:**

- `door(x, y, z, yaw, w)` declares a doorway (it draws nothing); `rec.front` is chosen from these.
- `sock(type, x, y, z, ry, o)` declares a socket (`sign`, `awning`, `emblem`, `flag`); `place()` calls `fillSockets()` after
  the builder, and the `zeijani` pack (core/sockets) draws it.
- `FURNISH(key, lx, ly, lz, lry, o)` and `FURNISH_HANG(key, lx, topY, lz, lry, o)` place a catalog piece (`91f-furnish.js`).
- `haloAt(x, y, z, hex, big)` records a lamp; `smokeAt(x, y, z, o)` a smoke source (`30-geo.js`).
- `rng()`, `rr(a, b)`, `pick(list)` for every random choice; never `Math.random`.
- `P(name)` a palette colour, jittered; materials by key (`27-mat.js`, `ZJ_LIB`: the rocks and finishes, ashlar, timber,
  thatch, hide, felt, ceramic, the culture's pattern sheets, `jali`).

## Placing: `place(key, x, z, ry, o)`

`place()` builds a def into the current frame and returns its record. `o.y` sets the height (else `terrainH(x, z)`),
`o.v` the variant, `o.name` and `o.tags` override the def's. Nested calls make compounds; a nested record names its parent.

Each placement becomes a record in `REG` and, **before it is drawn**, a core/tags record (`KTAGS.page`, the kit's `ZJTAGS`)
with its class, kind, culture, types, world position, size and parent. The record holds `x y z ry`, `decl`, `bbox`, `tris`,
`front` (`{source, local, world}`), `doorCount`, `children`, `furniture`, `tid` (its tags id), and `walk` (below).
A top-level built def with an interiors item writes its floors and walls to the walk map as it is placed (`zwItem`).

`ZJ_LIFE` and `zjLife(rec, o)` hold life records for anything the kit places alive (a faction, a job, a dummy schedule).
No def calls it yet.

## Sites

On the kit sheet `SITES` is laid out by `90-scene.js` from `89-rows.js`'s `FAMILIES` (rows by family, fronts toward +z;
a carved def's site is its front's foot). `?only=key,key` builds only those.

In Dhelv, `DH.SITES` (`settlements/dhelv/src/41-dhelv-layout.js`) holds `{key, district, x, z, ry, y, at}`:

| `at` | Frame |
|---|---|
| `'wall'` | the origin is the foot of a carved def's front on the hall's or a pit's wall (or the outpost's cliff), facing in |
| `'floor'` | the def stands on a floor (the hall's, a pit's, the apron) |
| `'ground'` | a carved def placed by itself (the cistern, the catacombs, the stores) |

`DH.FOOT[key]` repeats each def's footprint `[w, d, origin]` (`'front'` or `'centre'`); `settlements/dhelv/tests/test-layout.js`
reads the kit's sources and fails if one differs. A new def a world places needs its row there.

## The cavern (`40-zj-cave.js`)

A carved def's builder declares its **void plan** in its own frame. Each call turns local coordinates into world metres
through the running matrix and adds a primitive to the page's cavern, `CVC` (a `KCAVERN` from
`core/terrain/39-core-cavern.js`; read its header for each primitive's fields):

| Call | What |
|---|---|
| `cvMass(o)` | the def's block of host rock standing on the ground (the kit sheet). Skipped when `CV_OPTS.skipMass` |
| `cvRoom(o)` | a room: `poly`, `y`, `h`, `ceil` (`flat`, `vault`, `dome`), `rise`, `r` (corner radius), `rock`, `finish` |
| `cvTube`, `cvHall`, `cvStair`, `cvShaft`, `cvTrench` | lava tubes, bottle halls, stairs, shafts, pits |
| `cvMonolith(o)` | rock left standing inside a void (Kailasa's podium) |
| `cvDoor(o)` | where a void meets the air through a face |
| `cvWell(o)` | where a void meets the sky through the ground (a hatch, a light well) |
| `cvFixture(o)` | a carved bench, bed shelf or pillar: a `core/walk` block |

Ids are local to the def: `cvRoom({id: 'hall'})` is `'<site>.hall'`; `joins` names local ids (joined voids are exempt from
the minimum rock between them).

**`cvFromItem(item, o)`** carves a def's whole plan from its interiors item (below), in phases: masses and trenches, then
monoliths, then the item's carved rooms (and their fixtures as walk blocks), then every other void. `phase` on a void
overrides. The `voids` kinds: `stair tube hall shaft trench monolith mass door well room walk floor block`. `walk` is a
walk strip only (a built stair inside a void: the def draws its steps); `floor` a walk floor on rock left standing, less
its `holes`; `block` a walk block (a turned site's block is cut into 0.8 m pieces). `o.finish` is the default finish,
`o.rock` the default rock.

**`CVC` and `cvNew()`.** `cvNew()` makes the page's cavern (`cell: .5`, `chunk: 16`, `minRock: .8`, writing to `KWALK`,
the ground from `terrainH`). A world calls it at the start of each build. `CV_STATS` holds the last build's counts.
`CV_GROUP` holds the chunk meshes; `cvChunkMesh(key)` meshes one chunk (null when it holds no surface);
`cvFinish(parent)` meshes them all; `cvFinishOpenings()` tells the ground shader where the masses and wells are
(`ZJ_MASSES`, `ZJ_HOLES`); `cvGroundWalk(x0, x1, z0, z1)` writes the sheet's ground as walk rects less the masses and wells.

**`CV_OPTS`: the hooks a world sets** (all off on the kit sheet):

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `skipMass` | true: a def's block of rock is the world's rock already; `cvMass` adds nothing |
| `wellDoor(q)` | true for a hatch deep under the ground: it opens into a void as a doorway (`cvDoor`), not through the ground |
| `groundKeep(x, z, y)` | for a world that draws its own ground: the cavern's ground triangles are kept only where this is true (by a triangle's middle), so two grounds do not fight |
| `cutBoxes` | true: the rock shader takes the world's own cut boxes (`CVU.uBoxA`, `uBoxB`: up to 32) and opens the rock that is not a site's own above the site's floor + 2 m. Set it before the material compiles |

Dhelv sets all four in `90-dhelv-scene.js`.

**The rock material** (`cvRockMat`) blends the finishes and, in the basalt tubes and halls, the lining, the oxidised
breakdown, the rare colours and the crusts from per-vertex weights, triplanar over the library sets. In the cut-away a
carved void's rock opens 2 m above that void's own floor (a dollhouse at every level); a shaft opens whole.

## The walk registry (`KWALK`)

`core/walk` (`20-core-walk.js`) is the one list of floors and blocks. The walker (F), the probe's routes, Dhelv's nav graph
and the Godot export all read it. The kit writes:

- **carved defs**: the cavern writes their floors and blocks when it is built (rooms as polygons, stairs and passages as
  strips, fixtures as blocks);
- **built defs** (`37-zj-walk.js`, `zwItem(item, rec)`): each explicit room's floor (inset 0.3 m) and its walls as blocks with
  gaps at its doors; each planned body's storeys less their stairwells, its wall segments, its stairs as strips. Tag
  `built:<key>`;
- `cvFromItem`'s `walk`, `floor` and `block` entries (tags `built:stair`, `built:terrace` or the entry's own, `carved:edge`);
- the sheet's ground (`cvGroundWalk`, tag `ground`).

A planned body's walls, walk map, furniture and the probe's routes all instantiate it with one id prefix, the record's
(`zwPrefix(rec)`): the interiors planner divides a body by its prefix, so different prefixes meant different plans.

## Lights (`HALOS`)

Lamps are records, not lights. `HALOS` holds `{x, y, z, r, g, b, big}` in world metres (a 0.6 m grid keeps one per spot).
They come from `haloAt()` in builders and from the catalog pieces' lights (`91f-furnish.js`: fires big and warm, lamps small).
`91n-night.js` draws them as one point cloud and, at night, moves a pool of 10 point lights to the halos nearest the camera.
Dhelv's light (`94-dhelv-light.js`) uses its own pool of 14, underground at any hour. `halosReset()` clears them.
`SMOKES` holds the smoke sources the same way (`93-anim.js` draws them).

## Furniture and interiors

`91f-furnish.js` makes one `KFURN.create` for the page (`ZJF`), drawn by the catalog's batch. Every def whose key has an
item in **`kits/interiors/sets/zeijani.js`** (`zjItem(key)`) gets its rooms planned and furnished at its placement; each room is
registered as a `part` of its building and each piece as `furniture` with its room as parent, before it is drawn.

That set file is also the **carved defs' void plans**. An item holds:

- `rooms`: `ROOM()` data (`poly`, `y`, `h`, `doors`, `fixtures`) plus the cavern's own fields (`carved`, `round`, `ceil`,
  `rise`, `finish`, `rock`), which the interiors kit ignores. A room is the cavern's when it says `carved: true`, or when the
  item is `carved` and the room does not say `carved: false`;
- `voids`: the passages, stairs, shafts, doorways and the sheet's rock block (the interiors kit ignores them);
- `route` (optional): `[[label, x, z, expect], ...]` in the def's frame, from `routeFrom` (default `[0, 3]`), or `'planned'`
  for a planned body (the planner's own nav graph). The probe walks it.

So the rock, the walk floors, the rooms, their furniture and their tags read one record. Carved rooms are furnished in their
outline drawn in by 0.25 m (`zjFurnishItem`), because the cavern rounds corners and bulges at the floor.
`zfFixtures(item)` (`42-zj-forms.js`) draws a carved plan's fixtures (bed shelves, hearths, benches, pillars, basins, the
kiva's burner). The set's header lists the room kinds it adds (`kiva brewery lab cell ossuary cistern guardroom court alecap
taproom records`). `kits/interiors/sets/README.md` says how an item is written.

`?furniture=0` places nothing drawn (the records are kept); `?interiors=0` leaves the interiors out.

## The probe (`91-probe.js`, the sheet's host)

`window._api` on the kit sheet:

| Member | Returns |
|---|---|
| `REG`, `DEFS`, `SITES`, `ROWS`, `ZJ_LIFE` | the registries |
| `footprints()` | each top-level site's measured box against its declared `w d h`, its triangles and budget |
| `nanSweep()` | meshes with a non-finite position |
| `doors()` | each site's front (`source`: declared, a `door()`, or default) and its door count |
| `kitCoverage()` | the def keys not placed |
| `furniture(all)` | pieces placed, catalog keys, missing keys, triangles, pieces per site (`all`: the records) |
| `tags()`, `tagExport()` | the tags audit and record count; the whole export |
| `materials()` | `window._materials`, the material records |
| `life()` | `ZJ_LIFE` |
| `cavern()` | `CV_STATS` with the cavern's export and the walk export |
| `setNight(v)`, `setCut(v)`, `setView(cx, cy, cz, tx, ty, tz)` | the switches |

The sheet has no `exportParts()` / `export()`: PLAN.md 6.5 asks for one (GODOT-PLAN rule 10). Dhelv's page has them
(`settlements/dhelv/API.md`).

## Checks

```
cd kits/zeijani && python3 build.py
python3 verify.py dist/zeijani.html --assert            # add --views, --all-views, --cut, --night, --cam, --only, --eval
```

`verify.py --assert` prints the error panel (it must be clean) and runs:

- **its own:** `inside-declared-footprint`, `inside-declared-height`, `no-nan`, `buildings-typed`, `tags-vocabulary` (no unknown
  value, every record with a culture and types), `kit-coverage` (every def placed), `triangle-budget`, `front-door`,
  `furniture-keys` (no key missing from the catalog), `life-records`, `material-records`;
- **the probe's `hostChecks()`:** `cavern-meshed`, `cavern-sky` (no void meets the open air but at a declared opening),
  `cavern-rock` (every void at least 0.8 m from other buildings' voids and the air), `walk-on-mesh` (carved floors within
  0.15 m of the mesh), `walk-joins` (every carved passage meets a floor at both ends), and the routes `walk-route` (the test
  block), `walk-route-well`, `walk-route-estates`, `walk-route-houses`, `walk-route-sacred`, `walk-route-shops`,
  `walk-route-works` (every item with a `route`);
- **`hostNegatives()`:** each check against a broken copy of its input (the doors undeclared, a room 0.4 m behind a face, a
  floor 0.4 m high, a stair or ladder or passage left out, a doorway 1 m short). Each must fail; a negative that passes means
  the check is blind.

The cavern module has its own node test: `python3 tools/node_in_chromium.py core/terrain/test-cavern.js` (no node on the
owner's machine).
