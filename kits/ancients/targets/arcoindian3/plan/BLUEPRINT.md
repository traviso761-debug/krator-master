## 1. Architectural Synthesis of the Three Reference Images

1. **Central Barrel-Vaulted Stepped Spine (`../reference/ref1-vault-terraces.jpg`)**:
   - **Crown Barrel Vault (`+364..+498 m`)**: 124 m-wide warm sand-travertine barrel vault with a 20 m-deep shadowed arch hood, monumental dark-glazed curtain wall, and **7 tall narrow round-headed recessed arcades** along both east and west side walls.
   - **Six Cascading Semicircular-Arched Terraces (`T1..T6`, `+14..+364 m`)**: Each 58.3 m step opens through a 64–84 m semicircular arch housing a **nested trabeated (post-and-lintel) stone pavilion** with its own planted roof deck, flanked by **twin grand cascading exterior stairs** (`stair.west`, `stair.east`).
2. **Clustered Vertically-Fluted Chalice Towers & Parabolic Arch Skybridges (`../reference/ref2-fluted-towers.jpg`)**:
   - **Eight Fluted Bastion Drums (`W1..W4`, `E1..E4`, up to `+528 m`)**: Wrapped in 30–36 vertical organic golden-sandstone/timber ribs that flare into chalice rims around **ovoid curved-glass & hanging-garden scoops** and **circular summit woodland crowns**.
   - **Seven Sweeping Flared-Ribbed Arch Skybridges (`B1w..B4c`)**: Parabolic arch viaducts tying the fluted drums into the spine terraces, culminating in **The High Forest Arch (`B4c`, `+502 m`)** spanning 254 m between the two high crown drums `W4` and `E4`.
3. **Sheltered Terracotta Moon-Gate Courts & Emerald Mosaic Pools (`../reference/ref3-terracotta-oculus-pools.jpg`)**:
   - **Twelve Sanctuary Courts (`CW0..CW5`, `CE0..CE5`)**: Warm rose-terracotta concrete facades with semicircular ground arches and **two stacked rows of giant circular moon-gate oculi (`Ø 8.4 m`)**, **cantilevered slabs with circular soffit skylights**, warm glowing ring sconces in shadowed alcoves, trailing sill vines, and **24 sunken emerald-jade ceramic mosaic plunge & reflecting pools** divided by narrow stone causeways.

## 2. Work, Live, and Recreation Interior Programming (Original State, `d = 0`)

Following the daylight rule (*a dwelling requires an exterior window; windowless cliff-heart depth is reserved for works, hydroponics, and vaults*):

### LIVE — 170.64 ha gross / 159.12 ha daylight-lit (24,048 residents at 45 m2/person)
- **Moon-Gate Court Maisonettes (`CW0..CW5`, `CE0..CE5`, 12 wings × 8 storeys)**: Courtyard duplexes and flats lit through the giant circular terracotta oculi and ground arcades, stepping out onto shared cantilevered skylight balconies.
- **Stepped Spine Arcade Residences (`T1..T6` West & East wings, 12 wings × 13 storeys)**: Perimeter apartments lining the stepped spine's side arcades and overlooking both the gorge and the emerald pool courts.
- **Fluted Tower Panorama Drums (`W1..W4`, `E1..E4` upper 72% of each shaft)**: Curved-glass apartments behind the vertical organic ribs, opening onto scooped mid-air chalice gardens.

### WORK — 73.59 ha gross (4,963 workplaces)
- **Gorge River Quay & Barge Warehouses (`+2..+14 m`)**: West crane hall, east boatyard/slips, customs weigh-house, and freight hoist lobbies along the riverfront arcades.
- **Six Deep Rock-Keyed Cliff Galleries (`D1..D6`, `z = -214..-132 m`)**:
  - `D1` (`+14 m`): Lower Penstock Turbine and River Pump Hall
  - `D2` (`+72 m`): Rock-Cut Cisterns and Grain Vaults
  - `D3` (`+131 m`): Foundry, Ceramic Kilns (for the emerald tiles & terracotta), and Stoneworks
  - `D4` (`+189 m`): Lamp-Lit Mycelial and Aquaponic Tiers I
  - `D5` (`+247 m`): Lamp-Lit Hydroponic Orchard Tiers II
  - `D6` (`+306 m`, Civic/Archive): Deep Seed Bank and Memory Archive
- **Lower Fluted Tower Guild & Research Drums (`W1..W4`, `E1..E4` lower 28%)**: Hydraulics & fishery (`W1`), river customs & ropewalk (`E1`), geological cliff-bore institute (`W2`), agronomy guildhall (`E2`), aerology & glider hangar (`W3`), glassblowers & mosaic atelier (`E3`), astronomy & chronicle (`W4`), water-keepers & physicians college (`E4`).
- **High Acclimatization Conservatory & Solar Observatory (`+364..+472 m`, rear of Crown Barrel Vault)**: 14 tiers of botanical research labs and atmospheric instruments behind the Council Nave.

### RECREATION & CIVIC — 20.60 ha gross (8,225 recreation capacity + 1,200 Council Assembly)
- **Twenty-Four Emerald Mosaic Bath & Reflecting Basins (`CW0..CW5`, `CE0..CE5`)**: Fed by the twin high cliffside springs (`+386 m`), stepping court-to-court for thermal/cool bathing, music loggias, and shaded rest.
- **Six Nested Post-and-Lintel Arch Pavilions (`T1..T6`)**: Harbourmarket loggia, lower feast commons & amphitheatre, artisans bazaar, botanical music court, scholars cloister, and high vault forecourt.
- **Eight Circular Tower-Crown Groves + Six Hanging Orchard Decks + Seven Skybridge Walks**: `8.61 ha` of open planted parkland, orchards, and canyon overlooks.
- **The Great Barrel-Vault Assembly Nave (`+364..+436 m`)**: Six stepped civic balconies behind the 98 m arched glass curtain wall.

## 3. How the Original State Guides the Modern Condition (`d = 1`)

`ARCO3.ruin(P)` and `ARCO3.modernGuide(P, RU)` model five physical failures during and after the Cataclysm that directly shape any future settlement or gameplay layer built into Arcoindian III:

1. **The Sanctuary Neck (`+188 m`)**:
   - The lower skybridges (`B1w`, `B1e`), the third flights of both grand exterior stairs (`stair.west.2`, `stair.east.2`), and the lower spine lift core (`core.spine.low`) were severed at `+188 m`.
   - **Effect**: Nothing walking up from the gorge river can climb past Terrace 2 (`+130 m`) without a lowered winch or bridge.
2. **Surviving East Cliff Spring vs Breached West Cascade**:
   - A massive **West Brow Rockfall** sheared `Tower W3` at `+292 m`, dropped `B2w` and `B3w`, choked the west court arches at `T3..T5`, and breached the West Spring above `CW4` (`+246 m`), throwing a **238 m wild waterfall** straight down into the gorge and leaving `CW0..CW4` dry.
   - Meanwhile, the **East Cliff Spring (`+386 m`) survived intact**, keeping all six East Oculus Courts (`CE0..CE5`), the upper spine orchards (`T3..T6`), and the deep aquaponic/hydroponic galleries (`D4`, `D5`) watered!
3. **Three Resulting Modern Zones**:
   - **Zone 1 — The Kept Upper Sanctuary (`+188..+528 m`, 33 units, ~2,630 sustainable modern population)**: Encompasses the Crown Barrel Vault, Spine Terraces `T3..T6`, Deep Galleries `D4..D6`, East Oculus Courts `CE2..CE5`, East Fluted Towers `E2..E4`, and West Crown Drum `W4` (across the intact `High Forest Arch B4c`). Reached from outside only by the **Guarded Plateau Rim Stair (`core.rim.east`, `+560 -> +364 m`)**, with `15.94 ha` of watered terrace gardens, bath courts, and hydroponic tiers sustainably feeding ~`2,630` citizens inside housing built for `9,318`.
   - **Zone 2 — The River Port & Salvage Shore (`0..+130 m`, 28 units)**: The Gorge Quay, mooring piers, Barge Warehouses, Sentinel Bastions `W1` & `E1`, Terraces `T1..T2`, and Lower Courts `CW0..CW1` / `CE0..CE1`. Open to river vessels; natural site for a ferry market, boat-builders, and salvage guild working the fallen fluted drums on the west shore bench.
   - **Zone 3 — The Isolated West Ruins (`+130..+364 m`, 26 units)**: Dry terracotta moon-gate courts (`CW2..CW4`), the Geological Institute (`W2`), and the sheared Aerology Hangar (`W3` stump) — cut off from both river and sanctuary, preserving untouched Ancient rooms for rope-and-grapple exploration.
