# Arckibuz Blueprint & Interior Socket Plan

Derived directly from Paolo Soleri's **Plate 15: Arckibuz** (*Arcology: The City in the Image of Man*, 1969) and generated live from `8aw0-arckibuz-plan.js` and `8aw1-arckibuz-interiors.js` by `arckibuz_blueprint.py`.

## Design Principles

1. **Thermal Mass & Shadow in the Desert**: Rather than air-conditioning an exposed glass tower, Soleri sinks the industrial/agricultural base `~28 m` into cool desert bedrock (`CROP PROCESSING`, `STORAGE`, `WAREHOUSING`, `PUBLIC`, `PRODUCTION`) and cantilevers the `184 m` wide `HOUSING` crown overhead so the entire **Community Center**, **City Center**, and **Sunken Court Promenade** sit in permanent deep shade, cooled by gravity-fed water from the rooftop **Water Reservoir** (`+120 m`).
2. **Self-Contained Agro-Industrial Loop**: Five faceted polygonal **Vegetable Gardens** (`~4.8 ha` net beds inside `~7.84 ha` total domain) surround the north, west, and south rims, irrigated by gravity channels from the shoulder reservoir, while harvested crops move straight down the west/south causeways into subterranean **Crop Processing** and **Cold Storage**.
3. **Refurbished Settlement Centerpiece (`d = 3`)**: Designed as the centerpiece of a post-Cataclysm arcology town (`~1,660` inhabitants):
   - **Active Core** (`arckibuz/3/{pit,community,housing-n,housing-s,housing-w}`): Whole partitions, working `core/door` leaves, warm window lights, restored canvas shade canopies, active rooftop reservoir & irrigated gardens, pre-furnished in `'post-apoc'` (`occupied`) dress and re-dressable via `_api.arckibuzSocket(culture)`.
   - **Sealed Expansion Socket** (`arckibuz/3/dormant`): The **East Housing Quadrant (`housing-e`)** and **Deep Production Bay (`pit-east`)** (`~840` additional capacity) are isolated behind four structural bulkheads (`ARCKIBUZ.DORMANT_CUT`), ready to be unsealed as the settlement grows.
