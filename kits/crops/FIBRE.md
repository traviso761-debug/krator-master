# Fibre crops (survey and proposals, 2026-10-18)

Read from the biome kits' `harvest` tags (`50-biome-*-species.js`). **Finding: every fibre in the biome kits is wild-gathered.
None is a cultivated spinning fibre.** There is no cotton, flax, hemp, nettle, ramie, sisal or silk in any kit (only lore:
the Hykkousoi's "sea-linen"). What exists is cordage, mats, baskets and thatch.

Buckets (`PLAN.md`, group `material`): `spinning` (cloth thread), `cordage` (rope, bast), `weaving` (mats, baskets, withies),
`thatch`.

## 2. Proposals (seven crops cover all sixteen biomes)

| Crop | Analog | Bucket | Warmth / atm / aridity | Water |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunboll cotton** | cotton | spinning | subtropic to tropic, standard to dense, semiarid to subhumid | rain in subhumid, else irrigated |
| **Sea-linen flax** | flax (the Hykkousoi's sea-linen); seed also `oil` | spinning | cool to subtropic (winter-sown), standard, subhumid to humid | rain; irrigated if dry |
| **Cairn nettle** | Himalayan nettle | spinning | polar to cool, thin to standard, subhumid | rain |
| **Silk-nettle ramie** | ramie | spinning | tropic, standard to dense, humid to wet | rain |
| **Rope banana** | abaca | cordage | tropic, standard to dense, humid to wet | rain |
| **Socotran sisal** | agave/sisal | cordage | hot, any atm, arid to semiarid | rain; light irrigation in the driest |
| **Vale mulberry** | mulberry for silk (leaf crop; silkworm) | spinning | cool to subtropic, standard, subhumid to humid | rain; irrigated in dry valleys |
