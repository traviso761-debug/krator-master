# kits/dzagh-vardzi: Petty Gorge-Kingdoms of Dzagh-Vardzi (Vardzieti / Crookwash, K1-K7)

The building kit of the **Petty Gorge-Kingdoms of `Dzagh-Vardzi`** ("Gorge-Wards") of **`Vardzieti` (`Crookwash`, `+950..+2,274 m`)**, fractured across **7 isolated rain-fed lateral mountain valleys** (`Khāi-Gēti`, `Rēd-Spāiri`, `Tīn-Flītsi`, `Āirn-Dzāghi`, `Rōzi-Vārdzi`, `Mīst-Khōpri`, `Dzēdi-Khrōsi`) plus the neutral confluence trade-hamlet **`Flūmi-Brīdzi`** (`culture: 'dzagh-vardzi'`), where **"every king (*Dzagh-Mep'e*) is lord in his own valley"** ([LORE.md](../../LORE.md) §6.26).

Sheltered from lowland empires behind sheer volcanic-tuff and columnar-basalt gorges, the *Dzagh-Vardzi* remain **militarily archaic** — lacking artillery, firearms, pike drill, or standing armies: tiny cousin retinues in **300-year-old heirloom tin-bronze & magnetite scale hauberks, nephrite-bossed bucklers, and silver-chased broadswords** settle valley boundary feuds by **1-v-1 or 3-v-3 champions' duels on covered chasm sky-bridges**, weighed out in sawn emerald nephrite-slab weregild (`jade = 200`) and *qvevri* apricot-brandy feasts. The **only external enemy the *Dzagh-Vardzi* ever fight** are **`Timpar-Kuran` raiders** descending from the **Veiled Mountains** ([LORE.md](../../LORE.md) §6.25): when `Timpar-Kuran` war-bands strike outlying *aul* settlements, **free-roaming, chivalric Hero-Troubadours (*Ashugh-Gmiri*)** ride from gorge to gorge singing alarm-lays, shaming feuding *Dzagh-Mep'e* lords into temporary truces, and organizing relief retinues.

## Architectural Language (`DV2 + DV4 + DV3`, 52 Buildings, 31 Bespoke Furniture Pieces)

1. **Polychrome *Ablaq* Masonry & Nephrite Inlays (`DV2` — Armenian/Georgian *Akhtamar* & *Vardzia*):**
   - Warm **apricot-rose volcanic tuff (`tuff`, `tuffPolished`)** and **dark charcoal columnar-basalt (`basalt`, `colBasalt`)** horizontal *ablaq* courses.
   - Sawn **emerald-green nephrite (`jade`) cross-medallions**, chip-carved vine/rosette relief friezes (`carve`), and **golden-skarn arched tympanums (`skarn`)**.
2. **Terraced Cliff-*Aul*, `5:1` Dark-Slate *Koshki* Needle-Towers & 12-Pleat *Gumbat* Spires (`style: 'aul'`, `DV4 + DV2`, 26 Buildings):**
   - Stacked Dagestani stone roof-plaza houses (`9` poor/middle/rich dwellings) whose flat slate-and-cypress lower roofs form sunny apricot-drying terraces for stepped-back upper tiers.
   - Clustered razor-slender **`5:1` battered defense needle-towers** (Ingush *vovnashki* / Svan *koshki*) crowned with **corbelled crimson-and-apricot tuff cornices (`tuffCrimson` / `tuffPolished`), stepped dark mountain-slate (`slate`) & charcoal-basalt (`colBasalt`) pyramidal roofs (`dvSlatePyramid`), and patinated `verdigris` copper + `skarn` + `jade` finials** (zero snow-white quartz spires — white quartz belongs exclusively to `Hush-Kin`), plus projecting *mashikul* machicolation boxes and **aerial ropeway pulley-baskets**.
   - Civic, sacred & royal monuments (`17` buildings) crowned with **12-pleat star-fluted conical *Akhtamar* umbrella spires (*gumbat*)** in patinated verdigris copper (`Cu = 130`, `verdigris`) & crimson/apricot tuff — including **`dv-citadel-mepe`**, **`dv-hall-troubadour`**, **`dv-vault-mepe-qvevri`**, **`dv-sanctuary-gumbat`**, **`dv-hall-weregild`**, **`dv-bridge-duel`** (Champions' Duel Sky-Bridge), **`dv-tavern-qvevri`**, and **`dv-gate-flume`**.
3. **Three Wonders Travellers Cross the Mountains For (`src/56b-dv-wonders.js`, [LORE.md](../../LORE.md) §6.26):**
   - **Hall of Seven Strings & Hero-Troubadour Lodge (`dv-hall-troubadour`):** Polychrome *ablaq* lodge under a 12-pleat `verdigris` *gumbat* spire and dark-slate corner *koshki* turret, with a raised bardic performance dais, silver-strung *panduri* lute racks (`dv_panduri_lute_rack`), heirloom scale hauberks, seven-valley heralds' standards, and an emerald-nephrite-sealed chivalric errand & feud-arbitration tablet board.
   - **Century-Buried *Mep'e-Qvevri* Elixir Cellar & Tasting Loggia (`dv-vault-mepe-qvevri`):** Vaulted *ablaq* & golden-`skarn` subterranean elixir cellar and arcaded loggia embedding beeswax- and placer-gold-sealed *Mep'e-Qvevri* amphora necks (`dv_mepe_qvevri_seal`) steeping `30–100+ year` apricot brandy, highland `saffron`, `sky-poppy` resin, skarn spring-water, and placer-gold leaf for `< 0.75 atm` Outer Wall passes, alongside copper/terracotta infusion stills and nephrite vintage plaques.
   - **Chasm-Wind Pipe-Organ Raid-Beacon & Gondola Prow (`dv-organ-beacon`):** Zero-podium battered `colBasalt` cliff-prow mounting a 13-flue wind-driven acoustic Chasm-Organ of hollow `chamberbamboo` and `verdigris` copper resonance flues (`145–290 Hz` `Timpar-Kuran` raid-warning chords across `15 km` of gorge, triggered from `dv_chasm_organ_console`) alongside a hand-cranked bronze-and-cypress aerial cable-basket gondola.
4. **Specialized `core/hamlet` Flume-Forge & Chasm-Mill Mode (`style: 'flume'`, `DV3`, 26 Buildings, 40/44 Voth Trades):**
   - **`dv-flume-keep`** (Chasm-Straddling Flume-Forge Keep shooting a full `30 m` red-cypress log straight through its vaulted belly), **`dv-flume-forge`** (Stacked Triple Overshot Cliffside Waterwheel Stamp-Mill & Trip-Hammer Forge), **`dv-organ-beacon`**, **`dv-flume-trestle`**, **`dv-sawmill-jade`**, **`dv-sluice-fleece`**, plus foundry, heirloom armourer, *Mep'e-Qvevri*-enriched distillery (`dv-winery-qvevri`), quarry, `kits/resources`-linked mine, market, granary, terrace orchard, and mountain byre.
5. **Zero-Podium Steep-Gorge Engineering:**
   - Every building extends a battered `colBasalt`/`cobble` cliff root-skirt (`4.8-7.0 m` below `y = 0`) with zero boxy flat podiums, proven on both a **`25 deg` Terraced Gorge Slope Row** and the **`Crookwash` Chasm, Cascade & Log-Flume Showcase Row**, as well as the **`Flūmi-Brīdzi` (`--village`)** terraced gorge confluence hamlet (`15` kit buildings).
