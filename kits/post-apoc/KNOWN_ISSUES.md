# Post-Apoc set: known issues

Open items are lines starting `- [ ]`; `build.py` prints them on every build.

- [ ] Cultural packs are stand-ins built from the brief (Iziz orange awnings, Voth banners, Republic triskelion); replace the glyphs with each culture's real sigils when settled.
- [ ] Animation compromises: cloth flutters in the vertex shader only, so its shadow stays still; cloth with no rule (the dock's washing line, sacks, hides drawn straight in `cloth` by a builder) does not move; door light spills sit at ground level (`rec.front` has no door height), so a door up a stair spills onto the ground below it; smoke puffs are unsorted billboards (fine at their low opacity).
- [ ] Corrugated roofs still sparkle at close range under the night point lights (the rib texture doubles as a strong bump map, `bump` 1.2-1.6 in `src/22-mat.js`); the long-range shimmer is fixed, this near-range glint is not.

- [ ] Furniture is the catalog's (FURNISH, `src/91f-furnish.js`): its pieces come in fixed sizes, so some differ from what the kit drew: counters are 3.1 m (the kit's ran 4.6-5.6 m across a box front), lamp posts 3.2 or 3.8 m (the kit's 2.4-5 m; gantry lamps are taller), crates 0.6 or 0.8 m, tyre stacks of 2, 3 or 5, tyre-ring planters 0.9 m (the kit's 0.72), water butts stand on the ground on a 1.1 or 1.4 m stand (the kit's stilts ran to 2.6 m; two downpipes were shortened to meet them), porch goods rails 2.6 m. Catalog glow does not flicker in the shader (its halos and pool lights still do).
- [ ] Still drawn as geometry, though arguably furniture, for want of a catalog piece: single loose tyres (pit obstacles, the cages' yard tyres; the dw-tire and bottle-cottage tyre steps are structure), the small 1.3 m kennel cage in the cages yard, bracket lamps under 2 m (the tug's mast, the dock crane deck, the cages' lookout), the mess hall's dinner bell, the fuel pumps (gen-fuel, dock), the granary's weighbridge, the farm shed's hung tools, bucket and wheelbarrow, buckets (watchtower, cages), the bottle cottage's window box, the two plate-hung spikes in the big man's forecourt (the two hubcap spikes are `pa_junk_totem`).
- [ ] Rooms the interior set plans are empty by default (the shop boxes, the smithy shed, the longhouse and mess halls, the mess kitchen, the throne bay, the granary's loading bay): `?interiors=1` furnishes them. The interiors run for top-level placements only, not for the buildings a compound's slots place.

## By design

- Rust weathering is heavy by request (`WEATHER` in `src/22-mat.js`); metal is now matt (roughness ~.9, metalness ~.08) so dusk and moon light do not glaze it. Doors use unweathered `plain` paint so they stay readable against rusted walls.

- [x] Collision and nav are coarse by design: colliders are oriented boxes (cylinders, tyres and domes boxed, beams and poles skipped, so deck posts, rails and lamp posts do not block); the nav grid is 0.5 m, 2.5-D (up to 4 levels a cell), with a 0.6 m step, a body band of 0.45 to 1.55 m (lower parts are steps, thin lintels above are ignored) and no headroom test on stairs. Doors are checked for an approach from open ground, not walked through: interiors are not guaranteed connected. - 2026-10-20: by design, nothing open.
- [ ] Rotated compound slots: the footprint check swaps w and d for a quarter-turned site, but measures in the compound's frame only when the compound itself stands at ry 0 (as in the showcase).

## Doors, glass and the dark voids (core/door, 2026-11-03)

The owner: "remove all dark voids and make all windows, doors and portals actual doors or windows that have transparency", doors that open and shut with a key or by an NPC, "real glass", every interior region reachable. The records are core/door's; the kit's drawing of them is `src/35-pa-open.js` (`poLeaf`, `poCard`; `wallOpen` records each opening it leaves).
- [x] Before: every `door()` call drew a painted leaf on the wall's face (the walls are solid: no opening behind a door); the longhouse's closed front had two black plates standing for its outer doorways, with a leaf drawn ajar before each; the shaman's hut doorway was a black plate behind its hide; the prisoner cells' barred slits and the cell door's wicket were black slots; the 317 panes were the kit's 0.78-opaque glass on solid faces (about half moved to `winlit`, an opaque teal pane by day), the lit ones an opaque glow plate.
- [x] After: 137 doors in KDOOR (every `door()` of the showcase, the longhouses' outer leaves and the cell door; ids `<def key>.<placement>.d<n>`, `longhouse.0.d3`). 12 are leaves that open and shut (E within reach, the "E: open the door" tip, one InstancedMesh): every `door()` drawn in an opening its wall left (the bulkhead's, the chief's, the farm shed's, the cages', the dock's), and the longhouse's three front bays, now real doorways cut through its wall (`patchWall` takes `holes`; its patches still draw their random numbers), the outer two leaves standing ajar as drawn and the middle one open a crack. 125 are LOCKED (a solid face, nothing behind it): a record each, the leaf as drawn; their walk blocks are switched off, since the shells are not in core/walk and the walker kept the way it had. Panes: 317, KGLASS 'frosted' (salvaged, scratched glass: light through, little view; the bottle-green tint per pane), the `winlit` ones glass by day too and opaque only while lit; 1 stands in a real opening, the rest before a room card (KGLASS 'room'), or before a glow card where the builder drew a lit window (`o.lit`: the lamp-lit room behind the glass).
