# `kits/painted-men` — Known Issues & Ride-Along Notes

- **Catalog Culture File Re-Keying (`kits/catalog/krator-master-furniture-painted.js`):** The shared catalog file `kits/catalog/krator-master-furniture-painted.js` still holds the pre-revamp Pacific-Northwest / Tlingit painted cedar chests and feast dishes, which belong to `kits/timpar-kuran` (Veiled Mountains) and will be re-keyed on the next all-builds catalog pass (`PLAN.md` §1.1 & §4). Accordingly, `kits/painted-men/build.py` omits `krator-master-furniture-painted.js` from `FURN_CULTURES` and registers all `14` authentic Painted Highlands plateau furniture pieces (`painted_pitch_tus_rack`, `painted_burl_coolamon_set`, `painted_rarrk_bark_screen`, etc.) directly onto `KratorFurniture` under `culture: 'painted'` in `src/39-pm-furn.js`.
- **No Trade Gaps (`0` gaps):** All valid Voth census trades are carried by the `32` `pm_*` building definitions; `shipwrights and boatbuilders`, `coast guard`, `power-house stokers and engineers`, `beetle herders`, `elephant bug handlers and station hands`, plus `potters`, `glassmakers`, `bankers`, `scribes`, and `printers` are explicitly marked `na` with ecological and cultural rationale in `src/34-pm-trades.js`.
