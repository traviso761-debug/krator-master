# Biome fauna plan (proposal, 2026-10-12)

The brief: every biome gets at least **two unique grazers, one predator, one unique flyer and one water creature**; the Throne's
sub-biomes consolidate and share. `✓` = already in the kit (kept, counted as that biome's); **bold** = new, procedural in the kit's
style (a Meshy pass can replace any of them later). "Shares" = drawn once, tagged for several biomes.

**Throne consolidation.** Its ten sub-biomes (cloudforest, savanna, ashdesert, kipuka, caldera, vents, tube, glacier, isle, frontier) map
onto kits that already have creatures: the windward jungle uses the hyperjungle's, the savanna the swbay savanna's, ashdesert and kipuka the
crater drylands', the glacier Xanadu's yak and cloud leopard, the isle and coast the Ring Sea's. Only five creatures are the Throne's own
(kipuka tortoise-grazer, ash goat, vent hunter, pumice petrel, steam newt/kite shared with the geyser fields).

**Not planned yet:** Korona, the scablands and tuff country (the biomes are not placed on the map).

**Count.** About 55 new animals across 17 biome rows (3 to 6 variants each). Land animals need a shared procedural quadruped builder
(a body, neck and head, four jointed legs, a tail, coat or scale bands, horns and antlers as options) so they are variants of a few
bodies, not 40 bespoke builds; birds and flyers reuse `faHiBird`; water animals need a swimmer builder (fish, ray, newt, crab).

## Progress

- **Wave 1 (2026-10-13), built:** north-west lowlands, south-west lowlands (Dalab), Xanadu, the Rift, eastern badlands, the Throne and the
  geyser fields (29 animals in `krator-fauna-wild.js`, tagged by their biomes; the pumice petrel also `ringsea`). The Ring Sea has its sea lion
  (`krator-fauna-ringsea.js`). Hyperjungle already had its set.
- **Wave 2 (2026-10-13), built:** the northern, southern and eastern highlands, 15 animals in `krator-fauna-wild.js` (ridge ibex, upland elk, grey highland wolf, crag eagle, giant river otter; argali, chamois, mountain lion, mountain condor, giant hellbender; cushion llama, plateau viscacha, thin-air puma, thin-air kite, altiplano lake frog).
- **Wave 3 (2026-10-13), built:** the north-west bay (cliff sheep, marsh deer, shore bear), the south-west bay's second grazer (dune hartebeest), the eastern abyss (abyssal saiga, brine-flat onager, abyss hyena, giant brine crab), the eastern high desert (desert oryx, giant oasis terrapin), the crater drylands (burn-hawk, hot-spring axolotl) and the Ring Sea (isle tortoise, shore iguana, tide fox, ring shark, ring whale): 19 animals. Every biome in the table now has its two grazers, a predator, a flyer and a water creature.
- **Left:** Korona, the scablands and tuff country (biomes not placed yet); a Meshy pass on any of these (credits).

- **Domestic stock (2026-10-13):** donkey, mule, goose, turkey, rabbit, alpaca, reindeer, bactrian camel, homing pigeon, giant honeybee (tagged `domestic`, with traits for riding, draught and milk), rounding out the farm group's cattle, horse, sheep, pig, hen, goat, duck, dog, cat, yak, water buffalo and the lizard-world stock.
- **Sea and lake fauna (2026-10-13):** ring dolphin, giant sea turtle, manta ray, giant ring squid, glass jellyfish, reef octopus, ring marlin, banner reef fish, ring cormorant (Ring Sea and the bays); giant pike, lake sturgeon, giant catfish, lake plesiosaur, lake diver (lakes and rivers).
- **Meshy pass (pending credits):** the weakest procedural animals, to replace with baked models: goose, turkey, camel, and the biped/ small-bird builds; the account is at 6 credits (a preview is 20, a refine 10).
- **Flippers and hexapods (2026-10-13):** a `flipper` option on `faWdQuad` (flat paddles) now serves the giant sea turtle and the lake plesiosaur; eight large hexapods from `faWdHex`, one per country: dune runner (desert), frost trunker (cold plateau), marsh strider (wetland), cavern leaper (caves and lava tubes), cinder crawler (vents), plains hopper (grassland), tide crawler (coast), badland digger (burrower).
- **Reference-plate hexapods (2026-10-13):** `faWdHex` grew a neck, scythe-blades, club-ended back spines, a gaping maw, trunk mandibles, whisker tendrils, a swept crest, a spade snout, pillar legs with bands and hooked toes. Five animals after the owner's plates: scytheback (badland herds), hookstalker (stilt hunter), reedneck (long-necked jungle browser), gapemaw (Rift ambusher), clubback (club-spined desert browser).
- **Ambiguous anatomy (2026-10-13):** the owner's call: the plate hexapods should not read as insects. `faWdHex` has an `amb` option (skin, scale and horn families instead of chitin; small vertebrate eyes; thick tapering limbs with padded, blunt-toed feet; no antennae or spines) now used by the scytheback, hookstalker, reedneck, gapemaw and clubback. The earlier eight hexapods (dune runner ... badland digger) stay insectoid.
- **Hookstalker as a primate (2026-10-13):** `faWdHex` has a `primate` option (round brow-ridged head, short flat muzzle, forward-set eyes, small round ears, four-fingered hands, furred coat, long prehensile tail); the hookstalker uses it.
- **Earlier hexapods made ambiguous (2026-10-13):** all eight now use the `amb` anatomy and are renamed: dune runner, frost trunker (was ice weevil), marsh strider, cavern leaper (was cavern cricket), cinder crawler (was lava roach), plains hopper, tide crawler (was tide beetle), badland digger (spade hands, a pointed snout). No elytra, antennae or mandibles; hide, scale, coat and fur families; vertebrate eyes; limbs with padded feet.
- **The `centaur` gait and eight more six-limbed beasts (2026-10-13):** the owner's call: the front limbs are arms, the middle limbs read as arms at a walk and legs at a run. `animate(g, t, 'run')` (or `o.run`, or `o.speed` between the animal's walk and run speeds) blends the middle pair; the hind pair always walk. The hookstalker uses it. New: burden beast (domestic, rideable, draught, milkable; hippo-sized synapsid-like, with a saddle anchor), ember bull, horncrest (after Shringasaurus), barbed ravager (spikes, pincer arms, curled tail), capstilt (a flat cap for a head), slabhorn (a horizontal bony slab), gapebill (a blue-mouthed beak), dunewalker (a desert mount, rideable). `faWdHex` gained: pincers, spikes, a curling tail, a sail, brow horns, cheek nodes, tusks, a head slab, a cap, a beak, saddle and grip anchors. kits/scyvoi, desert-nomads and ash-nomads were rebuilt for the runtime change and pass their own verify.
- **Arboreal six-limbed critters (2026-10-13):** the owner's note: not every hexapod needs the `centaur` gait, but for arboreal critters, especially the hyperjungle's, it is the common one. Six hyperjungle climbers on it: canopy runner, moss sloth, bough panther, bark skulker, fruit gibber, veil glider (a gliding veil of skin). `faWdHex` gained `hands` (fingered hands without the primate head), a hanging prehensile tail (`tail.hang`) and a gliding veil (`glide`).
- **Quality pass on the six-limbed beasts, centaur gait for the forest and bay species, the fang glider (2026-10-13):** `faWdHex` now draws one seamless torso (shoulder to rump, lighter below, darker along the back), shoulder, hip and knee bulges so limbs grow out of the body, and a proper face (a tapering muzzle, a mouth line, nostrils, a brow socket, optional fangs); the thin-limbed beasts got heavier limbs and a narrower stance. Every six-limbed animal tagged for the hyperjungle, the south-west bay or the north-west bay is on the `centaur` gait. New: the fang glider (`faWdGlider`, a flyer): a native six-limbed reptilian glider the size of a dire wolf, great membranes on the upper limbs, smaller ones on the middle limbs that beat with them, small steering membranes on the hind legs, a long rudder tail, long upper canines; an ambush hunter of the hyperjungle canopy.
- **Arboreal climbing, the veil glider (2026-10-13):** every six-limbed animal tagged for the north-west bay, the hyperjungle or the Throne (the hypertree biomes) is on the `centaur` gait and has `climbs` in its locomotion. The centaur gait has a `climb` mode: the animal pitches nose-up (`g.rotation.x = -o.climb`, default 1.3 rad, order YXZ) and its limbs reach overhead in alternating tripods; it levels out in any other mode, and the host presses it to the trunk. The veil glider is now just reptilian (scales, a lizard head, a ribbed throat flap, no fangs) and a flyer that walks (`gait.walk: 'centaur'`): its membranes are a bat's, stretched between the limbs themselves (front to middle, middle to hind, hind to the tail); in flight the runtime swings the six limbs out level (the builder gives the angles as `spread<i>` anchors) and the web opens. `faWdHex` options: `limbWeb`, `patagia`, `dewlap`.
- **Spindliness, membranes, lowland gliders (2026-10-13):** `faWdHex` has a `stock` option (the body and limbs thicker by k, the legs shorter by k) used on the spindly beasts, and a better limb profile (a heavy thigh, a knee, a tapering shank). The fang glider's wings are now a bat's: panels of membrane between the arm and the finger struts (a propatagium, scalloped dactylopatagia between the fingers, a plagiopatagium to the hip), the middle wings the same on two fingers, and the hind-leg membranes run to the tail like a uropatagium. Four lowland gliders on the `limbWeb` (a web between all six limbs): meadow skimmer (a furred grazer, nwlowlands), hedge glider (a small night forager, nwlowlands), cypress glider (a reptilian fruit-eater, swlowlands), sail skimmer (a fish-eater skimming the water, swlowlands).
- **Gliders climb (2026-10-13):** the lowland gliders (meadow skimmer, hedge glider, cypress glider, sail skimmer) are climbers and walk on the `centaur` gait (`gait.walk: 'centaur'`, `climbs` in their locomotion, the `climb` mode works). New: the rim glider for the eastern abyss, a pale, long-tailed reptilian scavenger-glider that climbs the rim cliffs and the salt pillars and rides the heat off the salt pans on a web between its limbs, with a ribbed throat pouch that holds water.
- **Rift gliders (2026-10-13):** four six-limbed climbers on the centaur gait with a `limbWeb`, all bioluminescent (new `faWdHex` options `glowTips`, `glowSpots`, `lure`): lamp glider (raccoon-sized, masked, a night omnivore of the slime films, 1.2 m span), spore drifter (a glassy filter-feeder of the spore-clouds that hangs rather than falls, 3.4 m span), gloam hanger (an ambush predator hanging head-down from the lace-caps with a glowing lure and long fangs, 3.4 m span), Rift sailwing (quetzalcoatlus-scale scavenger-soarer of the Rift walls, 10.8 m span, budget 12000 triangles).
- **Plumelands (2026-10-13):** `plumelands` is a new biome tag: the ash plain downwind of the Throne's plume (the Ash Nomads' country, where the ash runner has had no biome of its own); the owner confirmed the reading. First animal: the ash racer, a raccoon-sized six-limbed runner (a small wild cousin of the ash runner: ash-grey, rust-banded, long balancing tail, burrows in the drift), tagged `plumelands` and `throne`. The ash runner is tagged `plumelands` too (its `swbay` and `crater-drylands` tags stay).
