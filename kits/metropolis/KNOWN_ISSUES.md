# kits/metropolis: known issues

- [ ] **The shells and impostors take no shadow and cast none** (they draw their own light): a tower's shadow on the street stops when it turns into a shell, past 300 m, mostly outside the shadow box (`core/shadow`, 90 to 600 m). One box, no cascades: far shadows wait on cascades (`core/shadow/README.md`).
- [ ] **Bath rooms are too narrow for a whole bath:** they take the catalog's opt-in Ancient bath set (2026-11-03; `TODO.md`), but the Spindle flats' baths are strips 0.55 to 1.05 m wide: all 216 get pieces, only 60 get a bath or shower, a toilet and a basin. A whole one wants about 1.5 m: widening them is a change to the flats' plan (`41-mp-plans.js`), the owner's call.
- [ ] **Site:** the sky stands at `core/atmos`'s default site. The valley's own comes with the valley.
- [ ] **Soffits under 200 m** break up in the shell, inside the hero's range: a problem only if the switch is held closer than 300 m.
- [ ] **The soffit's edge** where the Spindle's neck meets the lamp is stepped in the shell under about 200 m: the switch from the side map to the soffit map is a hard test.
- [ ] **Software GL loses the context, on every page here** (found 2026-11-03): the harness asks SwiftShader for `--use-gl=swiftshader`, which this machine's Chromium 151 no longer takes; its GPU process exits and the first WebGL context is lost, then restored about a second later, with shader errors and empty bake read-backs between. `--use-angle=swiftshader` fixes it (no loss on host-example 3 of 3, nor on this page): the fix is commit b00abe65a on branch `worktree-agent-a167240fec837e244`, cut from `claude/biome-rebuild` (`tools/harness.py`), not yet on this branch. Close this once the branch takes that harness.
- [ ] **One Arc run crashed the page on load** (`Page.goto: Page crashed`, 2026-11-02 04:15), once; 14 runs since (2 full verifies, 12 loads) did not. Not this page's memory: a renderer past V8's heap limit exits 0xe0000008 and fires no crash event (the load would hang for its timeout), while the crash event comes from a kill from outside or a real renderer crash. The machine was heavily oversubscribed (40.8 of 53.9 GB committed on 15.4 GB of RAM). The harness on b00abe65a prints the renderer's exit code on a death, so the next one names itself.
- [ ] **The Ledger's reception takes an opt-in recipe** (`kits/ancients-interiors` `reception`, with a counter) until the next interiors update; its frame is squared here (`mpFrameDoors`) until the kit's frame takes the door wall's normal (`TODO.md`).
- [ ] **Up to 24 real towers** stand within 300 m (the cap the tiers allow, 2026-11-02, up from 10): in a dense block every near tower is real, so its cost is the city's density. With more than 24, the rest are shells drawn close, which smear.
- [ ] **`AI.barPlate` draws its cross walls diagonally** (`kits/ancients-interiors/src/35-ai-plates.js`, the `.x` wall: `[inner[0], outer[m]]`, from the room's inner start to its outer end; it should be `outer[0]`). Every bar storey of the Ledger had a wall across each room corner to corner, one of them across the entrance. `41-mp-plans.js` straightens them here; the kit's fix reaches `kits/ancients` (some 30 pages) and Noah's Regret, so it waits for the next all-builds rebuild.
- [ ] **Rubble can still reach a neighbour at `?pitch=140`:** a ruined Spindle is turned so its fall line heads where the fewest neighbouring blocks hold towers, but with every block full a 120 m mound crosses into one. The Ledger's spills run along its long sides.
- [ ] **The far rubble is coarse:** past 1 km a ruin's heap is its bed on an 8 m grid with its few big blocks, not the cover's chunks or the pieces (about 20 k triangles a ruin). Its impostor holds a skeleton's frame and a stump's fins.
- [ ] **The Spindle shell breaks up at its base and top seen from above** (its skirt, its fins, its lamp): handed to Gemini to diagnose, `HANDOFF-SHELL.md` (2026-11-03), with what is fixed, what was tried and the hypotheses.
- [ ] **Impostors are 128 px a view** (fourteen states in a 4096 set, colour and normal, 128 MB; 160 px would be 5120, 210 MB): shells now stand to 1.4 km, where a Spindle is about 140 px tall on a 697 px screen, so the impostors start near 1:1. Closer than that they would be soft.
- [ ] **The fourteen designs are heroes only:** no plans, shells or impostors (they wait on `HANDOFF-SHELL.md`), so past 300 m they are not drawn. Their lobbies are empty floors.
- [ ] **The Spiral's ramp is not walkable:** a walker enters its gate, but the ramp has no walk floor and its portals do not open.
- [ ] **No tube network:** the Organ's transit tube was dropped (the owner, 2026-11-03) until buildings can be joined by tubes; the Pods' tube joins its own two pods only.
- [ ] **The Ancients kit's buildings are heroes only:** no walk outline (a walker passes through their walls), no plans, shells or impostors; their doors are the kit's, drawn, not opened. The Apartments (135 k) and Skyscraper A (107 k) are the heaviest heroes on the page.
- [ ] **The impostors bake at load, one a frame** (0.3 to 1.8 s each): the page is slow for its first frames, and a screenshot then can time out.
