# kits/metropolis: known issues

- [ ] **The farm belt's ruins leave loose remnants** (`46f`, `54f`, 2026-11-10): `MP.ruinDesign` (59b) keeps a skeleton's piers (instanced
  panels) to the frame's top and drops every merged triangle above the skin, so piers stand between slabs with a gap under them, and the tear can
  leave a pier's head over a dropped panel. The kit page counts them (`Farm ...: every state built`): from 0 (the pump house) to 216 (the stack's
  tiers); the whole and gutted heroes have none. Every design's ruins do the same; a skeleton that keeps its ribs (the pod's dome, the vault's)
  needs the ruin cut to keep merged frame members, not only instanced ones. A growing stack's ruin keeps its terraces' slabs over the tiers that fell.
- [ ] **The farm families' shells are boxes** (the valley's middle tier for every design): a pod's dome or the aqueduct's arches read as a box
  past the hero's band (600 m on the valley's page). The polar mode would suit the pod, the market and the water tower.

- [ ] **The shells and impostors take no shadow and cast none** (they draw their own light): a tower's shadow on the street stops when it turns into a shell, past 300 m, mostly outside the shadow box (`core/shadow`, 90 to 600 m). One box, no cascades: far shadows wait on cascades (`core/shadow/README.md`).
- [ ] **Site:** the sky stands at `core/atmos`'s default site. The valley's own comes with the valley.
- [ ] **Soffits under 200 m** break up in the shell, inside the hero's range: a problem only if the switch is held closer than 300 m.
- [ ] **The soffit's edge** where the Spindle's neck meets the lamp is stepped in the shell under about 200 m: the switch from the side map to the soffit map is a hard test.
- [ ] **Software GL loses the context, on every page here** (found 2026-11-03): the harness asks SwiftShader for `--use-gl=swiftshader`, which this machine's Chromium 151 no longer takes; its GPU process exits and the first WebGL context is lost, then restored about a second later, with shader errors and empty bake read-backs between. `--use-angle=swiftshader` fixes it (no loss on host-example 3 of 3, nor on this page): the fix is commit b00abe65a on branch `worktree-agent-a167240fec837e244`, cut from `claude/biome-rebuild` (`tools/harness.py`), not yet on this branch. Close this once the branch takes that harness.
- [ ] **One Arc run crashed the page on load** (`Page.goto: Page crashed`, 2026-11-02 04:15), once; 14 runs since (2 full verifies, 12 loads) did not. Not this page's memory: a renderer past V8's heap limit exits 0xe0000008 and fires no crash event (the load would hang for its timeout), while the crash event comes from a kill from outside or a real renderer crash. The machine was heavily oversubscribed (40.8 of 53.9 GB committed on 15.4 GB of RAM). The harness on b00abe65a prints the renderer's exit code on a death, so the next one names itself.
- [ ] **The Ledger's reception takes an opt-in recipe** (`kits/ancients-interiors` `reception`, with a counter) until the next interiors update; its frame is squared here (`mpFrameDoors`) until the kit's frame takes the door wall's normal (`TODO.md`).
- [ ] **Up to 24 real towers** stand within 300 m (the cap the tiers allow, 2026-11-02, up from 10): in a dense block every near tower is real, so its cost is the city's density. With more than 24, the rest are shells drawn close, which smear.
- [ ] **`AI.barPlate` draws its cross walls diagonally** (`kits/ancients-interiors/src/35-ai-plates.js`, the `.x` wall: `[inner[0], outer[m]]`, from the room's inner start to its outer end; it should be `outer[0]`). Every bar storey of the Ledger had a wall across each room corner to corner, one of them across the entrance. `41-mp-plans.js` straightens them here; the kit's fix reaches `kits/ancients` (some 30 pages) and Noah's Regret, so it waits for the next all-builds rebuild.
- [ ] **Rubble can still reach a neighbour at `?pitch=140`:** a ruined Spindle is turned so its fall line heads where the fewest neighbouring blocks hold towers, but with every block full a 120 m mound crosses into one. The Ledger's spills run along its long sides.
- [ ] **The far rubble is coarse:** past 1 km a ruin's heap is its bed on an 8 m grid with its few big blocks, not the cover's chunks or the pieces (about 20 k triangles a ruin). Its impostor holds a skeleton's frame and a stump's fins.
- [ ] **The Spindle shell's portal canopies are ragged at their tips seen from above** (2026-11-03): Gemini's diagnosis and fix (`HANDOFF-SHELL.md`, "Diagnosis and fix") cleared the skirt's banded dithering, the fins' smear and the lamp's flicker and teeth; the three canopies (5 m proud of the skirt at 8.2 m) still break into dark splinters at their ends at fov 20 from (260, 420, 260).
- [ ] **Impostors are 128 px a view** (fourteen states in a 4096 set, colour and normal, 128 MB; 160 px would be 5120, 210 MB): shells now stand to 1.4 km, where a Spindle is about 140 px tall on a 697 px screen, so the impostors start near 1:1. Closer than that they would be soft.
- [ ] **No tube network:** the Organ's transit tube was dropped (the owner, 2026-11-03) until buildings can be joined by tubes; the Pods' tube joins its own two pods only.
- [ ] **The impostors bake at load, one a frame** (0.3 to 1.8 s each): the page is slow for its first frames, and a screenshot then can time out.

- [ ] **The designs' heroes carry their floors in their triangles** (the Organ about 10 k more, the Swell 18 k): their PLAN.md budgets were set before the floors.

- [ ] **The shell shader holds the Spindle's own heights** (Gemini's fix, 2026-11-03): its skirt (0.65 to 22 m), portal canopies (7.6 to 8.7 m), lamp (over 231.3 m), dome (234.05 to 238.2 m, foot 13.6 m) and fin look-ahead (under 85 m) are constants in `60-mp-shell-host.js`, keyed only on polar mode. Only the Spindle's shells are polar today; before another polar shell is baked (the round designs), they must come from its record (per-instance bands), or its own heights will be read as the Spindle's.
- [ ] **A design's ruins are cut by triangles, not clipped:** the torn edge steps by the facade's storey quads (as the Spindle's does). The designs and the Ancients kit's buildings have no shells or impostors yet, so they stand only in the design and ruin views, not the tiers' city.
- [ ] **The Swell's lobes leave open floor where they meet** (mid-height): no rooms are planned there, the floor is a hall between the lobes' corridors and the concourse.
- [ ] **Two designs' ruins run over their hero budget** (counting the pieces still standing; the slabs now sampled about 6 m round and 8 m deep): the Wedge's crown 49 k against 45 k, the Organ's 99 k against 90 k (its whole hero is 89 k). The others are under (the Swell's crown was 161 k, now 66 k).
- [ ] **The Terrarium's legs carry nothing:** their pavilions are ticket halls with street doors only; the way up is the service stalk at the middle. Inclined lifts in the legs would need a shaft kind the plans, the drawing and the walk do not have (a stair up them would be about 40 degrees).
- [ ] **A short bridge's doorway is cut only through the kit's small pieces** (55, `MP.ancientDoorways`): where a grid plan's bridge of 8 m or less crosses a wall, the kit's instanced pieces and triangles under 16 m2 in its path go; a wall drawn as one large triangle keeps no opening (it would need the triangle split). The Honeycomb Block loses 88 pieces, the Reliquary 85, the Offices 13.
- [ ] **Sky A's core stands in its atrium on landings `MP.floors` reads under the spire's cover** (r 11 m, every storey from 60 m): a stair and lift tower with bridges to the office ring, rational, but the landings are the cells' own reading, not the kit's.
- [ ] **A grid plan's walls follow 1 m cells, straightened to 0.5 m:** a curved building's partitions are straight lines between steps, and its rooms' outlines are within half a metre of their walls.
- [ ] **The Organ's pipes have small rooms:** a pipe of r 9 m leaves a band of 3.1 m between its corridor and the skin (the core takes 3.2 m, the corridor 1.8 m); the wider pipes 4.1 and 5.1 m.
- [ ] **The Wedge's ruins are mullion cages:** it is all black curtain glass, so with the glass gone its frame stands bare in every ruin state; its slabs show inside.
- [ ] **An industrial hall takes a plate every storey** (`MP.floors` reads the hero as every Ancients building is read, one storey height a building): the Gantry Hall's 18 m sawtooth hall and the Furnace Stacks' halls get floors at 6 and 12 m, mezzanines through what would be one tall space. A tall single hall wants a storey height per part (or a record's `halls`), not yet written.
- [ ] **A big machine hall is worked only round its core:** 240 pieces a storey (about 125 k triangles) fill a cluster near the core of the Vault's 400 m floor, the rest bare. More would want the furniture instanced across storeys, or a hall drawn at range as a texture.
- [ ] **The ledges are left unworked:** a machine hall's part under 200 m2 that no shaft, bridge or street reaches (the Assembler's sheds' shelves, a mezzanine's end) is a ledge, as the room plan's thin parts are corridors; there is no way onto it.
- [ ] **The Terrarium's plants are the kits' as they are now:** they are vended through `SWLOW.treeAt`, `SWLOW.plantAt` and `BIO.kitBuilder`; a change to those kits' species moves the deck's planting, and the page's hash with it.
- [ ] **A wild shell holds no leaf-card plants** (2026-11-09): the biome kits' trees and floor plants planted on a wild hero are flagged `noShell` and left out of
  the bake (a leaf card bakes as a solid quad). The shell holds the strands, the moss, the soil and the kit's solid stand-ins; the planted trees show
  to the kits' own LOD range. A host that wants canopies in its shells draws solid stand-ins at the tree sockets (`MP.overgrow` with `standins: true`)
  and plants its own on top, or bakes canopy blobs of its own.
- [ ] **Moss is per vertex:** a blotch smaller than a facade quad (a 3 m bay by a storey) reads as a gradient across the quad, not a patch; the reaches are
  set wide (2.4 to 6 m) so it reads as damp staining. A moss layer in the materials would not reach the shells (the bake reads colours), so a finer
  moss wants the facade quads split or a moss map in the library blended by the same field.
- [ ] **Wild shells and impostors on this page are the Spindle's and the Ledger's only** (six wild looks shelled, two with impostors): the designs' box
  shells are the valley's (`biomes/ancientsvalley/70-host-city.js`), which bakes from its own flattened stubs; for a wild key it runs `MP.overgrow` on
  the stub after its rust (`API.md`). A wild design here is a hero in its `Wild, <family>` view only.
- [ ] **The Swell's growth is capped** (`MP.WILD_CAP`, 900 sockets of its 4,300 at a density of 1): its four lobes carry more sills than any other family,
  and uncapped its growth was 130 k triangles. The cap keeps the lowest unit hashes, so it is deterministic and the host's density still orders them.
- [ ] **A wild hero takes half a second to a second and a half to build** (the Spindle 0.5 s, the Swell 1.7 s, mostly the moss pass over its vertices):
  the page's boot grew by about 4 s for its six wild keys. A host building many wild keys should spread them over frames or bake them at build time.
