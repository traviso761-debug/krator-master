# kits/cthonian: the Cthonians' building kit (not started)

The Cthonians are the Throne's hidden cave people (`biomes/throne/NOTES.md`, "Peoples of the island"): cities built in and
around the lava tubes, dwellings carved into the tuff as spires and underground galleries; one capital (a little smaller
than Yuni, its place unknown to outsiders), three cave and tufa villages (Mungo's size), scattered surface hamlets; fungus
medicine and alchemy; the alecap, a purple mushroom brewed into beer.

`refs/` holds the owner's reference board (2026-10-07, 30 images; `islander port.jpg` is the Islanders', not theirs).
Nothing is built yet: the kit is the next step.
