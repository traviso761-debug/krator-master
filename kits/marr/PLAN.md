# Marr (`Porta Marr`): The Canal-Divided Ring Sea Port & Vassal Kit (`kits/marr`)

**Marr** (`sxmeztkqg7`, `px: 926, py: 1014`, Izani **`Porta Marr`**) is another long-assimilated sister city of Iziz and the **foremost ocean-going port of the Empire of Iziz** (`Empar de Son`) — eclipsing even **Port Tobin** (`s06eu913kl`, `px: 956, py: 944`), which serves Iziz directly at the mouth of `Bruk Falz`. Marr's sailors and pilots are renowned throughout the Ring Sea (`Si de Rriñ`), and its **Navigators' Guild** (**`Gilda de Navigadarz`**) holds the ***Greta Charta de Rriñ*** (the master sea-chart of the Ring). At the same time, Marr has a notorious reputation as a **den of vice, privateering, and smuggling**, with a stark class divide between its opulent, walled **Garden Quarter** (**`Qartar de Gardonz`**) and the labyrinthine canal slums of **The Shades** (**`De Xedaz`**) behind the eastern docks.

Like **Pylon** (`kits/pylon/PLAN.md`) and **Talia** (`kits/talia/PLAN.md`), Marr is built on the shared Iziz Vernacular engine (`settlements/iziz/VASSAL-KITS.md`): `kits/iziz-vernacular` buildings stand naturally along its streets and docks without clashing, while Marr's own kit (`kits/marr`) gives the city its unmistakable **Spanish Colonial + New Orleans + Barcelona + Bruges** character.

- **Sound-Change Rules (`tools/names/izani.py`):** `h` is lost (`house > om`, `hall > ol`, `harbour > arbar`); `w > gu` (`water > guadar`, `walk > guoka`, `wharf > guarfa`, `warehouse > gueraus`, `watch > guach`, `work > guorka`); `th > t/d` (`the > de`, `north > norta`, `south > sauta`); `-ng > ñ` (`Ring > Rriñ`, `landing > landiñ`); `s+C > is-` (`square > isqer`, `stone > iston`, `slip > islipa`, `smuggler > ismoglar`, `star > istar`, `stoop > istupa`); intervocalic/post-nasal `t/k/p > d/g/b` (`water > guadar`, `market > markita`, `beacon > bigon`, `navigator > navigadar`, `customs > kostomz`); final stop/cluster takes support vowel `-a` (`port > porta`, `court > korta`, `point > poinda`, `fort > forta`, `guild > gilda`, `dock > doka`, `shade > xeda`, `bridge > brij`); `-er/-or/-ire > -ar`; plural `-s > -z`; `sh/ks > x` (`shades > xedaz`, `fish > fix`); `j > j [ʒ]` (`pleasure > plejar`, `barge > barja`); `kw > q` (`quarter > qartar`, `quay > qi`); initial `r > rr` (`river > rrivar`, `row > rro`, `rope > rropa`, `rum > rrom`); unstressed first vowel dropped since the civil war (`canal > knal`); preposition **`de`**.

### 3.2 The Four-Way Architectural Synthesis
1. **Spanish Colonial Backbone (Antigua, Old San Juan, Havana — `image.png`, `image_2.png`, `image_3.png`, `image_4.png`, `image_8.png`):**
   - Continuous streetwalls forming perimeter blocks (`korta` blocks) with heavy studded timber carriage gates leading through a vaulted passage into an interior arcaded courtyard.
   - Carved pinkish-grey volcanic stone portals framing tall doors, projecting wrought-iron window cages on tiled corbel sills, and shaped Baroque parapets crowned with stone finials and marigolds/agaves.
2. **New Orleans French Quarter & Garden District (`image_9.png`, `image_10.png`, `image_11.png`, `image_12.png`, `image_13.png`, `image_14.png`):**
   - **Wrap-Around Wrought-Iron Galleries:** Two- and three-storey cast/wrought-iron galleries supported on slender iron columns with lacy valance friezes and scroll spandrel brackets, wrapping continuously around street corners (`image_10.png`) and overflowing with hanging fern baskets and cascading bougainvillea (`image_11.png`).
   - **Garden Quarter Mansions (`image_9.png`):** Detached white-and-ochre stucco villas set inside walled gardens, with double-tier black iron galleries, arched French doors with stained-glass fanlights (`image_14.png`), and hipped tile roofs.
   - **Low-Lying Creole & Shotgun Cottages (`image_13.png`):** Toward the marshy southern and eastern edges of the city (`Qartar Lo`), heights step down to single-storey raised cottages on brick pier stoops (`istupaz`) with short iron railings, decorative white timber eaves brackets, and tall shuttered windows.
3. **Barcelona (`image_5.png`, `image_6.png`):**
   - **Chamfered Corners & Glazed Bay Windows:** Corner buildings at major intersections cut a 45° chamfered face carrying a multi-storey projecting timber-and-iron glazed bay window with colored glass transoms.
   - **Polychrome Tilework & Vaulted Loggias:** Ceramic tile mosaics in pools, stair risers, and under-balcony soffits.
4. **Bruges & Havana Canal Urbanism:**
   - Buildings along the three great canals rise sheer from battered stone quays or low arcaded water-walks, featuring stepped and scroll-volute canal gables, projecting timber hoist-beams over upper warehouse doors, and barred boat-gates (`Geta de Guadar`) where skiffs slip directly into vaulted undercrofts.

## 4. Inhabited Interior Courtyards Rule (Mandatory — No Empty Space Between Buildings)

**User requirement:** *Interior courtyards must look inhabited — with gardens (whether of the vegetable or pleasure variety) or small outbuildings — never just empty space between placed buildings.*

In Marr, this rule operates at **two scales**:
1. **Inside multi-wing `VERN.def` compounds** (courtyard houses, `palas`, tenements, landmarks), the builder (`buildMar*`) populates the central patio directly using shared courtyard helpers (`maPatioPleasure`, `maPatioVegetable`, `maPatioWorking`).
2. **Inside city perimeter blocks** where perimeter row/corner buildings surround a central block interior, the city layout script (`settlements/marr`) **never leaves bare terrain**: every block interior is subdivided into walled parcel yards and filled with one of three dedicated `VERN.def` courtyard assemblies (`mar_patio_pleasure`, `mar_patio_vegetable`, `mar_patio_working`).

## 5. City Layout Plan, Enclosing Wall & Street Grid Morphology (`kits/marr/blueprint/city_layout.png`)

Marr occupies a `~780 × 720 m` footprint (`x ∈ [-390, +390] m`, `z ∈ [-360, +360] m`) at the head of the Cistal Estuary. The **Ring Sea Harbour** lies to the north (`z < -136 m`), sheltered by a stone **Breakwater Mole** (**`Mol de Porta`**) extending east from the rocky western promontory (**`Poinda de Navigadarz`**).

### 5.1 Enclosing Fortified City Wall (`Muraya de Marr`) with *Garitas* & *Garita*-Like Towers
The entire historic and expanded city is enclosed by the **`Muraya de Marr`** (`mar_curtain_wall`, `mar_garita_post`, `mar_garita_watch`, `mar_castillo_bastion`), modeled on Old San Juan and Havana:
- **Landward & Sea-Wall Ramparts:** Battered volcanic ashlar and *tapia* curtain walls trace the western, southern, and eastern perimeters (`x ≈ -374..+366 m`, `z ≈ +228 m` on the south) and step along the northern cliff-top of `Poinda de Navigadarz` and the eastern shoreline of `Kosta de Xedaz`.
- **Cantilevered Sentry *Garitas* (`Garita de Guach` / `mar_garita_post`):** 22 circular corbelled stone sentry boxes with terracotta-and-saffron domes project out over the water and curtain wall angles.
- **Octagonal/Circular *Garita*-Like Bastion Watchtowers (`Tauar de Garita` / `mar_garita_watch`):** 16 larger bastion towers with corbelled rings and domed cupolas anchor every salient angle (`Tauar de Pilotaz`, `Tauar de Palmaz`, `Tauar de Fontinz`, `Tauar de Sitrus`, `Baluarte de Guesta`, `Baluarte de Rropa`, `Tauar de Dais`, `Tauar de Smoka`, `Tauar de Karpindarz`, `Baluarte de Marxa`) and guard the three canal mouths into the harbour (paired verdigris-domed harbour chain towers).
- **Fortified Gates (`Portaz`) & Canal Water-Gates (`Arkaz de Agua`):** Twin-towered Baroque gates pierce the southern rampart at **`Porta de Gardonz`** (`x = -225, z = +228`), **`Arka de Agua & Porta de Iziz`** (`x = 0, z = +228`, spanning the Cistal Estuary entry), and **`Porta de Xedaz`** (`x = +220, z = +228`). Four *extramuros* plantation *fincas* (`Farma de Sitrus`, `Farma de Rrivar`, `Farma de Mil de Rrom`, `Farma de Koton & Indigo`) lie outside the southern wall.

### 5.2 Natural Coastline & River Delta Contours (Zero Floating Blocks)
From the south (`+z`), the **`Rrivar Cistal` & `Rrivar stara` confluence** enters through `Arka de Agua` and bifurcates around **Three Inner Delta Islands**, opening into the curving Ring Sea shoreline (`coast_z(x)`):
1. **`Knal Greta` ("Great Canal", `24–26 m` wide, central channel):** Runs north along `x ≈ 0` between Inner Island I (`Isla de Sidi Olda`) and Inner Islands II & III (`Isla de Marchondaz` & `Isla de Kanal`), spanned by **`Brij de Porta`** (`z = -84`), **`Brij de Isqer`** (`z = -18`), and **`Brij de Rrivar`** (`z = +88`).
2. **`Knal de Gardonz` ("Canal of the Gardens", `18–19 m` wide, western branch):** Diverges from `Knal Greta` at `z = +204 m` and curves northwest to `x = -130 m` at the harbour mouth, separating Inner Island I from the **Left Bank** (`Qartar de Gardonz` & `Poinda de Navigadarz`), spanned by **`Brij de Gardonz`** (`z = -54`), **`Brij de Rrozaz`** (`z = +35`), and **`Brij de Kreol`** (`z = +125`).
3. **`Knal de Xedaz` ("Canal of the Shades", `16–17 m` wide, eastern branch):** Diverges from `Knal Greta` at `z = +194 m` and curves northeast to `x = +158 m` at the harbour mouth, separating Inner Islands II & III from the **Right Bank** (`De Xedaz`), spanned by **`Brij de Xedaz Norta`** (`z = -64`), **`Brij de Xedaz Sauta`** (`z = +45`), and **`Brij de Barjaz`** (`z = +125`).
4. **`Knal de Ismoglarz` ("Canal of Smugglers", `13 m` wide, transverse channel at `z ≈ +2..+12 m`):** Splits the eastern delta into **Inner Island II (`Isla de Marchondaz`, north)** and **Inner Island III (`Isla de Kanal`, south)**, spanned by **`Brij de Ismoglarz`** (`x = +84 m`).

Every single block (*cuadra*), quay (`vern_quay`), and waterfront building is mapped through contour-conforming domain functions (`coast_inland(x)`, `gard_L/R(z)`, `greta_L/R(z)`, `xed_L/R(z)`, `ismog_cz(x)`) so that **zero blocks ever touch or float over water**. All 7 finger piers (`Kostgarda`, `Piar de Kostomz`, `Islipa de Feri`, `Guarfa de Kren`, `Piar de Marchondaz`, `Landiñ de Fix`, `Piar de Rropa`) project exclusively from the three solid stone quays between the canal mouths.

### 5.3 Dual Street-Grid Morphology: Chaotic Jittered Inner 3 Islands vs. Planned Radiating Left & Right Banks
- **The Older, Chaotic Inner 3 Islands (`Isla de Sidi Olda`, `Isla de Marchondaz`, `Isla de Kanal`):**
  - Built using the **jittered organic street-grid generator from `claude/kirstabal-plan-3bc37d-sl8bg9`** (`core/city/37-city-grid.js`, `KCITY.grid.plan` / `build_kirstabal_jitter_island`):
    1. Interior grid lines wander by `jit = 0.24–0.26` (`S(seed, 11/12, i)`), and interior street crossings are nudged by `0.52 * jit` (`S(seed, 13/14, i, j)`), while outer boundary vertices remain locked to the curving island canal banks.
    2. Internal street segments drop with probability `pDrop = 0.26–0.30` (never on main avenues, never leaving a crossing with degree `< 2`, and never disconnecting the street graph), merging adjacent cells into irregular L-shaped and double colonial *cuadras*.
    3. Street segments bend at their midpoint (`pBend = 0.48–0.50`, lateral deflection `0.065 * L`), giving the three original delta islands an unmistakable older, organic medieval-colonial fabric.
- **The Planned Left Bank (`Qartar de Gardonz` & `Poinda de Navigadarz`) & Right Bank (`De Xedaz`) Expansions:**
  - Mapped on **regular curvilinear coordinate grids** (`map_left_bank(u, v)` and `map_right_bank(u, v)`) whose avenues (`u = const`) **radiate outward from the curving river channels** (`Knal de Gardonz` and `Knal de Xedaz`) toward the outer city walls (`west_wall_x(z)`, `east_wall_x(z)`), and whose cross-streets (`v = const`) **radiate inland from the curving Ring Sea shoreline** (`coast_inland(x)`).
