## Ground storey and the grounds (terrace top y 1.2)

Depth is rank. Gate, forecourt (petitioners, the guard), the front flight, the loggia, the vestibule between its two guard rooms, the audience hall under the dome, and only then the governor's cabinet behind the dais. The city's magistrates stop at the Hall of the Talei Council (front left), which has its own door from the forecourt. Government (the Chancery and Customs of the East Road, front right) opens off the vestibule, not off the hall. Service runs behind: the kitchen in the round rear pavilion, the servery, the back stair, the cart gate.

Two routes, not one: petitioners and clerks climb the outside stair to the chancery's upper floor and never cross the hall. The galleries look down into the audience hall. The residence is the rear row and the two rear pavilions, reached only by the private stair from the cabinet and the back stair from the service side. The great officers live where they work: the garrison commander over the guard pavilion, the chief secretary over the chancery, the steward over the servery. An Izani envoy is lodged over the council pavilion.
