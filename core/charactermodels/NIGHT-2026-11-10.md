## 2. The vendored copies stripped from `build_inspired_outfits.py`

The builder carried 25 copies of the package's functions, some from an older core (its own `torso_tube`, `limb_tube`,
`smooth_push`, the arm and limb masks). They are imported from the package now, so 21 builders build on the one core.

- `handoff-shots/strip-2026-11-10.jpg`: every changed piece, full figure and collar views (four columns).
- 71 of 276 pieces change. Most by millimetres: the builder's old arm and limb masks differ from the core's, so

  sentinel mantle, watch cuirass), and four big robes move up to 17 cm inside their drape (the rustic envoy's
  gambeson, the Hykkousoi hierophant's robe, the aedile's agbada, the heliarch's mantle): the same silhouette in the
  full-figure shots, the hang and flare from the core's tube.

## 3. Issue 13: belts, sashes and medallions floating

A belt or sash below the shoulders conforms to the trunk at its own height plus 3 mm, in as well as out; its skin
weights and morph source come from the trunk under it; a medallion or buckle takes its weights from the trunk vertex
nearest its centre rather than a rigid spine joint.

- `handoff-shots/fix13-2026-11-10.jpg`: the 22 changed pieces at the waist, front and side. The bands come in by
  2.5 to 6.4 cm (the askari cuirass band most).

## 4. Issue 1, the builder half: tiers through each other at the hips

A tier that lost its chains to the tier over it kept the body's weights and swung through the outer tier; it now
takes the outer tier's chain weights where the outer covers it. Nine pieces re-weight, none moves.

- `handoff-shots/fix1-2026-11-10.jpg`: the fifteen in a walk and a run, front and side (the difference is in
  motion and small in a still; the full-set audit at the end measures it).

## 5. Issue 9: motifs stretching on flared skirts

A part's texture strip was painted at its mean circumference, so a hem band's motif repeated the same number of
times on every row: stretched at a flared hem, squeezed at the waist. Each row of a band is now tiled at that row's
own circumference, so a motif keeps its size in metres from waist to hem. Geometry unchanged; textures only.

- `handoff-shots/fix9-2026-11-10.jpg`: the pieces whose textures changed, full figure and hips.

## 6. Issue 5: holes at open edges

The hide mask hid the skin right up to an open edge that stands off the body (a lapel, a hem over a gap, a slit),
showing a hole into the body. Every open edge now keeps the skin drawn within a margin that grows with how far the
edge stands off the skin: none within 5 mm (a snug neck must still hide to its edge), the full 3.5 cm from 15 mm.

- Tried and taken back: by topology every grid's edge is open (armholes, band rows, hems), so the margin shed a
  quarter of the mask on coats and cuirasses. Not committed; the review's section 6 says what it wants instead.

## 7. Issue 4: sleeves too far off the arm

`limb_tube` caps an arm tube's ease at 2 cm and its flare at 2.8 cm for every caller (only `sleeve()` had the cap).

- `handoff-shots/fix4-2026-11-10.jpg`: the changed pieces in the A-pose, arms out, front and back.

## 8. A gate that lied, and the completion

Every dry build from issue 13 on had stopped halfway (the island shaman's arm wraps carry the word "wrap", were
taken for a belt, and failed Check 8), and the comparison counted only the pieces both builds held. So the commits for
issues 13, 1 and 9 adopted half the cultures. Fixed (an arm part is never a band; the sleeve cap spares pauldrons),
every culture rebuilt in one go, and every piece that differs from the repo's adopted in the completion commit.

- `handoff-shots/complete-2026-11-10.jpg`: every piece the completion changed (106: 30 Yuni, 26 Order, 16 Xanadu,
  11 Yusar, 9 Abyssal, 5 Eternian, 3 Rustic, 2 Republic, 2 nomad, 2 islander), full figure and arms; before is the
  strip's state, after has issues 13, 1, 9 and 4 together. Against the strip, 58 pieces move, the most by 5 cm (the
  Yuni trooper tunic and geomancer coat: their belts in, their sleeves capped).

## 9. The night in numbers

The 84 outfits that wear a changed piece, before against after: "inside" down 11 %, "under" down 12 %, skin down
3 %. 59 rows better, 37 worse. The regressions to look at first: the islander shaman (his mantle's "inside" doubled),
the Chichani ordinator and chosen, the Xanadu priest, the Order cham's band under its robe. The 60 Blender-built
baseline outfits are unchanged.

**Correction (2026-11-10, afternoon).** Section 9's numbers and its list of regressions are withdrawn: the scratch
pages used for the "before" side kept the repo's kit data with old pieces, so their hide masks and cloth chains did
not belong to the meshes drawn. The sheets in sections 1 to 8 have the same flaw in a milder form (shapes right,
hide masks and chains the repo's of the moment). Re-run on kit-consistent pages, the night stands as a modest net
gain on the 84 outfits it touched: "inside" down 5.8 %, "under" 2.4 %, skin 2.4 %, "hole" 3.6 %; 45 rows better, 30
worse, the worse ones smaller (the Yusar dronemaster, the Abyssal trade woman, the Xanadu priest, the Yuni trade
woman, the painted warden). The islander shaman and the Order cham were never regressions.
