# The night of 2026-11-10: Phase 2 on the garments, with pictures

What ran overnight on `claude/garment-defects`, in order, each step gated the same way: every culture dry-built into a
scratch root before and after (the repo untouched), the geometry of every piece hashed and compared, every changed
piece shot before and after in the Cosmetic Lab, and the sheet put in `handoff-shots/`. The numbers and the method are
in `REVIEW-GARMENTS-2026-11-06.md`, section 6; this file is the short list of what to look at.

Each sheet pairs **before (left) | after (right)** per shot; the file name says the view (`full` the whole figure,
`collar` head and shoulders, `waist` the belt line, `hips`, `legs` a crouch, `arms` the A-pose).

## 2. The vendored copies stripped from `build_inspired_outfits.py`

The builder carried 25 copies of the package's functions, some from an older core (its own `torso_tube`, `limb_tube`,
`smooth_push`, the arm and limb masks). They are imported from the package now, so 21 builders build on the one core.

- `handoff-shots/strip-2026-11-10.jpg`: every changed piece, full figure and collar views (four columns).
- 71 of 276 pieces change. Most by millimetres: the builder's old arm and limb masks differ from the core's, so
  every tube fitted against them shifts a little. Five trunk garments change their row count (the core's torso tube
  spaces rows by height, the old copy by a fixed step: the magistrate coat, heliophant mantle, praetorian lorica,
  sentinel mantle, watch cuirass), and four big robes move up to 17 cm inside their drape (the rustic envoy's
  gambeson, the Hykkousoi hierophant's robe, the aedile's agbada, the heliarch's mantle): the same silhouette in the
  full-figure shots, the hang and flare from the core's tube.

## 3. Issue 13: belts, sashes and medallions floating

A belt or sash below the shoulders conforms to the trunk at its own height plus 3 mm, in as well as out; its skin
weights and morph source come from the trunk under it; a medallion or buckle takes its weights from the trunk vertex
nearest its centre rather than a rigid spine joint.

- `handoff-shots/fix13-2026-11-10.jpg`: the 22 changed pieces at the waist, front and side. The bands come in by
  2.5 to 6.4 cm (the askari cuirass band most).

## 4. Issue 1, the builder half: tiers through each other at the hips

A tier that lost its chains to the tier over it kept the body's weights and swung through the outer tier; it now
takes the outer tier's chain weights where the outer covers it. Nine pieces re-weight, none moves.

- `handoff-shots/fix1-2026-11-10.jpg`: the nine in a walk, front and side.
