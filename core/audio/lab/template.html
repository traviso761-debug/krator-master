      Every clip here is public domain, CC0 or CC BY, so all of it can go into a game that is sold; CC BY clips say who to credit. Non-commercial, no-derivatives and share-alike recordings, and anything not verified at its source, never come in (core/audio/data/licenses.json). Royalty-free clips that may not be re-shared are kept off this shared page. These are small previews; the full-quality files stay in the audio folder. Rendered scenes and cuts come with a credits file.

      Cut

      Drag across the waveform to select a passage; drag a selection edge to adjust it; click to place the cursor. Shape it with the sound controls below, then save it as a new clip, send it to the scene, or save it as a WAV. "Find loop points" moves the end of the selection to where the sound matches its start, so the cut repeats cleanly.

      No selection
      Sound Heard as you play; saved with the clip and carried into the scene.

      Cut clips

        Cues

        A cue is a sound the game asks for by name, like sfx.combat.hit.metal. It holds several variants and plays a different one each time, with a little random pitch and volume, never repeating the last few. Add variants with "+ Cue" in the Library or "Add to cue" in Cut, then fire it to hear the variety.

            Scene

            Each track is a clip or a cut. A bed plays continuously; calls come back every few seconds, with a random spread, a chance to skip, and random pitch, volume and pan per call. Play the scene to hear it live, then render it: with seamless loop on, the end is crossfaded into the start so the file repeats without a seam.
