# Proposal: the Beast Riders on the sim layer (`Region 87`, Pinnacle Rock, Mav's Refuge, Girder, Talon & the six tribal ranges)

This proposal scopes **the Beast Riders** (`rmvlp5pd3ug40`, placeholder map label `"Region 87"`, together with their four permanent settlements, ruined Imperial border forts, arcology salvage grounds, and seasonal camp circuits) for `core/region`, `core/camp` (`C3–C7`), and `core/hamlet`, following the pattern established in `core/simulation/PROPOSAL-LONGSONG-PINNACLE.md` (§3) and `core/simulation/PROPOSAL-XANADU.md`.

All spatial, climatic, hydrological, 39-family geological, crop (`CROPS.forBiome` / `CROPS.at`), and band-census (`KCAMP.census`) numbers below come from a direct scan of Scale Model 5.1 (`51-region-store.js`, `60-settlements.js`), `kits/resources/data/geology.json` & `ledger.json`, `kits/crops`, and `kits/beast-riders/src/79-br-camp.js`.

## 2. 39-Family Geology, Licensed Oil Fields & Jungle/Savannah/Canyon Ecology

### 2.1 Full 39-Family Geology & Why `test-res.js` Reported *"Region 87: its towns reach every kind, it imports nothing"*

Across `Region 87`'s `5,589` geology land cells (`89,424 km²` at 4 km grid; `121` ruin-bit cells = `1,936 km²`), the bedrock is overwhelmingly **`sedimentary` (`83.0%`, `74,224 km²`)**, with upland **`granite` (`6.3%`, `5,664 km²`)**, **`crystalline` (`5.9%`, `5,232 km²`)**, and **`impact` (`3.1%`, `2,768 km²`)** restricted to the high eastern/northern crater-wall rim, plus small western **`volcanic` (`1.1%`)** and **`evaporite` (`0.5%`, `448 km²`)** patches inside the `Port Goto` overlap.

In `kits/resources/KNOWN_ISSUES.md:30` (from `46r-res-ledger.js` / `ledger.json`), `Region 87` famously triggered the fairness warning *"its towns reach every kind, it imports nothing"* (today in `ledger.json` its sole missing node kind out of 19 is `imports: ["turquoise"]`, while claiming `reach` of `iron: 812`, `coal: 1361`, `oil: 368`, `halite: 136`, `copper: 93`, `tin: 70`, `silver: 34`, `gold: 31`, `rare-earth: 20`, `potash: 19`, `saltpeter: 17`, `marble: 14`, `sulfur: 10`, `gypsum: 6`, `nickel: 6`, `emerald: 6`, `alkali: 5`, `zinc: 3`).

Our per-pin forensic scan proves that **this apparent self-sufficiency is an artefact of four compounding bugs in `geology.json` / `46r-res-ledger.js`**:

1. **Foreign `Port Goto` (`918,698`) counted as a Beast Rider town:** Because `Port Goto` sits inside the western `Shell Province` overlap, `RES_LEDGER` credits `Region 87` with `Port Goto`'s 30 km evaporite/skarn catchment — which accounts for **100% of `gypsum` (`7.0` yield), `potash` (`17.5`), `alkali` (`5.5`), and `alabaster` (`2.0`)**, **90% of `marble` (`13.0`)**, **100% of good `copper` (`4.0`)**, **100% of good `halite` (`5.5`, max byte `183`)**, and **100% of good `emerald` (`3.5`)**!
2. **Six uninhabited ruins counted as active 30 km mining towns:** `46r-res-ledger.js` sums 30 km circles around all 11 pins in `Region 87` without filtering `ruin` / `ruined arcology` types (`Fort Kreia`, `Fort Ordo`, `Sleeping City`, `Flooded Tower`, `Crescent`, `Nusidi`). Highland **`Fort Kreia` (`1049,736`) alone** sits on the eastern impact/granite rim and injects **`nickel: 5.0` (100%)**, **`zinc: 2.0` (100%)**, **`saltpeter: 11.0` (100% of good)**, **`tin: 33.0` (`29.0` good)**, **`silver: 19.0`**, **`rare-earth: 12.0`**, and **`iron: 176.0` (`160.5` good)**.
3. **Stale pre-`5.1e2`/`5.1e6` coordinates in `geology.json`:** `geology.json` still lists `Pinnacle Rock` at old `(1027, 681)` (closer to the granite rim: `150.5` good iron, `13.5` good tin vs `18.0` good iron, `1.0` good tin at current `1007,686`) and `Mav's Refuge` at old `(1024, 807)` (`8.0` sulfur, `13.5` good iron vs `0` sulfur and **`0.0` good iron** at current `1049,841`).
4. **No technology or grade filter:** Under `TRADE-SCOPE.md` §2.4, Beast Riders lack industrial drilling and chemical refining: **`oil` is unworkable without Yuni Geomancers**, **`rare-earth` is completely unworked**, and sedimentary bog/laterite iron or trace brine bytes (`max byte <= 40` or `<= 12`) cannot run blast furnaces or salt works.

### 4.3 Bio-Energetic Balance under the 2/3 Low-Gravity Appetite Rule

Applying the owner's **1/3 low-gravity appetite cut** (`2/3` of 1g baseline metabolic demand; `PROPOSAL-LONGSONG-PINNACLE.md:223-227`) across all `~3,841` tamed mounts and `25,700` humans proves why **predator meat-hunting range** and **salt** — NOT arable farmland — govern Beast Rider settlement spacing and seasonal migration:

1. **Carnivorous, Piscivorous & Insectivorous Mounts (`~2,300` head = `1,740` band + `560` town Wing/Spider/Raptor mounts):**
   - At **2/3 low-g appetite**, an average predator/flyer mount eats **`~2.7 kg meat, fish, or giant-arthropod prey/day`** (`0.985 t/yr`, vs `4.0 kg/day = 1.46 t/yr` at 1g; `PROPOSAL-LONGSONG-PINNACLE.md:225`), with smaller `Nightwing`/`Dragonfly` mounts (~`1.4 kg/day`) offsetting `7.5 m` `Quetzal`/`Tiger` mounts (~`3.8 kg/day`).
   - **Confederacy predator feed bill:** `2,300 × 0.985 t/yr` = **`~2,265 t/yr` of raw meat & fish** (`~6.2 t/day` across `80,808 km²`, or a modest `28 kg/km²/yr` harvest from dense 1.9 atm Carboniferous-scale insect, fish, and megafauna productivity).
   - **Local pinch point at `Pinnacle Rock` & `Mav's Refuge` (why `KCAMP` seasonal dispersal is mandatory):** If `Pinnacle Rock`'s ~220 permanent Wing mounts plus its wet-season predator bands (~180 predator mounts) stayed within a 10 km circle (`324 km²`) year-round, they would demand **`~394 t/yr` (`1,216 kg/km²/yr`)** — stripping local game within weeks! Dispersing the bands 30–90 km out into `R1`, `R4`, and `R5` during the dry season while supplementing town Wings with `Tsékooh Baatar` / `Crescent` smoked fish (`281` + `2,605 km²` fish-eq) drops harvest density below `120 kg/km²/yr`.
2. **Megaherbivore Browsers & Grazers (`~940` head = `772` band + `168` town/ranch Threehorns, Hardshells & Mammoths):**
   - At 2/3 low-g appetite (`~22 kg dry matter/day ≈ 8.0 t DM/yr/head`), `940` megaherbivores require **`~7,520 t DM/yr`**.
   - A ground band's fenced wet-season home paddock (`5–12 ha` in `79-br-camp.js`, yielding ~`8 t DM/ha` wet flush = `40–96 t DM`) feeds its `20–31` beasts (`160–250 t DM/yr`) for **only ~2.5–4 months**! In the dry season, the herds **must** rotate onto `R4`'s `13,595 km²` and `R1`'s `5,366 km²` open savannah/rim pastures (consuming `< 0.2%` of total regional forage, leaving 99%+ for wild prey herds hunted by the carnivore clans).
3. **Detritivores & Xylophages (`~600` head = `459` band + `141` town Longcrawlers & Staghorn Beetles):**
   - Feed on rotting hypertree logs and leaf-litter sheds (`br_tent_crawler_shed`) in a forest with `wood` byte `226/255` (`68,213 km²` timber-eq) — **zero pasture or meat cost**, making Longcrawlers and Staghorns the ideal zero-fodder capstan/pack beasts inside closed `XA` hyperjungle (`Mav's Refuge` & `Girder`)!
4. **Human Food vs Strategic Salt Deficit:**
   - **Food (`~4,112 t/yr` grain-eq for `25,700` humans at `160 kg/yr`):** Roughly **45%** comes from bushmeat, Ring Sea/lake fish, wild `mahogany-nut`/`cathedralfig`/`breadfruit`, and `combtree` honey; the **55% cultivated share (`~2,260 t/yr` of `taro`, `shade-manioc`, `rice`, `sandpan-fonio`, `ashmillet`, `plantain`)** requires only **`~23 km²` in crop (`~70 km²` with fallow)** out of `56,039 km²` `49r` farm-eq (`0.12%` utilization). Every settlement and clearing feeds itself locally.
   - **Salt (`~185 t/yr` required vs `0 t` domestic rock salt):** Humans (`25,700 × 4.5 kg = 115.7 t`), megafauna salt-licks (`3,841 × 11 kg = 42.3 t`), and smoked fish/hide/jerky curing (`~27 t`) require **`~185 t/yr` of salt**. Boiled coastal sea-brine in wet `1,600–2,300 mm` `R5` coves can supply at most **`~45–55 t/yr`** at heavy firewood cost; the remaining **`~130–140 t/yr` MUST come in through `Shade` Dasri caravans, `Talia`/`Girder` trade, Kautoa canoes, and `C7` raids/tribute on `Port Goto`!**
