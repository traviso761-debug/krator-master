## 1. Part I — The Scyvoi (`Borunsa`: `B1`–`B7` Kopje Tribes & Dryland Strip)

### 1.1 Physical Geography, Hydrology & the Kiln-Fire Cycle

In Scale Model 5.1, **Scyvoi** (`rmuoodllbc9ka`, indigenous ethnonym **`Borunsa`**, speaking **`Boruntun`**, a Turkish-Jurchen agglutinative tongue; `LORE.md` §6.16) occupies a **`39,316 km²`** (`39,148 km²` land, `168 km²` lake) step-faulted bench wedged between the Western Highlands (`Iron Republic`) and the NW Bay / League of Amphitriton coast:
- **Span & Relief:** Bounding box `X = 618..729` (`222 km` E–W) by `Y = 674..950` (`552 km` N–S), with the seven `Baelu` sites (`B5` at `725,706` down to `B7` at `652,907`) forming a **`415 km` chain** (`~340 km` core dryland strip from `B5` to `B1`, plus `100 km` southern `Csa` march to `B7`). Land elevation runs from **`-1,558 m`** on the northern Ring Sea/Trigon lake shore (`1.94 atm`) to **`+3,885 m`** along the western Iron Republic rim (`0.98 atm`), with **72.4% of all land sitting between `-1,000 m` and `0 m`** (median **`-391 m`**, **`1.68 atm`**).
- **Climate & Physiological Asymmetry:** `56.5% BSh` (`22,128 km²` hot semi-arid steppe), `19.5% Csa` (`7,624 km²` southern dry-summer bench), and `19.0% Aw` (`7,448 km²` twin-river savanna), with median rainfall **`518 mm/yr`** (`282–1,020 mm`) and median annual temperature **`27.0 °C`** (warmest month **`36.0 °C`**, extreme **`43.0 °C`**). The Borunsa are physiologically adapted to dry `36–43 °C` dense-air heat, whereas their amphibious **Hykkousoi (`Haykossa`)** enemies (`Trigon`, `Tethis`, `Pontus`) suffer fatal gill-drying if they march inland without water-trains (`LORE.md` §6.16). Conversely, the dense `~1.7 atm` oxygen-rich (`~0.4 atm` O₂) dry season turns cured grass and resinous frill-trees into **"kiln country"** (`biomes/crater-drylands/NOTES.md`): lightning and Scyvoi fire-lances drive fast, clean grass fires across a 4-stage mosaic (`char` `<0.5 yr` → `bloom` `0.5–2.5 yr` → `regrowth` `2.5–7 yr` → `old scrub` `>7 yr`).
- **Hydrology (`66 km` perennial rivers vs `100 km` seasonal wadis):**
  - **Perennial Twin Rivers (Southern/Central Heartland):** **`Cakmak Riviri`** (`r179`, Scyvoi tongue, `Q = 25.6 m³/s`, `150 km`, `46 km` inside Scyvoi at `-1,188..-361 m`) and **`Stahr Rhyver`** (`r243`, Hykkousik tongue, `Q = 10.8 m³/s`, `95 km`, `20 km` inside at `-1,049..-745 m`).
  - **Northern/Central Ephemeral Wadis (`Gulusu`):** `Sivift Gulusu` (`r496`, `6.0 m³/s` peak, `34 km` inside near `B4`), `Kumun Gulusu` (`r520`, `3.0 m³/s`, `22 km` near `B3`), `Karun Gulusu` (`r509`, `8.5 m³/s`, `12 km` at `B6`), `Serin Gulusu` (`r577`, `2.4 m³/s`) & `Filem Gulusu` (`r765`, `3.0 m³/s`) feeding the **`168 km²` northern lake arm** (`-1,500 m` near `B5`).
  - **Southern Vothic-Named Gulches:** `Brik Gülch` (`r646`, `9.3 m³/s`), `Hafn Gülch` (`r650`, `8.9 m³/s`), and `Sharf Gülch` (`r744`, `6.2 m³/s` right past `B7`), marking centuries of Vothic/Vinvolk linguistic contact north-west of Drum.

## 2. Part II — The Screamers (`Screamer Country` & `The Hexahedron`)

### 2.1 Physical Geography, Hydrology & Reconciling `827,668` vs `817,670` (*Screamer Wood*)

**Screamer Country** (`rmuca1tm8hito`) spans **`41,068 km²`** (`32,612 km²` land + `8,456 km²` NW Ring Sea shelf, `X = 769..879` / `220 km`, `Y = 621..745` / `248 km`) across the deepest, hottest northern floor of the Inner Crater:
- **Extreme Sub-Sea-Level Heat & Dense Air:** Land elevation plunges to **`-2,016 m`** in the southeastern alkali pans (**`2.06 atm`**!) and rises to **`+3,626 m`** on the northern Free Clans rim (`1.02 atm`), with **59.3% of all land below `-1,000 m`** (median **`-1,234 m`**, **`1.87 atm`**). Median land temperature is **`32.0 °C`** (warmest month **`38.0 °C`**, peak **`43.0 °C`**, coldest month **`26.0 °C`**), grading from wet **`XA` hyperjungle (`29.6%`, `9,652 km²`, `1,400–3,294 mm`)** along the coast and Steampits into **`XV` abyssal veldt (`20.4%`, `6,668 km²`)**, **`BSh` hot steppe (`20.4%`, `6,664 km²`)**, **`Aw` (`9.3%`)**, **`BWh` (`8.7%`)**, and **`XS` salt pan (`5.2%`)** inland.

### 4.2 Shared `C7` Balance Caps, `Standing` Floors & Escort Math
Aligned directly with `PROPOSAL-XANADU.md` and `PROPOSAL-LONGSONG-PINNACLE.md` §3.5 so raids never collapse the civilian economy:
1. **Subsistence Floor & Loot Cap:** A single `C7` raid steals at most **`15%` of movable stock above the victim's `14-day` `food` subsistence floor** (`maxLoot = 0.15 * Math.max(0, stock - floor14d)`), prioritizing high value-to-weight goods matching the raider's deficit (`salt`, `weapons`, `iron`, `cloth`, `grain` for Scyvoi/Painted Men; `captives`, `weapons`, `scrap` for Screamers).
2. **Population & `Standing` Guardrails:** Direct raid casualties + abductions cap at **`<= 2%` of target `pop` per raid**, with a per-band cooldown of **`60` sim-days** and per-target raid immunity window of **`30` sim-days**. Garrisoned frontier anchors (`Trigon`, `Tethis`, `Pontus`, `Tzunfort`, `Drum`, `Port Visas` with Iziz guard, `Shell`, `Pinnacle Rock`, `Shade`) stay at **`standing >= 0.70`** in steady state, well above `core/simulation`'s **`0.40` broken/sack threshold** (*historical calibration: `Port Visas` ~120 years ago represents an unescorted town pushed below `0.40` by a massed Screamer brood surge*).
3. **Highway Hazard & Convoy Escort (`ROADS`):** Instead of deleting trade routes (`303` `Scyvoi Lowland Raids` routes, `23` `Screamer Fringe` routes), `SIM.trade` applies an escort-cost multiplier (`+15–35%` effective km) or skims a **`3–6%` raid/toll attrition** into the nearest active `Baelu` / `Brood` stock when unescorted; state-funded armed escorts (e.g. `Iziz-Guarded Visas Corridor`, `Tzunfort` patrols) suppress the skim at the cost of garrison `weapons`/`food` upkeep.
