## §1. Executive Summary: Physical Reality in Scale Model 5.1 vs. Current Krator Cultural Coverage

### 1.1 From Off-Stage Trade Sinks to Physically Modeled Worlds
In `openworld/TRADE-GEMINI.md` (lines 7, 100, 103), **`The Crook`** (`240,000` abstract exterior population, exporting `259.39` units of `frost-coral` = *Crook Frost-Coral, Verdigris Jade & Glacier Pelts*) and **`Ria Valley`** (`357,500` abstract exterior population, exporting `287.18` units of `ria-amber` = *Ria River-Amber, Hexapod Musk & Sea-Byssus* plus `19.38 t` of `magnets`) are modeled purely as off-stage maritime trade sinks arriving by coaster into the Eternal State's quarantined monopoly port at **`Farport`** (`294, 588`). Meanwhile, `biomes/STATIONS-MAP-5.1.md` (lines 108–112) notes that across this exterior expansion ground, *"who lives there is undecided."*

Yet in Scale Model `5.1` and the 39-family `geology.json`, neither region is an abstract backdrop. They are **two vast, physically opposite exterior macro-environments** separated by the ultramafic spreading horsts of **`The Arch`** and the high periglacial rim of **`Farside` / `Yeti Kingdom` / `Sasquatch Tribes`**:

1. **`The Crook` & the Western Tsunami–Ultramafic Margin (`366,320 km²` net settled dry land + `107,916 km²` dry on `The Arch` / `Steaming Seamounts` / `Battered Isle` + `149,508 km²` alpine cryosphere on `Copper Glacier`):**
   - **Tectonic & Geomorphic Character:** A `1,400 km`-long (`28.5°S` to `46.0°S`) compressional trough wedged between the Western Outer Wall (`Farside` / `w outer wall`) and the buoyant felsic Tharn-crust Western Boundary Cordillera, opening northward into the magma-starved serpentinite spreading ridge of **`The Arch`** (`100% ultramafic`).
   - **Defining Physical Hazards & Wonders:** Directly exposed to periodic **`50 m` megatsunami trains** (`+65 to +100 m` funnel run-up) generated by phreatomagmatic eruptions and flank collapses at **`Arch Krakatoan Seamount`** (`176, 322`, `r = 32 km` submarine caldera) and **`South-West Surtseyan Vent`** (`166, 338`). Sheltered pockets occur only inside **`Crookshore`'s twin drowned secondary-ejecta bay craters** (`89, 604` [`2,464 km²`] and `81, 677` [`3,312 km²`]), behind the mossy hogback water-gap of **`Silent Vales`** (`196, 937`, two quiet ribbon lakes at `+1,410 m` and `+1,240 m`), and up the `980 km` **`Kopa-Tarn Warra` (`Crook River`, `920 m³/s`)** toward turquoise **`Copper Tarn`** (`146, 1147`, `+2,380 m`).
   - **Signature 39-Family Geology:** **Krator's #1 Nephrite Jade Province** (`jade = 200` across `247,072 km²`, `3,040` lode nodes in `rmvthecrook` alone), paired with **`The Arch`'s ultramafic `nickel = 180`** (`3,691` nodes, hyperaccumulating blue-sap verdigris flora) & **`lodestone = 150`** (`780` nodes), **`Steaming Seamounts` `sulfur = 255`**, coastal **`coal = 214` / `peat = 157` / `silica = 150`**, and **`Copper Glacier` skarn `copper = 240` / `zinc = 220` / `tin = 200` / `iron = 255`**.
   - **Ancient Infrastructure Hook:** `net51-gemini.json` route `ext.southwest` traces a **`982 km` (`50 km` tunnel) Ancient trunk extension** from `Farport` (`294, 588`) SW along `Crookshore` (`152, 774`) straight into **`Silent Vales`** (`194, 982`) — `kept` on its first `7` Eternian segments out of `Farport` and `decayed` beyond (`"lore to come"`).

2. **`Ria Valley` & the Northern Great-River Loess–Karst Margin (`850,868 km²` net dry [`748,832 km²` outside `Arachnoid Canyons`], `543,990–630,928 km²` `49r` farm-eq):**
   - **Tectonic & Geomorphic Character:** A `2,000 km` east-to-west warm-temperate (`Cfa 80.7%`, `+19 to +30 °C`, `1.29–1.60 atm`) alluvial, loess, and carbonate tableland sloping smoothly from `The Dome` (`+2,925 m` shield volcano) and `Spider Lake`'s `+1,860–2,120 m` ejecta rampart down to a **warm drowned dendritic ria sound** (`658, 148`, Galician *Rías Baixas* / Chesapeake analogue with 24-hour libration oyster visors) and the twin-lobed **`Double Delta`** (`518, 152`, split by `arch-rift-basalt` horst-gates and carpeted in colonial terrestrial sponges and calcified sea-pen fields).
   - **Hydrology:** Drained by **Krator's largest exterior river network** — **`9101` `Ria River` (`Mā-Rīa`, `2,140 km`, `Q = 4,850 m³/s`)**, fed by **`9102` `Hām-Tsēk` (`Web-Cleaver River`, `890 km`, `1,650 m³/s` from `Spider Lake`)**, **`9103` `Tōng-Mā` (`780 km`, `1,420 m³/s` from `The Arch`)**, **`9107` `Pāk-Mā` (`740 km`, `1,380 m³/s`)**, **`9104` `Sī-Mā` (`520 m³/s`)**, **`9108` `Mō-Mā` (`480 m³/s`)**, and **`9109` `Chīng-Mā` (`440 m³/s`)**.

     - **Krator's #1 Magnetic Anomaly (`Compass Hills` `rmweu4kpowzji`):** `lodestone = 220` across `100%` of the polygon (`46,784 km²`, `1,129` lode nodes), carrying the canonical `world-pins` note: *"Iron-rich hills magnetized by an Ancient installation at their center; prime source of magnets (lodestones for compasses and high-tech dynamos/servos)."*

## §5. Geopolitical Framing (A vs. B vs. C), Grounded Populations & Recommended Pairing

### 5.1 Resolving the Geopolitical & Population Framing Question (Options A, B, and C)
How populous and politically centralized should `The Crook` and `Ria Valley` be relative to the inner crater (`Voth` ~`80k–120k`, `Eternal State` ~`150k–220k`, `Dalab` ~`85k`, `Iron Republic` ~`60k`, `Yeti` ~`12k`, `Coyote Outlands` ~`35k–50k`)? Scale Model `5.1`'s `49r` physics exposes a **dramatic asymmetry** between the two regions:

* **`The Crook`** (`366,320 km²` dry, `188,154 km²` strict-arable fragmented across steep coastal hogbacks, cool `Dfb/Cfb` upland troughs, and `50 m` megatsunami scour zones): `TRADE-GEMINI.md`'s abstract `240,000` is **4×–6× too high** for a non-imperial, tsunami-battered fjord-and-shoal coast without state irrigation. At Krator's inner-crater rural/maritime densities, `The Crook` naturally supports **`30,000–52,000` people**.
* **`Ria Valley`** (`748,832 km²` core dry, **`535,813 km²` contiguous `Cfa` strict-arable loess/alluvial plain** along a `2,140 km`, `4,850 m³/s` navigable trunk river): Physically, `Ria Valley` is the **largest, flattest, most fertile warm-temperate river valley on Krator**. Even without an arcology-backed state bureaucracy (prevented by `Weeping Valley`'s haunted ruin-zone splitting the middle river and `Steaming Seamounts` tsunamis battering the outer delta), it effortlessly sustains **`110,000–175,000` people** across decentralized river-towns and bluff republics!
