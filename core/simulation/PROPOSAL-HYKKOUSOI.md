# Hykkousoi Sphere (`League of Amphitriton` & `Hookfleet`) — Polis, Cleruchy, Resource & Maritime Trade Proposal

**Scope.** This proposal scopes the **Hykkousoi maritime sphere** on Scale Model 5.1 (`tools/scale-model/51-region-store.js`, `60-settlements.js`), covering two seafaring political polygons:
1. **`League of Amphitriton`** (`rmuooc7krl9f8`, `94,104 km²`) — the classical-organic confederacy of rib-gilled **Hykkousoi** city-states (`Ys`, `Tethis`/`Tethys`, `Trigon`, `Pontus`) and their Throne, lake, and island outposts (`Nereus`, `Limnaia`, `Kyra Isle`, `Skopelos Isle`, `Kierson`, `Ybera`, and `Tethis`'s contested East-Ring shipyard isle of `Nephtos`), presided over at the water-girt **Amphitriton** by **Archon Jathrocles** (`LORE.md` §6.16);
2. **`Hookfleet`** (`rmwek559wleuj`, `5,708 km²`) — the displaced maritime fleet-polity and **50-km no-piracy truce haven** at **`Port Mira`** (`865,895`), anchored on three volcanic islands `78 km` ENE of **`Hook`** (`809,923`, retaken from Voth by Vinzenda IX's reformed Izani army a few months before game start) and operating between `Hook`, `Porta Marr` (`926,1014`, `~20,000` souls), `Port Tobin`, `Noa's Regret`, `Voth`, and the League.

*(Cross-reference: **`Dilihan Province`** `rmvlqd5ix7mfn` and **`Shell Province`** `rmvlqffsaj2v0` are Imperial Izani exclaves — `LORE.md` §6.2 — and are scoped in sibling `core/simulation/PROPOSAL-IZIZ-EXCLAVES.md`. Here they appear strictly as **external border and transit gateways**, notably Izani–Dilihan envoys and Cut/Ardent cargoes crossing League waters under Archon Jathrocles's watchful permission).*

All figures come from a full-raster scan of Scale Model 5.1 (`1821 × 1723`, `2 km/px`, `4 km²/px`), the 39-family `kits/resources/data/geology.json` tables, `core/world/49r-world-resources.js` continuous indices, `kits/crops` (`CROPS.at`, `CROPS.palette`), `kits/ringsea` (31 hulls), `core/hamlet`, sibling session blueprints for **`Porta Marr`** (`~20,000` souls) and **`Drum`** (`13,500` souls), and reconciliation against `openworld/TRADE-GOODS.md` (2026-11-12) and Gemini's 2026-11-14 world pass (`populations-gemini.json`, `trade-gemini.json`, `trade-fleets-gemini.json`, `net51-gemini.json`).

## 1. Macro-Geography, Gill-Ecology & Waters of the Hykkousoi World

Together, the **`League of Amphitriton`** and **`Hookfleet`** span **`99,812 km²`** (`24,953` cells on 5.1): **`50,060 km²` of dry land** (`50.2%`) and **`49,752 km²` of water** (`49.8%`), including **`48,464 km²` of Ring Sea (`wlev = -1,500 m`)**, **`1,288 km²` of inland fresh and caldera-apron lakes**, and **`5,856 km²` of warm shallow shelf (`depth <= 35 m`, `tm >= 26 °C`)** — the richest pearl, nacre, building-shell, and murex-purple nursery on Krator.

### 1.1 Biological & Climatic Envelope: Why the Hykkousoi Hug Wet Air (`LORE.md` §6.16)

Most trueborn Hykkousoi carry **rib-gills** (`core/charactermodels` `tools/mods.py`) across chest and back. A bare-chested Hykkousoi diver cannot drown and worships at submarine shrines on the sea floor — yet **prolonged exposure to dry air (`BSh`, `XS`, `BWh`) causes painful, fatal *gill-wither***. Scale Model 5.1's climate rasters confirm how tightly the League's borders track this physiological law:
- **Dense, Wet Crater-Basin Air:** Median surface pressure across League dry land is **`1.85 atm`** (`1.59–2.00 atm`; `1.88 atm` in `Hookfleet`), median temperature is **`32.0 °C`**, and median rainfall is **`1,804 mm/yr`** (`2,635 mm/yr` in `Hookfleet`).
- **99.1% Humid Köppen Classes:** `XA` hyper-tropical (`38.6%` League, `50.0%` Hookfleet), `Aw` wet-dry tropical (`19.7%`), `Af` rainforest (`18.2%` League, `44.7%` Hookfleet), `XV` humid seasonal littoral (`13.2%`), `Cfa` humid karst benches (`5.0%`), and `Am` (`4.2%`). Only **`0.9%`** (`428 km²`) of League dry land touches `BSh` steppe along the western Scyvoi border — an arid wall the Hykkousoi cannot colonize without gill-wither, leaving the `BSh` interior to their ancient theropod-riding enemies, the **Scyvoi (*Borunsa*)**, and the `XS`/`BSh` Cut trough to Izani **Dilihan**.

**Economic Consequences Validated by `TRADE-SCOPE.md` & `TRADE-GOODS.md`:**
1. **Scrap-to-Iron vs Mined Bronze:** Because mainland `Ys` (`-37 t` baseline iron deficit), `Trigon` (`-20 t`), and `Hookfleet` (`-60 t` in Gemini) lack iron and coal, **`Tethis` (`208 t/yr` arcology `scrap`, still `-14 t` net iron)** acts as the League's heavy salvage forge, burning charcoal and `Nereus` lake-coal (`756,820`), while Hykkousoi armourers, shipwrights, and sculptors favour **bronze** (`copper` from `Kierson`/`Pontus`/`Hookfleet` + `tin` imported from `Dilihan`, `Eternal State`, and `Ardent`/`Tungang` bronze ingots).
2. **Grown Mollusc Architecture (`shells` + `nacre` + `pearls`):** As *"the least electrical people on the Ring Sea; their biotech is grown"* (`LORE.md` §6.16), the Hykkousoi cultivate giant reef-oysters, barnacles, and nacre-depositing molluscs around drowned Ancient towers (`Ys`, and the Hykkousoi barnacle-pod quarter of `Zey'danin`). Across **`5,856 km²` of warm shallow shelf (`<= 35 m`)**, pearl diving (`hyk-pearl` dories) yields three linked outputs (`settlements/isle-demo/PLAN.md` ratio): **`1` unit `pearls` (`kg`) : `1` unit `nacre` (`t`, furniture/frieze inlay & grown-shell plates) : `4` units `shells` (`t`, burned for hydraulic sea-lime and village upkeep)**.
3. **Pontus & Volcanic Isle Sulfur Monopoly:** `Pontus` (`Kyra`, `Skopelos`, and `690,820` mainland vents) + `Kierson` hold **`1,347.5` `goodYield` of fumarolic `sulfur`** (`81%` of the League total), making the Hykkousoi the indispensable sulfur suppliers to `Porta Marr`/`Iziz`, the `Iron Republic`, and `Voth` (`TRADE-GEMINI.md`: `Pontus` #1 sulfur exporter at `1,837.5 t/yr`).

1. **Izani Exclave Transit Across League Waters (`Dilihan` & `Shell` — cf. `PROPOSAL-IZIZ-EXCLAVES.md`):**

Archon Jathrocles **permits and taxes this transit — while keeping Izani troops strictly on shipboard probation** lest Iziz try to turn alliance into annexation (`LORE.md:713`).
   - **The `Port Goto` (`Shell Province`) Sea-Lane (`103 km` NE of `Kierson`):** Izani munitions & `ship-timber` convoys (`Port Goto <-> Porta Marr`, `634 km`) skirt `Boiling Bay` (`866,723` pirates) and `Kierson`/`Nephtos`, buying League pilotage and water while losing stragglers to `Boiling Bay` raiders.
2. **The Iron Republic & Eternal State Lowland Portal (`Roketstad -> Trigon -> Tethis`):**
   - `Tethis` and `Trigon` form the **#1 maritime outlet for the NW interior**: Republican `firearms`, `gunpowder`, `scotch`, and `peat`, Yusar `scrap`/`cybernetics`, and `Farport` exterior luxuries (`frost-coral`, `ria-amber`) descend the kept `283 km` `Roketstad -> Trigon` trunk road to `Tethis` for shipment to `Porta Marr`, `Ys`, and `Hookfleet`, balanced by northward convoys of `Pontus`/`Tethis` **`sulfur` (`2,203 t` Republican import!)**, `Ys` **`pearls`**, `Nephtos`/`Ybera` **`sugar`**, and `Dilihan`/`Shell` **`saltpeter`**.

   - Scyvoi Baelu `B4` (`40.5 km` W of `Trigon`), `B5` (`68.3 km` N), and `B3` (`83.2 km` NW of `Pontus`) raid unescorted caravans along the western foothills (`Scyvoi Lowland Raids` hazard in `TRADE-GEMINI.md`), while trading `mounts` and dryland `incense` at `Petraia` and `Theion` in truce months. Further south, Vothic **`Drum` (`13,500` souls)** siphons southern Scyvoi ruin-scrap into its Under-Drum Salvage Bourse (`drum_stage1_blueprints.md`), sharpening `Tethis`'s dependence on local arcology ribs and `Nereus` iron.
