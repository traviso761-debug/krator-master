## 1. Executive Summary & Grounded Census vs `TRADE-GEMINI.md`

Across Krator's northern arc from the Farside tsunami escarpment (`X=327`) to the Outland Gate shoulder (`X=1154`), three distinct ecological and cultural regimes interlock:

1. **The Khaayken (`Yeti Kingdom`, `Khaay Qantïr`):** A post-human cold-adapted hominid kingdom (`1.15×` human height, heavy shaggy pelt) centered on **Cold Lake** (`lbl=76`, `448 km²` at `+5,680 m`) and **Qoold Lheek** (`410,506`, `+6,030 m`, `0.75 atm`), practicing seasonal vertical transhumance between permanent pycrete/ice-shell fellfield domes (`>4,500 m`) and Sakha-style larch `balagan`/`urasa` glens (`3,000–4,500 m`), holding the ruined-arcology pass at **Leng** (`490,554`, `+4,485 m`), raiding or extorting "winter gifts" from Eternal State borderlands (`yt_winter_fort`, trophy Eternian Heir stone heads), and trading compact alpine goods (`qiviut`, `goldroot`/`lung-salt`, `ivan-chai`, `silverthorn`, birch `pitch-tar`, `relics`) east to Jimjam over the **`Qoold Lheek <-> Leng <-> Veladiga <-> Jimjam` northern dirt road** (`891–924 km`, **zero Eternian cells, zero bulk log transport**).
2. **The Sasquatch Tribes:** Larger, reclusive subpolar hominid cousins (`1.35×` human height, `~2.30–2.42 m`, heavy double winter coat) ranging across four glacially carved `NW outer wall` troughs (`Frost-Jaw`, `Bone-Larch`, `Hoar-Wind`, `Scree-Tooth`, `+2,500 m` north/south valley mouths up to `+6,538 m` median fellfields). Completely lacking rock salt (`halite GoodY = 0.0`), copper (`0.0`), and iron (`0.0` in `rmvyj1kvhx4m4`), the Sasquatch descend on equinox truces to **Skywatch** (`776,470`) and **Serai** (`826,478`) to barter `qiviut`, prime furs, birch `pitch-tar`, `goldroot`, and north-rim stream-placer `gold`/`silver`/`tin`/`amber` (`rmwgv3ot4jv87`) for block salt, forged iron blades/kettles, and emergency winter grain.
3. **The N/NE Semiarid *Matok* Settler Khans, `Boatmurder` Highwaymen, `Serai` & `NE Outer Wall` Pockets:** The `142,548 km²` net northern semiarid steppe and breached-dam floor (`rmuqm5qn5ek5g`, `+959 m` median, `1.42 atm`, `BSh/Csa`) drained by *Matok*-named (`argot`) rivers (`Gete Rivar`, `Ankra Rivar`, `Roda Rivar`, `Pepa Rivar`, `Raida Kriki`), bounded on the northeast by `24,332 km²` of `<4,500 m` `NE outer wall` skarn/cauldron pockets (`rmvymakpc0pft`). Under the owner's locked Batch-1 ruling, a **tentative, poorly maintained Imperial highway climbs `541 km` due north from `Shell` (`902,659`) to `Silkhead` (`932,425` / `934,424`)**, feeding directly into the **`Silkhead <-> Serai (826,478) <-> Boatmurder (766,404) <-> Jimjam (745,546)`** northern overland and Soda-Lake (`lbl=54`) barge corridor—making **Serai** (`3,500`) the indispensable Soda-Delta caravanserai and **Boatmurder** (`1,950` incl. mobile `KCAMP.outlawCamp` bands) the premier ship-breaker stronghold and highwayman syndicate preying on silk, potash, metal, and almanac caravans.
