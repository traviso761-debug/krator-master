## 1.2 Hydrology, 39-Family Geology & `ehighlands` Harvest Ecology

### Hydrology Without Rivers
In `rivers.json`, **zero rivers cross Tiguantin's interior plateau**: all five nearby perennial rivers (`Níłch'i Baatar` `r099`, `Dootł'izh Baatar` `r103`, `Ch'il Baatar` `r325`, `Tł'aai Buruuk` `r326`, `Jóhonaa'éí Buruuk` `r362`, `Q = 28–46 m³/s`) head on the extreme western scarp lip (`X = 1087..1099`) and plunge immediately west into `Region 87`. Inside Tiguantin, water is governed by three perched mechanisms (`biomes/ehighlands/NOTES.md`):
1. **Carbonate-Paleosol Contact Springs:** Rain and snowmelt percolating through porous Lesser Throne `outland-tuff` perch on the baked paleosol and underlying carbonate beds, bursting out as cold perennial springs along the `3,200–3,600 m` bench rims (`Guari` `1095, 911` has `water` byte `mean 208, max 255` within 20 km).
2. **Mother-Cushion Peat Sponges & Flush Bogs:** Giant hummocks of **Mother Cushion** (*Azorella*-grade dense domes, `1–3 m` across) hold snowmelt like living cisterns, feeding year-round **cushion flush bogs** (`sphagnum`, `cottongrass`, `cairn-nettle`) in shallow `3,650–4,100 m` hollows.
3. **Closed Frost-Hollow Tarns & Fumarole Condensate:** Small meltwater tarns on the `T2` puna and warm acidic steam-condensate seeps around the `Lesser Throne` vent ring (`1130, 854`).
