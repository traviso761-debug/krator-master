# Proposal: The Ring Isles on the Sim Layer (Kingdom of Kautoa, Western Rumah Chiefdoms, Colonial Entrepôts & Freebooter Havens across the 5.1 Ring Sea)

This proposal scopes the **Ring Isles** (`Kautoa`, `East Ring Isles`, `West Ring isles`, and the `isle-demo` cluster across the Scale Model 5.1 `Ring Sea`) on the same regional-sim pattern established for Longsong (`PROPOSAL-LONGSONG-PINNACLE.md`) and the Xanadu Sultanate (`core/simulation/PROPOSAL-XANADU.md`). ## 1. Complete 5.1 Island & Archipelago Inventory of the Ring Sea

### 1.1 Basin Hydrography, Barometry & Connected Landmasses

On Scale Model 5.1 (`2048 × 2048`, `2 km/px = 4 km²/px`), the **Ring Sea** (`rmuql5r2ryukf`) is a continuous, warm, dense-air inland sea filling the depressed floor of the inner crater around the central **Throne** volcanic cone:

| Metric | 5.1 Value | Sim & Ecological Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Sea-surface elevation (`wlev`)** | **`-1,500.0 m`** | `~1,500 m` below global datum; barometric pressure at sea level is **`~1.93 atm`** (`P = exp(1500 / 8500)` is `~1.19×` datum, on top of Krator's dense crater column -> `CROPS` `air = 'dense'`). |
| **Continuous water area** | **`43,222 px = 172,888 km²`** | Spans `x = 688..1052` (`728 km` E–W) by `y = 698..1023` (`650 km` N–S); large enough for open-water swells, outrigger blue-water navigation, and multi-day sea lanes. |
| **Bathymetry (depth below `-1,500 m`)** | Median **`182 m`**, p90 **`567 m`**, max **`889 m`** (`min E = -2,389 m`) | Deep axial troughs isolate the Throne and outer island chains from mainland wading, while broad carbonate/volcanic aprons form shallow reef and pearl shelves. |
| **Shallow reef & lagoon shelf (`< 35 m` deep)** | **`6,408 km²`** around the `49` discrete islands (`9,180 km²` basin-wide) | Coral gardens, *makatea* benches, giant clam / nacre beds (Kautoan shell currency), `reef-teal` sea-slug dye grounds, and `salambao` / `oruwa` trap fisheries. |
| **Outer pearl & demersal shelf (`35–80 m` deep)** | **`3,048 km²`** around the `49` discrete islands | Deep *Pinctada* pearl-oyster banks worked by Kautoan divers and Hykkousoi `rs-hyk-pearl` diving baghlahs. |

Running an 8-connected dry-cell labeling (`scipy.ndimage.label`) across the entire Ring Sea basin yields **`51` dry components** touching Ring Sea water:

1. **Component `#1` — The Main Crater Continent (`10,981,164 km²` map-wide):** Bounds the Ring Sea on all outer shores (`Pontus`, `Almua`, `Hook`, `Porta Marr`, `Port Tobin`, `Port Goto`, `Ys`, `Tethis`) **and includes Kautoa's northern landmass (`4,364 km²` inside `rmvlpfnlxc6n5`)**, which on 5.1 is a rugged hyperjungle peninsula joined to the NE mainland rather than a detached island (see §1.3).
2. **Component `#91` — Main Throne Volcanic Island (`15,442 px = 61,768 km²`, center `836.6, 834.6`, peak `+12,394 m` above Ring Sea):** The central super-volcano holding `Zey'danin (795,860)`, `Temora (762,883)`, `Nereus (762,805)`, `Dhelv (840,776)`, `Ybera (898,787)`, `Noa's Regret (896,866)`, and **SE Throne Headland `#107` (`Port Mira`, `865,895`, `576 km²`)**, which touches `#91` diagonally by a single 1-px corner (an island under 4-connectivity, an isthmus/tidal-channel peninsula under 8-connectivity).
3. **Components `I-01` through `I-49` — The `49` Discrete Ring Sea Islands (`8,168 px = 32,672 km²`):** True offshore islands surrounded completely by Ring Sea water (`33` islands `≥ 96 km²` totaling `31,952 km²`, plus `16` minor islets of `16–80 km²` totaling `720 km²`).

**Physiographic & ecological structure across the `49` islands:**
1. **Two island archetypes (matching `biomes/ringisles/NOTES.md`):**
   - **High volcanic & plutonic cones (`I-01, I-03, I-05..I-12, I-15..I-23, I-25, I-27`; tops `+490` to `+1,414 m`, `29` active geyser/fumarole vents):** Steep basalt, andesite, and granite cores ringed by narrow *makatea* (uplifted coral-limestone benches `8–40 m` high) and pocket beaches.    - **Low sedimentary & carbonate-platform islands (`I-02, I-04, I-13, I-14, I-24, I-26, I-28..I-49`; tops `< +425 m`, `0` vents):** Broad, gently sloping shelves with wide `<35 m` barrier reefs and lagoons (`656 km²` around `I-02` alone, `436 km²` around `I-04`, `400 km²` around `I-13`). Hold **74% of the archipelago's coal seams**, **100% of its oil/bitumen seeps**, silica glass-sands, and the largest solar salt flats (`I-29`).
2. **Windward Hyperjungle vs Leeward Savanna & Salt-Pan Split:** Prevailing moist Ring Sea air drenches `92.0%` (`30,052 km²`) of island area in `XA`/`Af`/`Am` rainforest (`2,200–3,450 mm/yr`), clothing windward slopes in buttressed *tamanu*, *meranti*, *strangler fig*, and tree ferns (`biomes/ringisles`). Rain-shadow leeward pockets (`2,428 km²` total) appear strictly behind high cones or the Throne's eastern shadow — most dramatically on **`I-01` (`Valapo`, `620 km²` of `XV/BSh/Aw/XS`)**, **`I-06` (`620 km²` of `Aw/BSh/BWh`, rain down to `251 mm/yr`)**, **`I-07` (`340 km²` of `Aw/BWh/BSh/XS`)**, **`I-14` (`Tzinguard`, `388 km²` of `XV/Aw`)**, and **`I-29` (`224 km²` pure `XV/Aw/XS` at `848 mm/yr` in the Throne's lee)**.

## 5. Ring Sea Trade Lanes, Re-Export Hubs, Piracy & Monetary Regimes

### 5.1 Six Interlocking Maritime & Overland Corridors

The Ring Isles sit at the crossroads of five mainland/Throne civilizations, turning local island specialities into basin-wide trade relays:

1. **The South-Eastern Izani / `Porta Marr` Axis (`Porta Marr` `926,1014` & `Port Tobin` `956,944` <-> `P4 Seto / I-02` <-> `P2 Valapo` <-> `P1 Kautoa`):**
   - **`Porta Marr` (`~20,000`, `kits/marr`)** is the Empire's premier deep-water port and seat of the `Gilda de Navigadarz`. Its legitimate merchant houses in `Qartar de Gardonz` buy **Valapo bar-iron (`P2`), `I-02` steam/forge coal & `I-08` tin (`P4`), Kautoa soda-ash/glass/indigo ikat (`P1`), and `P3` clove/coffee**, shipping back Izani porcelains, machinery, silk, grain, and **Talents**. Meanwhile, Marr's `De Xedaz` canal smugglers quietly fence untaxed **Ranjrum** and captured cargoes from `P8` (`Noah's Regret` & `Shanty`).
2. **The South-Western Vothic Corridor (`Almua` `706,900` [feeding `Drum` `13,500` & `Torus`] <-> `P7 Kautono & Tzinguard` <-> `Temora / Zey'danin`):**
   - With **`Hook` (`809,923`) lost to Iziz**, Voth's sea bridge to its Throne colonies (`Zey'danin` & `Temora`) must thread through **`P7`** (`I-27 -> Kautono -> Tzinguard -> I-25`). **`Drum`** (`13,500` souls: Military Governor's garrison, Ordinator Bastion, Under-Drum Salvage Bourse) and **`Torus`** (`774,1007`) exert heavy mainland demand for **`P7` (`I-13/I-14`) coal**, **`Kautono` copper & abacá cordage**, and **cane sugar, coffee, clove, and kava**, paying in Vothic salvaged **scrap, ancient-alloys, woollens, and beer/grain** shipped out of `Almua`.
3. **The Newly Izani `Hook` Wedge (`Hook` `809,923` <-> `I-02` [`P4`] <-> `Porta Marr` & `Hook` <-> `I-18` [`P7`]):**
   - Under Vinzenda IX, Izani `Hook` connects inward by trunk roads (`net51-gemini.json`) to `Torus` (`199 km`), `Spirladunon` (`196 km`), and `Pylon` (`208 km`), while seaward it relies on **`I-02` (`P4`)** and **`Porta Marr`** convoys (`rs-iziz-dhoni` + `rs-iziz-patrol`) running past hostile `Hookfleet` privateers at `Port Mira` / `I-26` (`P8`).
4. **The Northern League Colonial Triangle (`Tethis` `736,753` & `Ys` `823,711` <-> `P6 Taikoro / I-04` <-> `P5 Kierson & Nephtos` <-> `P1 Kautoa`):**
   - Tethyan `corbita` coasters haul **`Nephtos` (`I-09`) copper, ship-hulls, coffee, cane sugar, and clove** west along the northern island chain (`I-21 -> Kierson -> I-22 -> I-04 -> I-23`), while **Ys** trades books, fine cloth, wine, and Hetānini royal patronage into `Kierson` (`P5`) and `Taikoro` (`P6`) for pearls, spices, and coal. In the west channel, **`Kyra` & `Skopelos` (`P6`)** bridge `Pontus` (`689,839`) to the northern sea.
5. **The Throne Alchemy & *Ranjrum* Loop (`Dhelv / Ybera / Šadū-Qarnu` <-> `Noah's Regret` [`P8`], `Kautoa` [`P1`], & `Zey'danin`):**
   - Zeijani and Andaru pack trains bring highland **Ranj**, sulfur, alum, and rock salt down to `Ybera` and `Noah's Regret`, where Throne Ranj meets Ring Isles **cane molasses (`P2/P7`)** to distil **Ranjrum** (`TRADE-SCOPE.md` §8.3), distributed across every tavern from `De Xedaz` in Marr to `The Cauldron` arena in Drum.
6. **The Beast-Rider Overland & Strait Bridge (`Pinnacle Rock` `1008,686` & `Talon / Mav's Refuge` <-> `Kautoa Ridertown` [`P1`] & `Bipo` [`P2`]):**
   - `4.0` pack/raptor caravans (`275 t/yr` in Gemini) descend the `166 km` NE jungle trail from `Pinnacle Rock` into Kautoa's `Ridertown`, trading inland **Ancient scrap, alloys, silk, and beast hides/mounts** for **Kautoan solar salt, glass, iron tools, and sugar**, supplemented by `rs-beast-barge` crossings between `Crescent` and `Bipo` (`I-06`).

### 5.2 Piracy, Convoy Escorts & Contraband Mechanics on the Sim

Rather than treating pirates as a static percentage tax, the regional sim can represent **`P8` (`Noah's Regret` + `Hookfleet` at `Port Mira`/`I-26`)** cleanly on existing `SIM.trade` primitives plus one lightweight interception hook:
- **Unescorted vs Convoyed Lanes:** Domestic `lakatoi` inside `P1–P2` sheltered waters sail unmolested; long-haul lanes crossing the central/southern Ring Sea (`Porta Marr <-> Hook`, `Nephtos <-> Tethis`, `Valapo <-> Marr`) face a per-passage interception roll proportional to active `lanong` / `Hookfleet` raider hulls at `P8` minus escort hulls (`rs-iziz-patrol`, `rs-isl-karakoa`, `rs-hyk-dromon`).
- **Prize Fencing (`Shanty` & `Porta Marr`):** Intercepted loads are diverted to `Noah's Regret` or `I-26` (`Hookfleet`), consumed locally (`food`, `salt` from `I-29`, `cordage`, `pitch-tar` for hull upkeep) or re-exported at a **discount (`0.65×` par)** via `rs-voth-sloop` smugglers calling at **`Shanty` (`I-31`)**, **`I-18` (`P7`)**, and **`Porta Marr`'s `De Xedaz`** docks!

### 5.3 Monetary Regimes: Kautoan Shell Currency, *Hikau* Strings, Drachmae & Talents

- **Kingdom of Kautoa (`P1–P4`) — Royal Nacre Shell Money (`1 Talent = 20 Shells`, `1 Shell = 0.05 T`):** Carved, polished, and stamped from giant-clam and pearl-oyster nacre harvested on `P1–P3`'s `<35 m` coral shelves (`ie-shell-mint` in Kautoa) under strict royal monopoly (`LORE.md`). Domestic wages, hamlet tithes, kava-house tabs, and Valapo iron purchases settle in Shells; Kautoa's **Harbour Exchange (`Izanitown` & `ie-customs`)** converts foreign **Izani Talents** and **Hykkousoi Drachmae** at the royal peg.
- **Western *Hikau* Chiefdoms (`P6–P7`):** Use un-stamped *Hikau* shell-bead strings (`fae`/`kula`-style prestige strings for bridewealth and `drua` charters) alongside direct commodity barter and Vothic/League coin in `Kautono` and `Taikoro`.
- **Colonial & Mainland Gateways (`Nephtos`, `Kierson`, `Tzinguard`, `Porta Marr`, `Hook`):** Price wholesale manifests in **Izani Talents** (the universal `core/simulation` unit of account; `Tethyan silver drachmae` at `Nephtos`/`Kierson` clear at fixed bullion parity).
