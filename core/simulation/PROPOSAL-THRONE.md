### 2.2 Endemic Ecology: Wild `Ranj`, Failed Plantations, Dark-Bed Fungi & `CROPS.at`

1. **The `Ranj` Paradox (`throne.spice` / `RHEO_PHYS.ranjViability`):**
   - **Chemistry (`biomes/rheolith/PLAN.md`, `TRADE-SCOPE.md` §7.1):** Wild **Ranj** (reddish-amber fumarolic shrub resin — the Ring Sea's premier surgical **wound-knitting coagulant**, infection-blocker, combat stimulant, Zeijani temple incense, and spiced liqueur base) fruits **only within `<85 m` of active `180–550 °C` rheomorphic/fumarolic throats** emitting cyclic siloxanes (`D3/D4`) and volatile dimethyl selenide `(CH3)2Se` (`soilTemp 24–48 °C`, `acidLoad < 0.72`). Away from active vent gas, cuttings grow woody and sterile within two seasons.
   - **Why `Zey'danin` (`795, 860`) failed & pivoted (`settlements/zeydanin/PLAN.md` §1, `TRADE-SCOPE.md` §8.2):** Voth conquered Hetānini's capital expecting plantation Ranj monopolies, but the SW shield has **zero radial rift throats** and `2,447 mm/yr` of monsoon rain that leaches selenium/siloxanes out of the laterite. After the Ranj seedlings went barren, Vothic factors converted the walled estates to **`transplanted` `coffee` and `sugar-cane`** (`TRADE-SCOPE.md` §8.2), while strong-arming wild Ranj down from upland tappers (`S3`/`S4`). *(Data note for `kits/crops`: `coffee` and `sugar-cane` carry `warmth: [3, 4]`, so at raw `-1,180 m` `+32 °C` (`warmth 5`) `CROPS.at` rejects them unless grown on `+100..+600 m` terraced benches (`warmth 4`) or flagged `transplanted` — matching Zey'danin's upland estate terraces.)*
   - **Where wild & semi-tended Ranj actually yields:** (a) **E/SE Radial Rift Throats (`S4`, `Stn 8` & `Breached Throat` `884, 839..842`)** harvested by Andarū ash-nomads and Zeijani tube-foragers; (b) **NNE Blast-Scar & Fissure Aprons (`S1`, `Stn 13` `858, 799` & `Nūru-Šaplu`)**; (c) **W/SW Ring-Fault Geyser Domes (`S3` `Stn 4` `762, 827` & `Temora` `762, 883`)**, where small fractured geothermal seeps let independent tappers and `Temora` factors gather modest wild crops; and (d) **NW Steampits Sinter Collars (`S6`, `Boiling Bay` / `Stn 7`)**, where Hykkousoi outcasts pack crushed zeolite vent-sinter mulch (`throatcollar`) around shrubs for low-grade contraband resin.
2. **Dark-Bed Lava-Tube Fungiculture (`works: "dark-beds"` / `"sealed-beds"`):**

### 5.1 How a Ship-Less Mountain Trades: The Four Coastal Valves

Because **Zeijani (`38,000`) and Andarū (`9,500`) operate zero ocean-going hulls**, every tonne leaving or entering the interior passes through four coastal valves whose politics shape the Ring Sea:
