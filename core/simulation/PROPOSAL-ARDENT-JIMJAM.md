### Why These Two Regions Form One Integrated Simulation System
1. **Complementary Resource Lock:** `Jimjam` holds **`30,292 km²` of `farm-eq`**, **`4,768 km²` of `clay=255`** slackwater brick-silt, **`gypsum`/`alabaster` (`#1` export)**, **`saltpeter`**, and **inland lake navigation** (`43,408 km²` across Great Trough Lake `lbl=77` and East Dam Soda Lake `lbl=54`), yet lacks heavy highland timber (`FC1`–`FC3`), cold-climate livestock/mounts, and rich native `tin` right at the capital. Conversely, `Ardent` commands **`copper` + `tin` -> `bronze`**, **`iron` (`1,479.5` goodY)**, highland **`mounts`**, **`wool`**, **`peat`**, and **`scotch`**, but relies on `Jimjam` for winter grain buffer, `salt`, `alabaster`, `glass`, and **almanacs**.
2. **Northern Bypass Around the Locked `14,000–28,750` `Hexahedron` Screamer Swarm:** With `Hexahedron` (`817,670`, `-1,243 m`, `1.87 atm`) now locked at the higher deep-vault swarm (`14,000–28,750` souls, `PROPOSAL-RAIDING-FACTIONS.md`), the hyperjungle floor south of `Free Clans` is an extreme-attrition kill-zone. East-west caravan traffic between the northeastern rim (`Shell`, `Korona`, `Silkhead`) and the northwestern interior (`Jimjam`, `The Cut`, `Iron Republic`, `Eternal State`) **must** climb onto the high benches of `Ardent` (`+2,802 m`) and `Serai` (`-27 m` / `Dam 2`) to connect with the **newly locked tentative `Shell -> Silkhead` Imperial Highway** (`X ≈ 902..934`).
