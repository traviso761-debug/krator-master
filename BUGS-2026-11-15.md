# Bugs found and not fixed, 2026-11-11 to 11-15

The owner's second survey (2026-11-15) asked 34 sessions for their pitfalls, tips and **the bugs they found and did not fix**;
the coordinator added two findings of its own. This file is every such bug, deduplicated across sessions and grouped by module
(core first, then tools, kits, settlements and biomes, then characters, fauna, audio and the gallery), followed by state the
sessions left owed. The lessons are the skill's story 5x (`painting-to-3d-world.skill`, `references/stories-rounds.md`) and
the check requests CORE-IMPROVEMENTS.md items 33-52.

Each entry: where (file:line when known), the symptom, how to reproduce it, who reported it (with a count), the severity
(**breaks a page**, **wrong result**, **cosmetic**, **tooling/false alarm**) and the reach (one build, or many pages: then it
rides along with the next all-builds rebuild, TODO.md "Ride along"). Lines are as of origin/main 930e5c920 (2026-11-12) where
checked; others are as the session gave them. Session names are the survey's (`<topic>`, with its branch where it matters).

**Checked against origin/main before listing.** Several sessions reported pages broken "on main" that origin/main had already
fixed; their branches were cut from 2009202c8, 0908aee8d or a local `main` (b446e8593) that origin/main had passed. Those are
listed under "Reported broken, already fixed on origin/main" at the end of the core section, not as open bugs.

**Size.** 85 open bugs, by their first severity: 16 break a page, 24 give a wrong result, 17 are cosmetic, 28 are tooling or
false alarms. Five more were reported broken and are already fixed on origin/main (C25).
