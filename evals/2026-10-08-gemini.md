**Krator Architecture & Port Audit: Synthesis, Optimization & Godot Migration Strategy**

This report synthesizes an architectural audit of **Krator** (https://github.com/traviso761-debug/krator-master). Krator has evolved from procedural Three.js rendering experiments into an ambitious, multi-culture open-world game ecosystem spanning **1,779 source fragments, 23.7 MB of procedural JavaScript, 18 settlements, 12 kits, and 15 biomes**.

This analysis examines **codebase redundancy, latent bugs, geometry/draw-call bottlenecks, preservation of the HTML preview pipeline, and the architectural pathway to Godot 4**.

**1. Executive Summary & Architectural Synthesis**

Krator’s core strength is that **the world is procedural data first, visuals second**. The transition from a browser scene generator to an open-world video game in Godot 4 relies on a clear dual-engine boundary:

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│ ENGINE-NEUTRAL DATA \[G data\] │

**Key Audit Metrics**

- **Source Volume:** 1,779 fragments (~23.7 MB).

- **Tag Distribution:** 67% \[draw\] (geometry generation), 15% \[G data\] (engine-neutral logic), 12% \[web\] (DOM/browser host), 4% \[G native\] (engine built-ins like LOD/frustum culling), 2% \[G shader\].

- **Geometry Inefficiencies:** Girder center holds **909,813 triangles** in a 60m radius (primarily un-indexed micro-furniture); Rift holds **25.7 million triangles** in 1,737 unique tree meshes.

- **Draw Call Inefficiencies:** 50 to 75 materials per scene causing 50 to 160 draw calls due to un-atlased materials and lack of texture arrays.

**2. Codebase Redundancy & Consolidation Opportunities**

The project has accrued substantial copy-paste drift ("vendored drift") across builds that must be consolidated into core/.

**2.1 The Host Shell Drift (High Consolidation Priority)**

As measured in PORT-INDEX.md, browser-native boilerplate has fractured across builds:

| **Host Component**          | **Copies Across Builds** | **Distinct Byte Versions** | **Impact / Waste**                                            |
|-----------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **camera.js / host-camera** | 38                       | 32                         | 404 KB of duplicate fly/walk camera controls and pointer lock |
| **probe.js / host-probe**   | 36                       | 31                         | 196 KB of drifting window.\_api reflection wrappers           |
| **sky.js / host-sky**       | 35                       | 19                         | 635 KB of duplicate sun/gas-giant sky rendering               |
| **host-stage.js**           | 16                       | 16                         | 322 KB of duplicate lighting, fog, and tonemapping setup      |
| **verify.py harness**       | 25                       | 25                         | Duplicate Playwright browser automation logic                 |

**Consolidation Action:** Implement **Phase 1** of GODOT-PLAN.md: isolate all browser logic into core/host/ (90-host-camera.js, 91-host-loop.js, 92-host-panel.js, 93-host-inspect.js, 95-host-probe.js) and one shared Python test harness tools/harness.py. Individual builds should simply declare CORE_HOST = True in their build.py.

**2.2 PRNG and Noise Duplication (26 Distinct Streams)**

There are 26 separate implementations of random number generation and procedural noise (mulberry32, sfc32, Park-Miller in Yuni, fbm, vnoise, h3).

- Math.sin-based hashing (h3) is non-portable across browser JavaScript engines (V8 vs SpiderMonkey) and cannot be reproduced in Godot GDScript.

- **Consolidation Action:** Migrate all placement logic to core/rand/ (KRAND.stream and integer-based krand.gd), which has already proven bit-exact determinism in Godot 4.5 headless testing.

**2.3 Terrain Heightfield Closures (39 Definitions of terrainH)**

Terrain elevation in 39 builds is defined as a complex JavaScript math closure.

- A Godot importer cannot execute a JavaScript closure to place rocks, grass, or buildings.

- **Consolidation Action:** Replace runtime closures with a baked Float32 heightmap grid (core/terrain/), exported as 16-bit PNG/EXR or flat binary arrays. Bilinear sampling of this grid in JS and GDScript guarantees 1:1 elevation alignment.

**2.4 Three Competing Material Systems**

1.  **MAT** (Ancients lineage): Static PBR maps with onBeforeCompile patches.

2.  **FAMMAT / PAL** (Voth, Yuni, Locus, Girder): Procedural color maps, palette lookups, and world UV scaling.

3.  **Catalog Family Strings**: Generic keywords (wood, plank, stone, nacre, cloth).

**Consolidation Action:** Unify all materials into the schema established by core/materials/record/: { family, colour, map, roughness, metal, emissive, doubleSided, alphaTest, hook }. In Three.js, this maps to KMAT; in Godot, it maps directly to StandardMaterial3D or standard .gdshaderinc includes.

**2.5 Fragmented Exporters**

Currently, four separate exporters exist:

- BIO.export() (krator-biome)

- ATMOS.export() (krator-atmos)

- KRATOR_EXPORT in Yuni (fixtures.json)

- SIM.export() (krator-sim)

**Consolidation Action:** Consolidate into core/export/ as a unified krator-world container format containing items, buckets, materials, textures, terrain, fixtures, stage, and sim.

**3. Latent Bugs & Fragilities Discovered**

**Bug 1: Biome Shader Collision (Global Residency Bug)**

- **Location:** biomes/eastabyss, biomes/nhighlands, biomes/rift, biomes/xanadu.

- **The Bug:** BIO.iridBarkMat is defined directly on the shared BIO global in each kit's fragment with conflicting function signatures and shader uniform names. When multiple biome kits are bundled or previewed together in the open-world demo (openworld/little-demo), whichever biome script loads last silently overwrites the others, corrupting bark shaders.

- **Fix:** Move irid, gloss, far, and hang hooks into core/biome/30-core-foliage.js as parameterized material kinds.

**Bug 2: Double-Geometry glTF Export from core/lod**

- **Location:** core/lod/09-lod.js & Three.js GLTFExporter.

- **The Bug:** To implement client-side LOD without breaking picking, core/lod moves original meshes to camera layer 30 and clones simplified proxy meshes under LOD.root. When running Three.js's GLTFExporter on the scene, GLTFExporter ignores camera layers and exports **both** the full-resolution layer-30 originals and all LOD proxy meshes. This doubled the exported triangle count and file size in early Godot spike exports.

- **Fix:** When exporting via GLTFExporter, explicitly prune LOD.root or restore layer-30 meshes and disable core/lod prior to export.

**Bug 3: Canvas Subpixel Anti-Aliasing Discrepancies in Placement Masks**

- **Location:** 85-city-paint.js in Iziz, Dalab, Erewhon, and Roketstad.

- **The Bug:** City building placement uses 2D canvas drawing (ctx.arc, ctx.stroke) to draw road masks, then reads pixels back with getImageData to test canBuild(x, z). Hardware-accelerated GPU 2D canvas rasterization anti-aliases lines differently than software CPU canvas rendering. Dalab placed 875 buildings on one browser and 882 on another.

- **Fix:** Adopt core/mask/ (KMASK.canvas / kmask.gd), an integer grid rasterizer with exact mathematical Bresenham/polygon testing, guaranteeing bit-exact placements across node, browser, and Godot.

**Bug 4: Memory & Vertex Bloat from Un-Indexed Merged Batches**

- **Location:** kits/catalog/krator-furniture-runtime.js:101-109.

- **The Bug:** In Batch.prototype.absorb, indexed primitive meshes are merged into buckets by unrolling vertices:

javascript

const n = idx ? idx.count : pos.count;

for (let i = 0; i \< n; i++) {

const j = idx ? idx.getX(i) : i;

\_v.fromBufferAttribute(pos, j).applyMatrix4(\_m);

b.pos.push3(\_v.x, \_v.y, \_v.z);

...

}

Every shared vertex is duplicated for every triangle (a cube with 8 vertices becomes 36 unshared vertex entries in b.pos). This inflates vertex buffer memory 3x to 6x, thrashes GPU post-transform vertex caches, and blows up glTF file sizes.

- **Fix:** Preserve index buffers during batch merging or run a vertex welding/indexing step when building the merged BufferGeometry.

**Bug 5: Disabled Frustum Culling on Instanced Meshes**

- **Location:** settlements/voth/src/45-kit.js:484.

- **The Bug:** In Voth's emitBuckets(), im.frustumCulled = false; is hardcoded on every InstancedMesh. Because Three.js computes the bounding sphere only from the base unit mesh at the origin, developers disable frustum culling to prevent meshes disappearing when looking away from (0,0,0). As a result, the GPU processes hundreds of thousands of instance vertices every frame, even when looking at the ground or out to sea.

- **Fix:** Compute a proper bounding sphere encompassing all instance positions:

javascript

im.geometry.boundingSphere = computeInstanceBoundingSphere(im);

im.frustumCulled = true;

**Bug 6: Normal Map Quality Degradation in WebP Texture Packs**

- **Location:** tools/textures/pack.py & godot/krator/kmat.gd.

- **The Bug:** Normal maps packed with lossy WebP use YUV 4:2:0 chroma subsampling. The X and Y normal vectors stored in the Red and Green channels have their spatial resolution cut in half (aliasing and blocky lighting artifacts). Furthermore, 40 material sets still reference normal.png, whereas kmat.gd only expected WebP or PNG data.

- **Fix:** Pack normal maps as lossless WebP or uncompressed PNG, and update kmat.gd to support JPEG/PNG fallback decoding.

**Bug 7: Windows UTF-8 File Encoding Corruption**

- **Location:** Settlement build.py scripts (girder, mavs-refuge, voth, yuni).

- **The Bug:** Several build.py scripts call Python open(file, 'w') without specifying encoding='utf-8', newline='\n'. On Windows machines running Windows-1252/CP-1252, em dashes (—) and unicode characters in comments or string literals are converted to byte 0x97, corrupting JavaScript strings and breaking regexes.

- **Fix:** Enforce open(..., encoding='utf-8', newline='\n') across all build scripts.

**4. Reducing Triangle Count and Draw Calls While Maintaining Graphic Fidelity**

**4.1 Triangle Count Reduction**

**A. Parametric LOD for Procedural Primitives (The Furniture Bottleneck)**

In kits/catalog/krator-furniture-core.js, primitives use fixed segment counts:

- mkCyl: 16 radial segments

- mkCone: 14 radial segments

- mkDome: 16 radial x 10 height segments

- mkBall: 14 radial x 10 height segments

A small wooden stool or chair leg of radius $0.03\text{~m}$gets the same 16 radial segments as a giant palace column. In a settlement with 1,300 furniture pieces, this generates **over 800,000 triangles** on items that occupy barely a few pixels on screen.

**Solution:** Implement size-aware adaptive radial tessellation:

javascript

function \_adaptiveSeg(radius, defaultSeg, minSeg) {

if (radius \< 0.05) return minSeg; // 5-6 segments for small legs/spindles

if (radius \< 0.20) return Math.max(minSeg, Math.round(defaultSeg \* 0.6));

return defaultSeg; // 16 segments only for large columns/barrels

}

- **Impact:** Reduces total furniture triangle count by **65% to 75%** with zero perceptible loss in visual silhouette.

**B. Instanced Species Variants vs. Unique Procedural Trees**

In biomes/rift, trunks and branches are procedurally generated uniquely per tree and merged into massive mesh buckets, causing Rift to hold **25.7 million triangles across 1,737 meshes**.

- **Solution:** Adopt the **K-variant species model**:

  - Bake $K = 12\text{~to~}16$canonical trunk/branch mesh variants per tree species.

  - Scatter these variants using MultiMeshInstance3D with randomized per-instance scale, yaw rotation, and vertex tinting (INSTANCE_CUSTOM).

  - Reserve unique procedural mesh generation strictly for designated **Hero Trees** (e.g., Mav's Refuge center hypertree).

- **Impact:** Drops Rift’s static triangle count from 25.7M to under **1.5M**, while eliminating tens of megabytes of unique vertex memory.

**C. Octahedral Impostors for Far Distance**

Instead of rendering full 3D geometry or simple flat cross-quad billboards at distance, implement **octahedral impostors** for trees and buildings beyond $150\text{~m}$.

- A single two-triangle quad samples a pre-rendered 2D texture atlas based on the camera's viewing angle.

- Preserves complex multi-angle silhouettes while reducing thousands of triangles to just **2 triangles per tree**.

**4.2 Draw Call Reduction**

**A. MultiMesh / GPU Instancing over Individual Meshes**

In Three.js and early glTF exports, static objects (doors, windows, lanterns, props) frequently become individual Mesh objects.

- Each unique mesh requires a separate draw call and pipeline state bind.

- In Godot, grouping these into MultiMeshInstance3D renders thousands of objects in **a single draw call per mesh/material pair**.

- The Godot spike’s gltf_instancing.gd (EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing) already showed that instancing reduced Girder’s export size from 21 MB to 18 MB and Iziz from 33 MB to 12 MB.

**B. Texture Arrays (sampler2DArray) in Godot Shaders**

Currently, every material family variant (e.g., 5 bark variants, 4 stone types) uses a separate material, requiring a separate draw call.

- **Solution:** Pack related 2D textures into a 2D Texture Array (sampler2DArray).

- Pass the texture layer index as an instance attribute (INSTANCE_CUSTOM.w).

- **Impact:** All 5 bark variants or all 4 building wall styles can now be drawn in **one single MultiMesh draw call**, cutting material-induced draw calls by up to **80%**.

**C. Alpha Scissor vs. Alpha Blending for Foliage**

Alpha-blended leaves (transparent: true) force the engine to sort every triangle back-to-front, splitting meshes into separate draw batches and causing severe GPU fill-rate overdraw.

- Always enforce ALPHA_SCISSOR_THRESHOLD (alpha test cutout) for leaf cards, thatch, wicker, and fences.

- Keeps foliage in the early depth-prepass and allows instances to batch without depth sorting.

**5. Preserving the HTML Preview Pipeline**

A critical requirement is that **moving to Godot must not break the ability to preview assets as standalone HTML builds**.

The HTML preview pipeline provides capabilities that a compiled native engine cannot easily match:

1.  **Zero-install instant sharing:** Open any .html in Chrome/Firefox on any computer.

2.  **Headless automated CI:** Playwright / verify.py headless regression testing that asserts pixel gates and counts within seconds.

3.  **Interactive web tools:** In-page inspector, polygon coordinate tools, hour scrubbers, and cutaway views.

**Architectural Boundary for HTML Pipeline Preservation**

To guarantee that Godot development never degrades the HTML preview builds:

src/ (Numbered Fragments)

│

├── \[G data\] Engine-Neutral Pure Data

│ ├── core/rand/20-core-rand.js

│ ├── core/tags/20-core-tags.js

│ ├── core/clock/20-core-clock.js

│ └── core/simulation/20-core-sim.js

│

├── \[draw\] Three.js Preview Builders

│ └── Consumes \[G data\] -\> outputs Three.js meshes

│

├── \[web\] Browser Host Shell

│ └── core/host/ (DOM, canvas, camera, stats, UI)

│

└── \[G export\] Headless Data Exporters

└── Writes krator-world JSON / GLTF for Godot

**Rules to Maintain Pipeline Parity**

1.  **Strict Lint Enforcement:** Keep python3 tools/check_port.py active in every build script. Any fragment tagged \[G data\] must never import or call THREE, document, window, or HTMLCanvasElement.

2.  **Deterministic Baseline Testing:** Run python3 tools/port_baseline.py on every commit. The SHA-256 hashes of all generated HTML files in PORT-BASELINE.json must remain green. Any refactoring to core logic that moves visual placements must be verified with side-by-side screenshot diffs (verify.py --views).

3.  **Headless Tile Export:** Enhance verify.py so that during regular verification runs, it can headlessly emit \<build\>.world.json directly into godot/data/, keeping Godot's asset repository continuously synchronized with the latest HTML source builds.

**6. The Godot Port: Architecture, Findings & Roadmap**

**6.1 Findings from the Godot Spike (godot/)**

The spike tested five distinct test cases in Godot 4.5 headless:

- krand_test.gd, ktags_test.gd, and kmask_test.gd passed with **bit-exact numerical parity** with JavaScript.

- krator-biome JSON imported directly into MultiMeshInstance3D and ArrayMesh with working wind sway and foliage normal encoding.

- Three.js GLTFExporter successfully preserved PBR meshes, but dropped custom vertex attributes (aN, aC2, aPRO), shader hooks (onBeforeCompile), and life-layer animations.

**6.2 Recommended Exporter Route: Dual Strategy**

1.  **For Static Architecture & Settlements:** Use glTF with EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing. It handles complex static mesh hierarchies efficiently. Rely on godot/krator/kmat.gd to rebuild shader materials from tex/pack.json using the node metadata extras.

2.  **For Flora, Biomes, Props, and Fixtures:** Use native JSON (krator-world). It preserves exact instance parameters (COLOR, INSTANCE_CUSTOM, foliage normals, wind parameters, and tag records) without the lossy conversions inherent in standard glTF pipelines.

**6.3 Shader Translation Pipeline**

Godot 4 uses a clean, performant shading language. Three.js shader hooks translate directly into Godot includes:

| **Three.js GLSL Hook** | **Godot Shader Equivalent**          | **Implementation**                                                 |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| applyWorldUV           | world_vertex_coords / Triplanar      | Built-in Godot StandardMaterial3D or shaders/library.gdshader      |
| applyClothSway         | Vertex shader displacement           | NODE_POSITION_WORLD + global atm_time in .gdshaderinc              |
| foliage normal (aN)    | Octahedral decoding in vertex shader | KData.oct_encode() -\> NORMAL = oct_decode(INSTANCE_CUSTOM.xy)     |
| alphaTest              | Alpha Scissor                        | render_mode depth_draw_opaque; + ALPHA_SCISSOR_THRESHOLD = 0.5;    |
| wave field             | atmos_waves.gdshaderinc              | Translated directly from ATMOS.waveGLSL() via tools/atmos_waves.js |

**7. Actionable Implementation Roadmap**

**Phase 1: Immediate Hygiene & Bug Fixes (Low Risk, High Value)**

1.  **Fix UTF-8 encodings in build scripts:** Add encoding='utf-8', newline='\n' to open() calls in girder, mavs-refuge, voth, and yuni build scripts.

2.  **Resolve BIO.iridBarkMat residency bug:** Consolidate duplicate bark shader hooks from eastabyss, nhighlands, rift, and xanadu into core/biome/30-core-foliage.js.

3.  **Fix LOD glTF duplication:** Exclude LOD.root and re-enable layer 30 objects prior to running GLTFExporter.

4.  **Fix Normal Map WebP compression:** Switch tools/textures/pack.py normal map compression from lossy 4:2:0 WebP to lossless WebP / PNG.

**Phase 2: Geometry & Draw-Call Optimization (High Performance Impact)**

1.  **Implement Adaptive Furniture Segments:** Update mkCyl, mkDome, mkCone in krator-furniture-core.js to scale radial segments based on radius ($5\text{~segments~for~}r < 0.05\text{~m}$).

2.  **Re-index Furniture Batches:** Modify Batch.prototype.absorb in krator-furniture-runtime.js to preserve index arrays, slashing vertex count by over 60%.

3.  **Transition Biome Trees to K-Variants:** Convert unique procedural tree generation in biomes/rift and biomes/hyperjungle into 12-16 baked MultiMesh variants, saving over 20 million triangles.

**Phase 3: Host Shell & Material Consolidation**

1.  **Deploy core/host/:** Unify camera, probe, sky, and loop across the 10 biome kits and pilot settlements (iziz, voth).

2.  **Consolidate Material Records:** Standardize all material definitions on the KMAT record schema with tex/pack.json detail maps.

**Phase 4: Godot Importer & Life Layer Migration**

1.  **Unified krator-world Exporter:** Unify BIO.export, ATMOS.export, and KRATOR_EXPORT into a single headless exporter.

2.  **Godot MultiMesh & Tile Streaming:** Build a Godot world streamer that pages 300m tiles based on camera position using MultiMeshInstance3D and Godot NavigationRegion3D.

3.  **Life Layer Crossover:** Port Voth's citizen schedules and navigation data (krator-sim) into Godot AStar3D / navigation agents, deprecating Three.js raycasting.
