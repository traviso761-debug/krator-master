# Scene ideas

Ideas for new scenes in the World Menagerie, collected on 2026-10-06. None of these is built unless the
**Built** column says so. Each line says what the scene is and why it suits this project: what it can reuse, or
what single idea carries it.

Kinds of build:
- **OSM city:** a real place on the shared engine (`src/engine/`), like Venice or Portland; the work is in a few
  landmark models.
- **Interior:** its own renderer on the Oldest House's kit (baked light, the cutaway, events).
- **World:** a whole generated place, like Mordor, Hyrule or Kyrene.
