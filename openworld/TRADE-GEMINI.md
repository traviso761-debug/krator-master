### Key Structural Rules Active in This Model
- **Three Distance-Decay Cargo Tiers**: `bulk` (`alpha = 2.15`, `sea = 0.16x`, `river/canal = 0.34x`) forces heavy coal, timber, iron, scrap, salt, oil, marble, and food onto ships, barges, locked Eternian canals, and the Tungang train; `medium` (`alpha = 1.48`) covers industrial metals, cordage, pitch, sugar, firearms, and mounts; `compact` (`alpha = 1.08`) lets gems, spices, silk, qiviut, alabaster, fine ceramics, books, almanacs, cybernetics, lung-salt, and oversea luxuries cross `1,500–2,800 km` inter-regional trunks.
- **Maritime Poleis & Break-of-Bulk Ports**: `League of Amphitriton` competes as five distinct polis accounts (`Ys`, `Tethys` [with built Ring Isles colony `Nephtos`], `Pontus`, `Trigon`, `Kierson`); `Port Goto` is nudged onto the Ring Sea shoreline (`920,703`) to link `Shell` by sea; cargo can only board or leave ships at actual ports (`+22..+26 eq km` handling/customs transfer).
- **Living Ruin Communities & Unpinned Peoples Restored**: `Span` & `Blade` (`Realm of Dalab`, trading with `SW Highland Frogmen`), `Carcosa` & `Henge` (`Yusar` cyberneticists), `Qoold Lheek` & `Leng` (`Yeti Kingdom` winter pycrete road), `Stair` (`Catch` tunnel-divers), `Western Spice Isles`, `Shumari` (`Shumar` plateau), `Spider Lake Guilds`, `Outlands Coyote` villages (`The Gate` Seed-Law wall + `The Sill` serai), `Scyvoi` (`B1–B7`), and `Korona Tribes`.
- **Ring Sea Piracy (`Noa's Regret`)**: Bloody Ruephus's catamaran stronghold (`896,866`) intercepts `2.5%–7.5%` of Ring Sea ship-borne cargoes depending on lane proximity and **fences those captured prizes onward by ship**.
- **Books & Kiln-Country Reconciliations**: Literate states (`Eternal State`, `Iziz`, `Voth`, `Dalab`, `Yuni`, `Jimjam`, `League` poleis, `Iron Republic`, `Xanadu`, `Dilihan`) satisfy `80%–106%` of internal book demand via domestic presses/scriptoria, while `Longsong` (`Order of Historians`) exports curated rare histories and copied codices (`0` to `Voth`, where the Order is banned). `Jimjam` (*Kiln Country*) imports carved `marble` up The Cut from NW/SW Bay contact-marble belts and exports `alabaster` (for Eternian bronze-tracery palace windows), `fine-ceramics`, `almanacs`, and regional `brick`.
