## 2. Planetary-Physics Synthesis: Pillars 1–4 + Tharn & Capsica Models

### 2.1 Pillar 1 — Multi-Ring Impact Basin Geometry (`√2` Spacing Law around `(865, 935)`)
Measuring radial distances on the `1821 × 1723` (`2 km/px`) 5.1 canvas from the Inner-Wall centroid **`px = (865, 935)`** reveals that Krator's major concentric rings follow the classic **Spudis / Hartmann–Kuiper $\sqrt{2}$ multi-ring basin spacing law** ($R_{n+1} \approx \sqrt{2}\,R_n$, analogous to Orientale, Valhalla, and Chicxulub):

1. **Central Heat-Pipe Plume Focus (`R = 0–220 km`)**: `Throne` (`847, 834`), centered over the impact-thinned lithosphere and deep mantle-uplift plug, surrounded by the flooded inner melt-sheet depression (`Ring Sea`, `-1,500 m`).
2. **Ring 1 — Inner Wall / Peak Ring (`R ≈ 490–660 km`, $D \approx 1,200\text{ km}$)**: `n highlands`, `e inner wall`, `s highlands`, `sw highlands`, and `the cut` — collapsed central uplift / mid-crustal megabreccia peak ring with a differentiated Sudbury-style `norite–granophyre` impact melt sheet along its inner footwall (`Ni–Cu–PGE`).
3. **Annular Inter-Ring Megatrough (`R ≈ 660–1,080 km`)**: `n semiarid`, `nw lowlands`, `ancient's valley`, `sw lowlands`, `sedesert`, `the bowl`, `e abysss`, and `Korona / NE` — rotational megaslump graben floored by megabreccia and filled with mare flood basalts, red beds, coal mires, and evaporites.
4. **Ring 2 — Outer Wall / Main Crater Excavation Rim (`R ≈ 1,120–1,460 km`, $D \approx 2,500\text{ km} \approx 2\,R_1$)**: `nw outer wall`, `w outer wall` + unmarked W crest (`Comp #26`), `ne outer wall`, `n/s outlands` + `e badlands` unmarked E crest (`Comp #12`), `se outer wall`, and `stalker range` — listric-slumped megabreccia, pseudotachylite fault mirrors, and overturned ejecta rampart rising into Class `O` (`8–15.5 km`).
5. **Exterior Circum-Crater Elastic-Flexural Moat (`R ≈ 1,480–1,710 km`)**: Both **`Ria Valley`** (`R = 1,484–1,708 km` across the north) and **`The Crook`** (`R = 1,502–1,591 km` down the west) sit concentrically in the **lithospheric flexural foredeep** depressed by the load of the Outer Wall + proximal ejecta blanket against high-standing map-edge Tharn continents.
6. **Ring 3 — Exterior Valhalla-Type Concentric Lithospheric Ring-Graben (`R ≈ 1,790–1,880 km` $\approx \sqrt{2}\,R_2$)**: Every point along **`The Arch`** (`(75,510)` `1,794 km`; `(140,380)` `1,826 km`; `(220,260)` `1,867 km`; `(330,165)` `1,875 km`; `(470,125)` `1,802 km`) — **plus its SW landfall into `Crookshore` (`1,665–1,890 km`) and NE landfall across `Double Delta` / northern `Ria` (`1,710–2,020 km`)** — traces a concentric exterior ring-fault opened during inward asthenospheric flow toward the collapsing transient cavity and kept active today by diurnal Laplace tidal flexing!

### 2.2 Pillar 2 — Stagnant-Lid Crustal Dichotomy: Tharn Continents + Capsica Basins Without Plate Tectonics
Combining Chris Wayan's **Tharn** (`worlddreambank.org/T/THARN.HTM`) and **Capsica** (`worlddreambank.org/C/CAP.HTM`) planetographic models on a **colder, non-plate-tectonic stagnant-lid moon** resolves every tectonic puzzle on the map:
- **Map-Edge Buoyant Felsic "Tharn" Continents (`ρ ≈ 2,650–2,750 kg/m³`)**: Primordial magma-ocean / early heat-pipe tonalite–trondhjemite–granite (`TTG`), gneiss, granulite, and anorthosite cratonic blocks (`tharn uplands`, `Far-North Massif`, `Western Boundary Cordillera` south of `y=712` and above `4.4 km`, `Far-SW Rim`, `Far-Eastern Margin`, and the **Unmarked SE Airless/Ice Continental Massif** southeast of `SE outer wall`). Because their crust is `45–65 km` thick and `~300–550 kg/m³` lighter than the `3,000–3,300 kg/m³` basaltic/mafic stagnant lid, Pratt–Airy isostasy floats them `5–13 km` above sea level into cold, thin Class `O` air — **no collisional mountain-building required**.
- **Capsica-Style Dense-Air Basins & Leaky Concentric Arches**: Like Capsica, Krator pairs deep `1.8–2.2 atm` warm troughs (`Ring Sea`, `The Rift`, `East Abyss`, `Korona` trenches) and shallow steaming island chains (`The Arch` = *Capsica's Arch*) with high cold continental borders — **but** instead of seafloor spreading or subduction arcs, all extension/compression is driven by **impact ring inheritance, volcanic-load flexure, heat-pipe burial shortening (`Slabfields`), and Laplace-resonance tidal strain**.
- **Anorogenic Silicic Rocks Without Subduction**: Evolved rhyolites, ignimbrites, and obsidian (`Korona` & `Firebliss` pancake domes, `Opal Valley` tuffs, `Neo-Ozarks` red porphyries, `Twins` obsidian) arise via **bimodal plume-rift crustal anatexis** (hot Ti-basalt/picrite sills partially melting pre-impact Tharn felsic crust or differentiated impact-melt granophyre — exact Earth/Moon analogues: Yellowstone, Afar, St. Francois Mtns, Gruithuisen Domes).

### 2.3 Pillar 3 — Laplace-Resonance Tidal Heating (`53–133 TW`) & Hydrogeochemistry
- **Heat Budget Hygiene**: Krator's `2–5×` Earth surface heat flux (`0.18–0.46 W/m²`, `53–133 TW` total) focuses into sub-jovian/anti-jovian mantle plumes (`Throne`, `Korona`, `Godthrone`, `Firebliss`) and tidally pumped ring/rift fractures (`The Rift`, `High Rift`, `Outland Gate`, `The Arch`, `White/Red Gills`). `Firebliss Patera` (`48 × 32 km` = `3.4 TW`, `2.5–6.4%` of planetary flux) fits comfortably inside this budget.
- **Ring Sea Carbonate Factory vs. `NW Bay` Dramatic Karst**: Warm `+28–34 °C`, `1.93 atm` Ring Sea water fed by intense `Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺` weathering of mafic/impact rocks and `Steampits` `Ca–HCO₃` springs must precipitate carbonate wherever clastic river mud and `Throne`'s SE ash plume are absent (`NW bay`, `SW Bay` ramp, `Port Goto`–`Crescent` northern shelf, `Talon` dry eastern shelf, `Ring Isles` shoals). **`NW bay` + `k-guilin` uniquely develops dramatic `135 m` `fenglin` towers, `tsingy` pinnacles, and `84 m` `tiankeng` shafts** because it combines (1) the widest upwind ash-free platform (`300–600 m` pure massively bedded limestone), (2) `700–1,060 m` of block-tilted hinterland hydraulic head, (3) orthogonal jointing + aggressive allogenic acid runoff off `Northern Highlands`, and (4) `~30 m` of local flexural-moat subsidence around `Throne`'s growing shield load (which drowns `NW bay`'s vadose speleothems *without* changing global Ring Sea sea level!).

### 2.4 Pillar 4 — Low Gravity (`7.4 m/s² = 0.75 g`) & Extreme Pressure Variation
- **Mechanical & Geomorphic Scaling at `0.75 g`**:
  - Brittle cliff heights, lava-tube roof spans (`48 m` in `Godstep`), cave chamber widths (`60–100 m` in `Hearths`), Bingham pancake-dome thickness (`520–650 m`), and selenite crystal beams (`14–16 m`) all scale up by `$g^{-1/2}$` to `$g^{-1}$` (`+15%` to `+33%`).
  - Tsunami run-up (`Farside` `50–100 m` from `Steaming Seamount` phreatomagmatic blasts) and megaflood boulder plucking (`Scablands`, `Outland Gate`) scale as `~1.33×` Earth equivalents, while catenary tension in `Spider Lake` silk curtains and `Loom Chasms` living cables drops `25%`.
- **Scale Height & Class `O` ("Airless") Physics**:
  - At `g = 7.4 m/s²` and `T = 250–288 K`, true hydrostatic scale height is $H = RT/(Mg) \approx 9.8\text{–}11.3\text{ km}$ (`smodel/climate.py` uses a steeper `H = 8.0 km` approximation: $P = 1.6\,e^{-h/8\text{ km}}$).
  - Even on the steeper `H = 8.0 km` curve, `8,000 m` has **`0.59 atm`** (`9,000 m` = `0.52 atm`, `14,000 m` = `0.28 atm`, `25,000 m` = `0.07 atm`). Therefore, Class `O` (`>8–9 km`) is a **stratospheric above-tropopause freeze desert (`0 mm` rain) + lethal hypobaric hypoxia zone for dense-air-adapted Kratorians**, *not* `0 Pa` lunar vacuum — which is why `wouterwall` katabatic winds can still transport loess dust off the `>8 km` rim and `Godthrone`'s `25 km` (`70 mbar`) fire fountains still experience aerodynamic drag beyond `2–6 km`.
