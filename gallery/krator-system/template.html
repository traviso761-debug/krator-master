The Krator System

Krator is a tidally locked moon of an ice giant, one of seven bodies in a tight family. The giant hangs fixed in the north-east of every sky; six other moons ride the line of its rings. The figures follow the shared sky model in core/atmos/sky.

The star's planets

The giant, with Krator and its moons, goes round a Sun-like star once a year at 1 AU. Three planets circle inside its orbit and three outside. Names are working names; none is drawn in Krator's sky yet.
